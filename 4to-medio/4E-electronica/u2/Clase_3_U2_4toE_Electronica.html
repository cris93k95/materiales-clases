📖 Reading: Three families of actuators

Actuators turn the signal from the PLC into real movement. There are three big families: electric, pneumatic and hydraulic.

Electric actuators use motors and solenoids. They are clean and easy to control. Pneumatic actuators use compressed air, often with cylinders and valves. They are fast and simple. Hydraulic actuators use oil pressure and can apply very high forces, but they need more maintenance.

In a typical assembly line, we mix the three families: an electric motor moves the conveyor, a pneumatic cylinder pushes the part, and a hydraulic press joins two pieces.
