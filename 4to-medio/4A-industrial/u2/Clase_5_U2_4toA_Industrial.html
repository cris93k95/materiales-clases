📖 Actividad 3 — Reading a CNC program

Este programa hace un agujero en el centro de una pieza. Léelo línea por línea.

N10 M03 S1200 ; start spindle at 1200 rpm
N20 G00 X0 Y0 ; rapid move to center
N30 G01 Z-5 F100 ; cut 5 mm down at 100 mm/min
N40 G00 Z10 ; lift the tool
N50 M05 ; stop spindle

📝 Actividad 4 — Read the code and answer

1. Which line starts the spindle, and at what speed?

Line N10, at 1200 rpm.

2. How deep does the tool cut, and at what feed rate?

5 mm down, at 100 mm per minute.

3. Which two lines move the tool without cutting?

Lines N20 and N40 (both G00).
