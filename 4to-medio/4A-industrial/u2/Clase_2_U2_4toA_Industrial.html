📖 Actividad 3 — Reading: The three axes

Every CNC machine moves the cutting tool in three directions. We call these directions axes. The X-axis is the horizontal movement, from left to right. The Y-axis is the movement from front to back. The Z-axis is the vertical movement, up and down.

When the operator writes a program, every line tells the machine how much to move on each axis. For example, the line X50 Y20 Z-5 moves the tool 50 mm to the right, 20 mm to the back, and 5 mm down into the metal.

The depth on the Z-axis is very important. If the tool goes too deep, it can break. If it does not go deep enough, the cut is incomplete.
