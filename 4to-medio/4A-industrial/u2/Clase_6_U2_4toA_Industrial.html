📖 Actividad 3 — Integrated reading

At the workshop, every part follows the same workflow. The engineer designs the piece in a CAD program. Then MasterCAM, the CAM software, prepares the toolpath and the operator simulates the cut on the screen.

When everything is ready, the program is sent to the CNC machine. The spindle starts (M03) and the tool moves along the three axes following lines like G01 X20 Y10 Z-3 F100. Each operation has its own job: turning shapes shafts, milling cuts flat surfaces, drilling makes holes and threading prepares the part for screws.

At the end of the program, command M05 stops the spindle and the operator checks the final piece with a caliper.

📝 Actividad 4 — Read and answer

1. What does the operator do before sending the program to the machine?

Simulates the cut on the screen.

2. What does the line G01 X20 Y10 Z-3 F100 represent?

A linear cutting move to X=20, Y=10, 3 mm down, at 100 mm/min.

3. Which command stops the spindle at the end?

M05.
