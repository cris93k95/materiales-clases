📖 Actividad 4 — Reading: Four operations, one machine

A modern CNC machine can do more than one operation. The four most common ones are turning, milling, drilling and threading.

In turning, the piece of metal rotates and a fixed tool removes material from the outside. We use this when we need a round shaft.

In milling, the piece is fixed on the worktable and the tool rotates and moves. With milling we can cut flat surfaces, slots or curved shapes.

Drilling makes round holes, and threading cuts the spiral inside a hole or outside a shaft, so we can use screws and nuts.

📝 Actividad 5 — Read and answer

1. Which operation rotates the piece instead of the tool?

Turning.

2. What can we cut with milling?

Flat surfaces, slots or curved shapes.

3. Why do we need threading?

So we can use screws and nuts.
