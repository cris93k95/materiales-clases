📖 Actividad 4 — Reading: What is a CNC machine?

A CNC machine is a tool that follows a computer program to cut, drill or shape metal. The letters CNC mean Computer Numerical Control.

The operator does not move the cutter by hand. Instead, the computer reads the program and moves the tool along three axes: X, Y and Z.

In a workshop you can find two common CNC machines: the lathe, which turns the piece, and the milling machine, which moves the tool around the piece. Both can repeat the same job hundreds of times with the same accuracy.
