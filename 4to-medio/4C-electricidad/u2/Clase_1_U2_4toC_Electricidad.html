📖 Actividad 4 — Reading (25 min): Chile's Renewable Energy Revolution

Texto adaptado nivel B1. Primera lectura: scanning de ideas principales (5 min). Segunda lectura: con la tabla de análisis al lado.

1. Chile is going through one of the fastest energy transitions in Latin America. In 2014, less than 6% of the country's electricity came from solar and wind. By 2024, that number reached around 35%, according to the Coordinador Eléctrico Nacional. The Atacama Desert, with the highest solar radiation on the planet, has become a global reference for photovoltaic plants such as Cerro Dominador (110 MW) and Quilapilún (110 MW).

2. The impact on the electrical sector is enormous. New jobs are being created across the country: solar panel installers, wind turbine technicians, inverter specialists, energy storage operators and SCADA supervisors. Companies like Enel Green Power, AES Andes and Colbún are actively hiring electrical technicians, but they all require the same combination: SEC certification (Class A or B), basic computing skills and the ability to read technical documents in English.

3. For technicians, the opportunity is clear. A certified solar installer in northern Chile can earn 30-50% more than a general electrician. However, the work also brings real challenges: long shifts in remote areas, high temperatures during the day and very cold nights in the Atacama, and the constant risk of working with high-voltage DC systems (600-1500 V) on rooftops or on tall structures. Safety protocols are strict, and any mistake can be fatal.

4. The workforce is also changing in domestic installations. The new "Ley de Generación Distribuida" allows houses and small businesses to install their own solar panels and sell extra energy to the grid through net billing. This has created a strong demand for residential electricians who can size a system, install an inverter, connect it to the grid and explain the technical details to the client. Although the technology is increasingly accessible, many installations still fail because of poor wiring or wrong sizing.

5. The sustainability dimension is one of the strongest arguments. Chile aims to be carbon-neutral by 2050, and the renewable transition is the main path to reach that goal. Solar and wind reduce dependence on imported gas and coal and improve air quality in cities. On the other hand, renewable energy is intermittent: the sun does not shine at night and the wind does not always blow. This creates a real challenge for the infrastructure — the grid must be modernized and battery storage must grow fast to keep the system stable.

6. The renewable revolution is not a future event for Chile: it is happening now. The question for every electrical technician is no longer "Will I work with renewables?", but "How fast can I get the certification and the English skills to be part of this growing sector?"

📝 Actividad 6 — Comprehension & analysis (6 preguntas)

1. (Explícita) ¿Qué porcentaje de la matriz eléctrica chilena era renovable en 2014 y en 2024?

2014: menos del 6%. 2024: alrededor del 35%.

2. (Explícita) Menciona 3 cargos nuevos que se están creando en el sector renovable.

Instalador de paneles solares, técnico de turbinas eólicas, especialista en inversores, operador de SCADA, técnico de almacenamiento energético.

3. (Explícita) ¿Qué tres requisitos comunes piden las empresas renovables (Enel, AES, Colbún)?

Certificación SEC (Clase A o B), habilidades básicas de computación e inglés técnico para leer manuales.

4. (Inferencia) ¿Por qué el texto dice que el grid necesita modernizarse?

Porque la energía solar y eólica es intermitente (no hay sol de noche, no siempre hay viento). El grid actual debe poder integrar baterías y manejar variaciones rápidas de generación para mantener estable el sistema.

5. (Análisis) ¿What does this mean for YOUR career? Escribe 2 acciones concretas que tú podrías tomar este año.

Respuesta personal. Ejemplos válidos: tramitar SEC Clase A, hacer práctica en empresa fotovoltaica (norte o local), tomar curso de instalador fotovoltaico, practicar lectura de fichas de inversores en inglés.

6. (Crítica) El texto cierra: "How fast can I get the certification and the English skills to be part of this growing sector?" ¿Cómo responderías HOY?

Respuesta personal y crítica. Debe incluir autoevaluación (sí, ya estoy en camino / no, aún no / parcialmente) + al menos un argumento basado en el texto o en tu experiencia.

🗣 Actividad 7 — Critical discussion en grupos (20 min)

En grupos de 3-4 estudiantes, debatan:

"How will the renewable energy revolution affect YOUR future job? Is this a threat or an opportunity?"

  1. Cada miembro comparte su postura inicial (1 min).
  2. El grupo construye una postura común con 2-3 argumentos apoyados en el texto.
  3. Anotan: "Our group believes the renewable transition is mainly a __________ because __________, __________ and __________."
  4. Un representante por grupo comparte la postura (1 min) en el cierre.

Lenguaje útil: "We believe…", "Our main argument is…", "According to the text…", "On the other hand…", "In conclusion…".

📋 Seguimiento de la clase

Formativa: el docente recoge la tabla de análisis y observa el debate. Criterios: (1) tabla completa con información del texto, (2) postura grupal con al menos 2 argumentos basados en el texto, (3) uso de al menos 4 palabras del vocabulario, (4) participación activa.

🟣 Cierre (15 min)

🗣 Group sharing + ticket de salida

Un representante de cada grupo comparte la postura común. El docente anota cuántos grupos vieron la transición renovable como threat, opportunity o both.

Preview: "Next class, we will analyze a real workplace problem — what happens when a city suffers a major blackout because of poor maintenance?"

🎫 Exit ticket (3 líneas)

Completa en inglés y entrega al salir:

"In my opinion, Chile's renewable energy revolution is __________ (positive / negative / both) because __________ and __________."
