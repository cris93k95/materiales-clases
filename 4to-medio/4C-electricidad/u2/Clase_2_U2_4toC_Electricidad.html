📖 Reading: Inside the motor

When we open an electric motor, we find a clear structure. The outer case is the housing. Inside, the stator surrounds the rotor.

The rotor has an iron core wrapped with copper wire (the winding). Two bearings let the rotor turn with low friction.

In a DC motor, the brushes touch a small ring called the commutator. The commutator changes the direction of the current and keeps the rotor turning in the same way.

📝 Read and answer

Responde con información del texto.

1. What part keeps the rotor turning with low friction?

The bearings.

2. What is inside the rotor iron core?

Copper wire (the winding).

3. What is the job of the commutator in a DC motor?

To change the direction of the current and keep the rotor turning.
