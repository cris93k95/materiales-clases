switchgear
equipment that controls, protects, and isolates power systems
Pron.: SWICH-guir
load
the amount of power demanded by a system
Pron.: LOUD
transformer
equipment that changes electrical voltage levels
Pron.: trans-FOR-mer
grounding
a safety connection that sends electricity safely to the earth
Pron.: GRAUN-ding
regulator
a device that keeps a value, such as voltage, stable
Pron.: RE-gu-lei-ter
phase
one part of a multi-part electrical power cycle
Pron.: FEIS
