📖 Reading: How brakes work

When the driver presses the brake pedal, the brake fluid travels through a thin tube to the caliper at each wheel.

The caliper closes and pushes two pads against the brake disc. The friction between the pads and the disc slows the wheel.

If the pads are worn, the braking distance becomes longer and the car is less safe. Mechanics check the pad thickness every service.
