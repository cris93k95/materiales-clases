📖 Reading: Comfort and control

The suspension keeps the car stable when the road is rough. Each wheel has a spring and a shock absorber. The spring supports the weight, and the shock absorber softens the vibration.

The steering system lets the driver turn the car. When the driver turns the steering wheel, a tie rod moves the front wheels to the left or the right.

A good wheel alignment is important: if the alignment is wrong, the tires wear out faster and the car pulls to one side.

📝 Read and answer

Responde con información del texto.

1. Which two parts work together at each wheel of the suspension?

A spring and a shock absorber.

2. What does the tie rod do?

It moves the front wheels left or right.

3. What happens if the alignment is wrong?

The tires wear out faster and the car pulls to one side.
