📖 Reading: First contact with the customer

When a customer arrives at the workshop, the service advisor welcomes the person and asks: "Good morning, how can I help you?"

The customer explains the issue: a strange noise, a brake problem, or a service due. The advisor listens carefully and writes the information on a work order.

A clear conversation in English is important when the customer or the car brand is international.
