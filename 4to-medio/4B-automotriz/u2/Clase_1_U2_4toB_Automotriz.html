📖 Actividad 4 — Reading (25 min): The Rise of Electric Vehicles in Chile

Texto adaptado nivel B1. Primera lectura: scanning de ideas principales (5 min). Segunda lectura: con la tabla de análisis al lado.

1. The Chilean automotive market is in the middle of a clear transition. According to the National Automotive Association (ANAC), in 2024 around 10% of all new cars sold in the country were hybrids or fully electric vehicles (EVs). Just five years ago, that number was less than 1%. The Santiago Metro now operates fully with electric buses, and the public network of charging stations has grown from less than 100 in 2020 to more than 500 in 2024.

2. This trend has a direct impact on automotive workshops. A traditional combustion engine has more than 2,000 mechanical parts; a modern EV has fewer than 200. However, EVs add new systems that did not exist before: high-voltage battery packs (400 V or more), electric motors with permanent magnets, regenerative braking, on-board diagnostic software and connectivity modules.

3. For automotive technicians, this is both an opportunity and a challenge. The opportunity is clear: dealerships like Kaufmann, Tesla and Indumotora are actively hiring technicians who can read service manuals in English and use scanner software (OBD-II, manufacturer diagnostic tools). Salaries for certified EV technicians in Santiago can be 20-30% higher than for general mechanics. The challenge is that working with high-voltage batteries requires training and certification: a wrong connection can cause electric shock or fire.

4. The workforce in Chile is adapting slowly. INACAP and Duoc UC now offer specialization modules in "Mecánica de Vehículos Eléctricos", but most older mechanics did not study these systems during their original training. Many feel uncertain: "Will my workshop survive if all cars become electric?" The truth, according to ANAC, is that combustion cars will still be repaired in Chile for at least 15 more years, but the workshops that survive will be those that can also service hybrids and EVs.

5. The transition also has a sustainability dimension. EVs produce zero local emissions, which is important in cities like Santiago and Rancagua with serious air pollution problems. On the other hand, the production of batteries requires lithium — mostly mined in the Atacama region — and the recycling of used batteries is still a global challenge. Chile is a major lithium exporter, although very little of that lithium becomes batteries inside the country.

6. The infrastructure is also evolving. Big cities have growing networks of charging stations, but in the south and the regions the coverage is still uneven. For an automotive technician today, the question is not "Will EVs replace combustion cars?", but "Am I learning the new skills fast enough to be part of the new automotive industry?"

📝 Actividad 6 — Comprehension & analysis (6 preguntas)

1. (Explícita) ¿Qué porcentaje del mercado chileno de autos nuevos fueron híbridos o eléctricos en 2024?

Alrededor del 10% (vs. menos del 1% cinco años antes).

2. (Explícita) Compara el número de partes mecánicas de un motor a combustión y de un EV.

Un motor a combustión tiene más de 2,000 partes; un EV tiene menos de 200.

3. (Explícita) Menciona 2 sistemas nuevos que los EV agregan al taller.

Batería de alto voltaje (400 V+), motor eléctrico con imanes permanentes, frenado regenerativo, software de diagnóstico a bordo, módulos de conectividad.

4. (Inferencia) Según el texto, ¿qué talleres "van a sobrevivir" a la transición?

Los talleres que mantengan la capacidad de reparar autos a combustión y además aprendan a dar servicio a híbridos y eléctricos. No es necesario abandonar la combustión, pero sí sumar las nuevas habilidades.

5. (Análisis) ¿What does this mean for YOUR career? Escribe 2 acciones concretas que tú podrías tomar este año.

Respuesta personal. Ejemplos válidos: tomar curso de EV en INACAP/Duoc UC, practicar lectura de manuales OBD-II en inglés, hacer práctica en taller que ya repare híbridos, certificarse en seguridad de alto voltaje.

6. (Crítica) El texto plantea: "Am I learning the new skills fast enough to be part of the new automotive industry?" ¿Cómo responderías HOY?

Respuesta personal y crítica. Debe incluir autoevaluación (sí / no / parcial) + al menos un argumento basado en el texto o en tu experiencia.

🗣 Actividad 7 — Critical discussion en grupos (20 min)

En grupos de 3-4 estudiantes, debatan:

"How will the EV transition affect YOUR future job? Is this a threat or an opportunity?"

  1. Cada miembro comparte su postura inicial (1 min).
  2. El grupo construye una postura común con 2-3 argumentos apoyados en el texto.
  3. Anotan: "Our group believes the EV transition is mainly a __________ because __________, __________ and __________."
  4. Un representante por grupo comparte la postura (1 min) en el cierre.

Lenguaje útil: "We believe…", "Our main argument is…", "According to the text…", "On the other hand…", "In conclusion…".

📋 Seguimiento de la clase

Formativa: el docente recoge la tabla de análisis y observa el debate. Criterios: (1) tabla completa con información del texto, (2) postura grupal con al menos 2 argumentos basados en el texto, (3) uso de al menos 4 palabras del vocabulario, (4) participación activa.

🟣 Cierre (15 min)

🗣 Group sharing + ticket de salida

Un representante de cada grupo comparte la postura común. El docente anota cuántos grupos vieron la transición a EVs como threat, opportunity o both.

Preview: "Next class, we will analyze a real workplace problem — what happens when technicians depend too much on computer diagnostics and forget manual inspection?"

🎫 Exit ticket (3 líneas)

Completa en inglés y entrega al salir:

"In my opinion, the rise of EVs in Chile is __________ (positive / negative / both) because __________ and __________."
