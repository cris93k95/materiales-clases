🔵 Desarrollo

🧠 Actividad 2 — Language Focus

Since apunta a un punto del tiempo. For apunta a una duracion.

Since vs For with Present Perfect
Examples
  • SINCE + point in time: since 2015, since Monday, since I was 10.
  • FOR + duration: for 10 years, for 2 months, for a long time.
  • He has worked at the factory since 2015.
  • He has worked at the factory for 10 years.
Sentence frames & tips
  • I have + V3 + since + point in time.
  • I have + V3 + for + duration.
  • Both connect a past action with the present.

📖 Actividad 4 — Reading Text — Five Professionals, Five Stories

1. Maria — Electrician: Maria has worked as an electrician since 2010. She has installed home wiring systems for fifteen years. She has been a foreman since 2018.

2. Roberto — Auto mechanic: Roberto has been a mechanic for twenty-two years. He has worked at the same workshop since 2003. He has trained eight apprentices during his career.

3. Daniela — Graphic designer: Daniela has worked as a graphic designer since 2016. She has designed posters for over fifty clients. She has not stopped learning new software since she started.

4. Felipe — Electronics tech: Felipe has been an electronics technician for eight years. He has assembled circuits since he was 17 years old. He has earned several certifications since 2020.

5. Carmen — Industrial mechanic: Carmen has worked at the same steel factory for twelve years. She has welded structures since 2014. She has never had an accident.

📝 Actividad 5 — Reading comprehension

Responde regresando al texto. Busca evidencia concreta antes de revisar la respuesta sugerida.

1. Since when has Maria worked as an electrician?

Since 2010.

2. For how long has Roberto been a mechanic?

For twenty-two years.

3. Which professional has trained eight apprentices?

Roberto.

4. What has Daniela not stopped doing since she started?

She has not stopped learning new software.

5. Who has never had an accident?

Carmen.

6. Infer: in Felipe's case, why does the text say since he was 17 years old and not for many years?

Because since shows the starting point of his early interest, which is more specific and personal than a duration.

🚀 Actividad 6 — Apply & Extend: tu trayectoria personal en una infografia

Construye tu propia infografia de trayectoria con oraciones SINCE/FOR. No es relleno: es practica para usarlas correctamente.
  1. Crea una linea de tiempo de tu vida con 5 hitos: estudios, hobby, deporte, idioma, especialidad tecnica.
  2. Escribe 5 oraciones, alternando SINCE y FOR (al menos 2 de cada uno).
  3. Anade UNA pregunta con How long...? para hacer a tu companero.
  4. Disena la infografia con colores: azul para SINCE, verde para FOR.
✅ Auto-chequeo antes de entregar
  • Tengo al menos 2 oraciones con SINCE y 2 con FOR.
  • SINCE va con punto del tiempo; FOR va con duracion. Sin confusiones.
  • Mi pregunta usa How long have you...?
  • Mi infografia se lee facil y los colores ayudan a distinguir.
