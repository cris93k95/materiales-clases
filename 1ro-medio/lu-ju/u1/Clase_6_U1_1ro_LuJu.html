🧠 Actividad 2 — Language Focus

Un texto instructivo necesita orden. Los conectores de secuencia son la columna vertebral de cualquier manual tecnico.

Sequence connectors in instructions
Examples
  • First, connect the negative wire to the battery.
  • Then, place the LED on the breadboard.
  • After that, add the resistor to limit the current.
  • Finally, close the circuit and check if the LED lights up.
Sentence frames & tips
  • Order: first, second, then, next, after that, finally.
  • Always end with finally or last for the final step.
  • Use a comma after the connector: First, ___ .

📖 Actividad 4 — Reading Text — How to Build a Simple LED Circuit

Materials needed: one LED, one 220-ohm resistor, one 9-volt battery, two wires (red and black), one breadboard.

First, place the LED on the breadboard. Identify the long leg (positive) and the short leg (negative). Insert each leg in a separate row.

Then, connect the resistor in series with the long leg of the LED. The resistor protects the LED so that too much current does not burn it.

After that, connect a red wire from the battery's positive (+) terminal to the resistor. Next, connect a black wire from the negative (-) terminal of the battery to the short leg of the LED.

Finally, place the battery and check the circuit. If you have connected everything correctly, the LED lights up. If it does not light up, check the polarity of the LED first.

📝 Actividad 5 — Reading comprehension

Responde regresando al texto. Busca evidencia concreta antes de revisar la respuesta sugerida.

1. How many materials do you need to build this circuit?

Five: one LED, one resistor, one 9-volt battery, two wires and one breadboard.

2. Why is the resistor important?

Because it limits the current so that too much current does not burn the LED.

3. Which leg of the LED is the positive one?

The long leg is the positive (+) leg.

4. What color wire goes to the negative terminal of the battery?

The black wire.

5. What should you do if the LED does not light up?

Check the polarity of the LED first.

6. Infer: why does the text emphasize the order of steps?

Because if a step is skipped or done in the wrong order, the circuit can fail or the LED can burn out, so order is essential in instructions.

🚀 Actividad 6 — Apply & Extend: tu propia hoja de instrucciones

Vas a escribir un manual real, no un resumen. Otro estudiante deberia poder seguir tus instrucciones sin verte ni leer el texto original.
  1. Elige otro mini-proyecto: connect two LEDs in parallel, add a switch to a simple circuit, connect a buzzer instead of an LED.
  2. Lista los materiales (3-5 items) en formato lista.
  3. Escribe 5 pasos usando un conector de secuencia diferente en cada uno (first, then, next, after that, finally).
  4. Cierra con una seccion Troubleshooting: 2 problemas posibles + como detectarlos.
✅ Auto-chequeo antes de entregar
  • Mi proyecto NO es el LED simple del texto.
  • Uso 5 conectores DIFERENTES, no repito then dos veces.
  • Mis pasos son ejecutables: nadie tiene que adivinar.
  • Mi troubleshooting es realista (polarity, broken wire, weak battery, etc).
