Reconocer y categorizar vocabulario técnico básico de herramientas comunes a las cinco especialidades técnico-profesionales.

Inicio (15 min)

Prepare the reading by activating prior knowledge and predicting the professional context of the text.

  1. Read the objective and highlight two words that show today's reading focus.
  2. Review key vocabulary from class 1: hammer, screwdriver, wrench, wire.
  3. Predict what kind of professional routine, problem, or career situation will appear in the text.
  4. Share one prediction with a partner before reading.

📚 Activity 3 — Reading

Texto graduado A2: cinco parrafos, secuencia clara y apoyo de vocabulario para comprender la rutina tecnica.

Sofía is a first-year technical student. Every day, she learns about tools and safety rules in the workshop. The reading introduces the workplace, the people involved, and the main task in simple language.

Before the routine continues, the student notices words such as hammer and screwdriver. These words are important because they explain what is used, what is checked, and what should happen next.

In class, she reads a short text about a mechanic who checks engines, changes oil, and uses different tools. The actions appear in a clear order so the reader can follow the procedure without losing the main idea.

While reading, the student identifies the purpose of each action, the safety detail, and the result of the task. This helps the class connect vocabulary with real workshop decisions.

After reading, she identifies key words and explains what each tool is used for.
