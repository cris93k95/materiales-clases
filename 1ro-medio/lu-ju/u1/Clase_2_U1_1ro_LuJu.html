🧠 Actividad 2 — Language Focus

Estas herramientas aparecen en automotriz, mecanica industrial, electricidad, electronica y artes graficas. Conocerlas por su nombre en ingles es la base del semestre.

Tools across the 5 specialties
Examples
  • Cutting: hacksaw, wire cutter, scissors.
  • Measuring: measuring tape, caliper, multimeter, level.
  • Joining: welding torch, soldering iron, clamp.
  • Safety: safety goggles, gloves, helmet.
Sentence frames & tips
  • A ___ is used to ___.
  • You need a ___ when you want to ___.
  • Pronunciation tip: stress falls on the first syllable in most tool names (HAM-mer, PLI-ers, WREN-ch).

📖 Actividad 4 — Reading Text — The Right Tool for the Right Job

Mr. Soto is a supervisor in a small workshop. Every day, he sees young workers make the same mistake: they use the wrong tool for the job. A wrench is not a hammer. A screwdriver is not a chisel. And pliers are not a wrench.

When you use the wrong tool, three things can happen: the work is bad, the tool breaks, or someone gets hurt. Last month, a young worker tried to remove a nut with pliers. The pliers slipped and his finger was injured.

A good technician always asks two questions before starting: what is the job? and what is the correct tool? If the right tool is not available, the work waits. It is better to wait ten minutes than to lose ten weeks of work because of an injury.

Mr. Soto says it every morning: the right tool for the right job. No shortcuts. No excuses.

📝 Actividad 5 — Reading comprehension

Responde regresando al texto. Busca evidencia concreta antes de revisar la respuesta sugerida.

1. What is the common mistake of young workers?

They use the wrong tool for the job.

2. Give two examples of wrong tool use from the text.

Using a wrench as a hammer, or using pliers as a wrench (also: using a screwdriver as a chisel).

3. What happened to the young worker last month?

He tried to remove a nut with pliers; the pliers slipped and his finger was injured.

4. What two questions should a good technician ask before starting?

What is the job, and what is the correct tool?

5. Why is it better to wait ten minutes?

Because waiting is much better than losing weeks of work because of an injury.

6. Infer: why does Mr. Soto repeat the rule every morning?

Because workers forget under pressure and only constant repetition turns the rule into a real safety habit.

🚀 Actividad 6 — Apply & Extend: tu inventario tecnico personal

No basta con saber nombres: hay que conectarlos con tareas reales. Crea tu propio inventario profesional.
  1. Elige UNA especialidad de las 5 (automotive, industrial, electricity, electronics, graphic arts).
  2. Lista 8 herramientas en ingles que un trabajador de esa especialidad usa.
  3. Para cada una escribe: name — what it is for (use is used to) — one risk if used incorrectly.
  4. Cierra con una regla de seguridad propia que aplique a tu lista, escrita en imperativo (Always wear..., Never use...).
✅ Auto-chequeo antes de entregar
  • Mi lista tiene exactamente 8 herramientas, todas relevantes a la misma especialidad.
  • Cada herramienta usa la estructura is used to + verb.
  • Identifico un riesgo concreto, no it is dangerous.
  • Mi regla de seguridad es accionable y especifica.
