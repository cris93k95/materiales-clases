🧠 Actividad 2 — Language Focus

Usamos Present Perfect para hablar de experiencias, logros y acciones inconclusas, sin importar el momento exacto.

Present Perfect Simple
Examples
  • Experience: Have you ever welded steel?
  • Achievement: She has installed 200 panels.
  • Unfinished: He has worked here for 10 years.
  • Negative / interrogative: I have not used a lathe. / Has he ever fixed a brake?
Sentence frames & tips
  • Structure: Subject + have/has + past participle.
  • Irregular participles to memorize: built, done, made, cut, put, seen, written, broken, chosen, driven, taught, taken.
  • Affirmative: have/has + V3 — Negative: have/has + not + V3.

📖 Actividad 4 — Reading Text — An Experienced Mechanic: Interview with Don Pedro

Reporter: Don Pedro, how long have you worked as a mechanic?

Don Pedro: I have worked as a mechanic for thirty years. I have seen many changes in this profession. I have repaired thousands of cars, from old trucks to new electric vehicles.

Reporter: Have you ever had a serious accident?

Don Pedro: No, I have never had a serious accident. I have always followed the safety rules. But I have seen accidents in other workshops, and they have taught me to be very careful.

Reporter: What is the most difficult thing you have done?

Don Pedro: I have rebuilt a complete engine three times. Once, a customer brought me a 1965 Chevrolet. The engine had been destroyed by years of bad maintenance. I have learned that patience is more important than fast hands.

Reporter: What is your advice to young mechanics?

Don Pedro: Listen, observe and ask. I have taught many young people, and the best ones always ask questions. I have not stopped learning, and I am 60 years old.

📝 Actividad 5 — Reading comprehension

Responde regresando al texto. Busca evidencia concreta antes de revisar la respuesta sugerida.

1. How long has Don Pedro worked as a mechanic?

For thirty years.

2. Has Don Pedro ever had a serious accident? Why?

No, he has never had a serious accident, because he has always followed the safety rules.

3. How many times has Don Pedro rebuilt a complete engine?

Three times.

4. What car case does he remember in particular?

A 1965 Chevrolet with an engine destroyed by years of bad maintenance.

5. What advice does he give to young mechanics?

Listen, observe and ask questions.

6. Infer: why does Don Pedro say I have not stopped learning at 60?

Because in a technical profession technology keeps changing (electric vehicles, new tools), so continuous learning is necessary to stay useful and safe.

🚀 Actividad 6 — Apply & Extend: tu mini-entrevista en Present Perfect

  1. Imagina que entrevistas a un profesional tecnico (NO mecanico).
  2. Escribe 5 preguntas con Present Perfect: 2 con How long...?, 2 con Have you ever...?, 1 con What is the most ___ thing you have done?.
  3. Inventa respuestas creibles para cada pregunta, todas en Present Perfect.
  4. Subraya cada forma de Present Perfect en color (sujeto = azul, have/has = verde, participio = rojo).
