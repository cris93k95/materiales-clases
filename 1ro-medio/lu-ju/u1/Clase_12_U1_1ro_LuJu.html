🧠 Actividad 2 — Language Focus

Hoy aplicamos 3 estrategias en orden: predecir antes de leer, skim para idea general, scan para detalles especificos.

3 reading strategies: predict + skim + scan
Examples
  • Predict: what will the text be about? Use the title and images.
  • Skim: read titles, first sentences, last sentence. Goal: main idea.
  • Scan: move eyes quickly to find specific names, numbers, dates.
  • Use the strategies in this order, not all at once.
Sentence frames & tips
  • Predict → Skim → Scan → Deep read.
  • Who? What? Where? When? Why? How? are scan questions.
  • What is the main idea? is a skim question.

📖 Actividad 4 — Reading Text — Case Study: The Day Everything Went Wrong at Rivera's Workshop

On Friday morning at Rivera's Workshop, three young workers were preparing to weld a metal frame. The order was urgent and they were behind schedule.

Worker A took off his welding mask to see better. Worker B forgot to clean the floor of oil stains. Worker C did not check the gas connection of the welding torch.

When the arc started, three things happened at the same time. The intense light burned Worker A's eyes. A spark fell on the oil and a small fire started. The gas connection leaked and the flame became unstable.

The supervisor heard the noise and ran. He turned off the gas, used the fire extinguisher and took Worker A out. The fire was controlled in two minutes. Worker A was sent to the doctor; he could not work for a week.

The investigation found that three small mistakes at the same time caused the accident. After this case, Rivera's Workshop changed its protocol: now no welding starts without a 3-point check (mask, floor, gas connection).

📝 Actividad 5 — Reading comprehension

Responde regresando al texto. Busca evidencia concreta antes de revisar la respuesta sugerida.

1. How many workers were involved and what were they doing?

Three young workers were preparing to weld a metal frame.

2. What mistake did Worker B make?

He forgot to clean the floor of oil stains.

3. What three things happened at the same time when the arc started?

Worker A's eyes were burned, a spark started a small fire on the oil, and the gas connection leaked, making the flame unstable.

4. How did the supervisor respond?

He turned off the gas, used the fire extinguisher and took Worker A out.

5. What was the conclusion of the investigation?

Three small mistakes happening at the same time caused the accident.

6. Infer: why is the new protocol called 3-point check?

Because the three things that failed (mask, floor, gas) are now checked before any welding starts; the rule was made directly from the lessons of the accident.

🚀 Actividad 6 — Apply & Extend: tu protocolo de 3 puntos

Vas a producir un protocolo profesional, no un resumen. Otro estudiante podra implementarlo manana.
  1. Elige una actividad tecnica de riesgo (NO welding): drilling, grinding, electrical wiring, soldering, lifting heavy parts.
  2. Disena tu 3-point check: 3 verificaciones obligatorias antes de comenzar.
  3. Para cada punto escribe: what to check + why + what happens if you skip it.
  4. Cierra con una orden visual (icono o senal) que se podria colgar en el lugar de trabajo.
✅ Auto-chequeo antes de entregar
  • Mi actividad NO es welding (esta cubierta).
  • Los 3 puntos son claros, breves y verificables en menos de 30 segundos.
  • Cada punto tiene una consecuencia real si se omite.
  • Mi senal visual transmite el mensaje sin necesidad de texto.
