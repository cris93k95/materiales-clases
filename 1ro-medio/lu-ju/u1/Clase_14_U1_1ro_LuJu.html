🔵 Desarrollo

🧠 Actividad 2 — Language Focus

En jigsaw reading cada estudiante se hace experto de un fragmento y luego ensena al resto. La comprension total depende de todos.

Jigsaw reading strategy
Examples
  • Phase 1: read your assigned text and become an expert.
  • Phase 2: meet other experts of the same text to confirm.
  • Phase 3: form mixed groups with one expert per text.
  • Phase 4: each expert teaches their text to the others.
Sentence frames & tips
  • In my text, the professional works in ___.
  • He/She uses ___ and works with ___.
  • Something unique about this profession is ___.

📖 Actividad 4 — Reading Text — Five Professionals from Five Countries

1. Hans — Automotive engineer, Germany: Hans works at a BMW plant in Munich. He has been an engineer for fifteen years. In Germany, training is very long (3-4 years apprenticeship) and certifications are strict. Hans uses computer diagnostics every day.

2. Yuki — Electronics technician, Japan: Yuki works at a small electronics company in Osaka. She assembles micro-circuits with very high precision. Japanese workshops follow the philosophy of kaizen (continuous improvement). Yuki cleans her workspace every hour.

3. Camila — Electrician, Chile: Camila works at a construction company in Santiago. She has installed wiring in over 200 apartments. In Chile, electricians need an SEC license. She uses a multimeter every day and trains junior workers on weekends.

4. Mike — Graphic designer, USA: Mike works in a small design studio in California. He has designed posters for tech startups since 2018. American studios value creativity and individual portfolios. Mike works remotely from home three days a week.

5. Jake — Industrial mechanic, Australia: Jake works at a mining company in Western Australia. He has been a mechanic for ten years. Australian mining sites require strict safety protocols and Jake completes 50 hours of safety training every year.

📝 Actividad 5 — Reading comprehension

Responde regresando al texto. Busca evidencia concreta antes de revisar la respuesta sugerida.

1. How long is the apprenticeship in Germany?

3-4 years.

2. What is the Japanese philosophy mentioned in Yuki's profile?

Kaizen (continuous improvement).

3. What license does Camila need in Chile?

An SEC license.

4. How does Mike's work schedule reflect American culture?

He works remotely from home three days a week, valuing flexibility.

5. Why does Jake complete 50 hours of safety training every year?

Because Australian mining sites require strict safety protocols.

6. Infer: what is one common feature across all 5 countries?

All professionals value training and continuous learning, but each country emphasizes a different aspect: precision, philosophy, regulation, creativity or safety.

🚀 Actividad 6 — Apply & Extend: tu carta a un profesional extranjero

Vas a redactar una carta-correo a un profesional de OTRO pais. No copies el texto; crea preguntas reales.
  1. Elige UNO de los 5 profesionales del texto.
  2. Escribe un email corto (8-10 oraciones) con: greeting + brief presentation of yourself + 4 questions about their work + closing.
  3. Las 4 preguntas deben usar al menos 2 con Present Perfect, 1 con used to, 1 abierta.
  4. Cierra con una razon por la que te interesa esa cultura tecnica.
✅ Auto-chequeo antes de entregar
  • Mi email respeta el formato (saludo, cuerpo, despedida).
  • Mis 4 preguntas son sustantivas, no How are you?
  • Uso correctamente Present Perfect y used to.
  • Mi razon final es especifica.
