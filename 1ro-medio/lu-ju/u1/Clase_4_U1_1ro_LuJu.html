🧠 Actividad 2 — Language Focus

En mecanica industrial los riesgos son mayores: maquinas pesadas, soldadura, alta temperatura. El vocabulario de EPP no es opcional.

Industrial mechanics + safety vocabulary
Examples
  • Heavy machinery: lathe, milling machine, drill press, grinding wheel.
  • Materials: steel, iron, bolt, nut, screw.
  • Joining/cutting: welding torch, hacksaw, file.
  • PPE: safety goggles, welding mask, leather gloves, helmet, steel-toe boots.
Sentence frames & tips
  • Before using a ___, you must wear ___.
  • A ___ is a heavy machine used to ___.
  • Always remember: PPE = Personal Protective Equipment.

📖 Actividad 4 — Reading Text — Welding Safety: A Case Study

Diego was a new worker at a metal structures factory. On his second day, he was asked to weld two steel plates. He was in a hurry and decided that just for one minute he didn't need the welding mask or the leather gloves.

He turned on the welding torch and the bright arc started. The intense light burned his eyes and small sparks landed on his arms. Within five seconds, he had to stop because of pain. His skin had small burns and his eyes were red and watering.

The supervisor took Diego to the first-aid kit and then to the doctor. The diagnosis: arc eye (flash burn). Diego could not work for three days and was placed on a mandatory safety training.

The factory used Diego's case as a lesson for all workers: PPE is not optional. One minute without protection can mean weeks without work, permanent eye damage, and a serious legal problem for the company.

📝 Actividad 5 — Reading comprehension

Responde regresando al texto. Busca evidencia concreta antes de revisar la respuesta sugerida.

1. Why did Diego decide not to use his PPE?

He was in a hurry and thought he only needed one minute.

2. What two pieces of PPE did he skip?

The welding mask and the leather gloves.

3. What injuries did he suffer?

Small burns on his arms and arc eye / flash burn (red, watering eyes).

4. How long was he unable to work?

Three days.

5. What did the factory do as a consequence?

It used the case as a lesson and placed Diego on mandatory safety training.

6. Infer: why is the story used to train OTHER workers, not only Diego?

Because seeing a real consequence is more powerful than reading a rule, and it prevents the same mistake from being repeated.

🚀 Actividad 6 — Apply & Extend: redacta una SAFETY CARD

Vas a producir una tarjeta de seguridad para colgar en un taller. Debe ser util en menos de 30 segundos de lectura.
  1. Elige UN equipo de riesgo: welding torch, grinding wheel, drill press, lathe, milling machine.
  2. Disena una tarjeta con 4 secciones: Equipment name — Required PPE (3+ items) — 3 DOs — 3 DON'Ts.
  3. Cierra con una oracion de consecuencia usando If you ___, you can ___.
  4. Las indicaciones deben estar en imperativo (Always wear..., Never touch...).
✅ Auto-chequeo antes de entregar
  • Mi equipo NO es el welding torch (lo cubre el texto).
  • Mis DOs y DON'Ts son ESPECIFICOS, no genericos.
  • Mi oracion de consecuencia es realista.
  • Una persona sin conocimiento previo entenderia la tarjeta.
