🧠 Actividad 2 — Language Focus

El vocabulario automotriz tiene capas: partes del auto, problemas comunes y acciones del mecanico. Hoy nos enfocamos en las partes.

Automotive vocabulary & descriptive reading
Examples
  • Engine area: engine, battery, alternator, radiator, oil filter, spark plug.
  • Outside: hood, tire, exhaust pipe.
  • Inside: dashboard, steering wheel.
  • Action verbs: check, replace, inspect, repair, change.
Sentence frames & tips
  • The mechanic checks the ___.
  • The ___ is replaced when it is worn out.
  • Many car parts have everyday English words (hood, tire) plus technical ones (alternator, suspension).

📖 Actividad 4 — Reading Text — A Day at Gonzalez Automotive Workshop

Mr. Gonzalez owns a small automotive workshop in Maipu. On Monday morning, a customer arrived with a problem: the car was making a strange noise and losing power on uphill streets.

Mr. Gonzalez did not start replacing parts. First, he opened the hood and checked the engine visually. He inspected the spark plugs and noticed that one was very dirty and another was almost burned.

Then, he tested the battery with a multimeter: the voltage was correct. He looked at the oil filter and the radiator: both were clean. The problem was probably in the ignition system.

After he replaced the spark plugs with new ones, he started the car. The strange noise disappeared and the engine recovered its normal power. The customer was happy and Mr. Gonzalez added the case to his notebook for future reference.

📝 Actividad 5 — Reading comprehension

Responde regresando al texto. Busca evidencia concreta antes de revisar la respuesta sugerida.

1. What problem did the customer report?

The car was making a strange noise and losing power on uphill streets.

2. What was the first thing Mr. Gonzalez did?

He opened the hood and checked the engine visually.

3. What did he find on the spark plugs?

One was very dirty and another was almost burned.

4. How did he test the battery?

With a multimeter; the voltage was correct.

5. What was the solution?

He replaced the spark plugs with new ones.

6. Infer: why does Mr. Gonzalez write each case in a notebook?

So he can compare future problems with previous ones and improve his diagnostic speed.

🚀 Actividad 6 — Apply & Extend: etiqueta y describe un auto

Vas a producir material profesional, no copiar el texto. Tu producto sera leido por alguien que NO ha leido el texto del taller de Gonzalez.
  1. Dibuja (o usa la silueta entregada) la vista lateral de un auto y etiqueta 8 partes en ingles.
  2. Junto al dibujo, escribe una mini-ficha de cada parte (3 lineas): Name — What it does — What problem can it have.
  3. Inventa una orden de trabajo (work order) corta: customer reports + first inspection + part to check, en 4 oraciones.
  4. Tu orden de trabajo NO puede ser sobre bujias (ya esta en el texto).
✅ Auto-chequeo antes de entregar
  • Mis 8 etiquetas estan en ingles correcto, no spanglish.
  • Cada parte tiene su What it does y What can fail.
  • Mi orden de trabajo es realista y distinta del caso del texto.
  • Uso al menos 3 verbos tecnicos: check, inspect, replace, test, repair, measure.
