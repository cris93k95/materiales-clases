Identificar el nivel de competencia en inglés de los estudiantes mediante una evaluación diagnóstica integrada con contexto técnico-profesional.

🟢 Inicio (15 min)

Activate prior knowledge and prepare the reading context before working with the technical text.

📚 Activity 4: Reading

Carlos at the Workshop

Texto graduado A2: cinco parrafos, secuencia clara y apoyo de vocabulario para comprender la rutina tecnica.

Carlos works at a small automotive workshop in Santiago. Every morning, he checks the engine and prepares his tools. The reading introduces the workplace, the people involved, and the main task in simple language.

Before the routine continues, the student notices words such as hammer and screwdriver. These words are important because they explain what is used, what is checked, and what should happen next.

He changes oil, inspects the brakes, and uses a wrench and screwdriver to adjust parts. He also checks the battery and electrical circuit. The actions appear in a clear order so the reader can follow the procedure without losing the main idea.

While reading, the student identifies the purpose of each action, the safety detail, and the result of the task. This helps the class connect vocabulary with real workshop decisions.

At the end of the day, Carlos writes a short report and cleans the work area. Technical English helps him read manuals and safety instructions. 📝 Activity 5: Guided comprehension

Answer the questions with evidence from the text. Underline the sentence that helped you before sharing your answer.

  1. What is the main task or routine described in the text?
  2. Which tool, system, or material appears first in the reading?
  3. What actions help the reader understand the sequence of the job?
  4. Which technical words are essential for understanding the text?
  5. Why is English useful in this professional situation?
  6. What idea from the text can you use in your own future workshop context?
