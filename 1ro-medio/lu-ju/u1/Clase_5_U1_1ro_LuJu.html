Electrical vocabulary + reading strategies
Examples

  • Components: wire, cable, switch, outlet, plug, fuse, transformer.
  • Safety: circuit breaker, ground/earth, insulation.
  • Action: connect, disconnect, install, measure, ground.
Sentence frames & tips
  • The current flows through the ___.
  • A ___ protects the circuit from ___.
  • Pronunciation: circuit = SER-kit (the C is silent in the middle).

📖 Actividad 4 — Reading Text — Understanding Basic Electrical Circuits

An electrical circuit is a closed path that allows electric current to flow. Without a closed path, electricity cannot reach the lamp, the appliance or the motor.

A simple circuit has four basic elements: a power source (the battery or the outlet), a conductor (a wire), a load (a lamp or a motor) and a switch. When the switch is closed, the current flows; when the switch is open, the current stops.

In a home installation, the system also includes fuses or circuit breakers. These are safety devices: if too much current flows, they cut the circuit automatically to prevent fires and protect the appliances.

Every conductor is covered with insulation. The insulation prevents the current from escaping and protects people from electric shocks. A good electrician always checks the insulation before any other test.

📝 Actividad 5 — Reading comprehension

Responde regresando al texto. Busca evidencia concreta antes de revisar la respuesta sugerida.

1. What is an electrical circuit?

A closed path that allows electric current to flow.

2. Name the four basic elements of a simple circuit.

Power source, conductor (wire), load (lamp or motor), and switch.

3. What happens when the switch is open?

The current stops; the circuit is not closed.

4. What is the function of a fuse or circuit breaker?

To cut the circuit automatically when too much current flows, preventing fires and protecting appliances.

5. Why is insulation important?

Because it prevents the current from escaping and protects people from electric shocks.

6. Infer: why is checking insulation the FIRST step a good electrician takes?

Because a problem in the insulation is the most common cause of shocks and fires, so detecting it early prevents bigger failures.

🚀 Actividad 6 — Apply & Extend: disena y explica un circuito propio

Pasa de teoria a diseno: vas a crear un circuito simple para una situacion real y a justificar cada componente.
  1. Imagina una situacion real: una lampara de mesa, una linterna casera, un timbre, un ventilador pequeno.
  2. Dibuja el circuito con los 4 elementos basicos. Etiqueta cada uno en ingles.
  3. Escribe 4 oraciones explicando como funciona, una por elemento (The current comes from..., then it goes through..., it reaches..., the switch controls...).
  4. Anade UN dispositivo de seguridad (fuse o breaker) y justifica por que lo agregas.
