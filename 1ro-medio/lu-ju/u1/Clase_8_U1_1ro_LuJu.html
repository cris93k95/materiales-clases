🧠 Actividad 2 — Language Focus

Vas a leer 5 mini-perfiles, uno por especialidad, y comparar lo que hacen, las herramientas que usan y lo que les gusta.

Cross-specialty comparison
Examples
  • Compare verbs: repair (automotive) / weld (industrial) / install (electrical) / build (electronics) / design (graphic).
  • Compare tools: wrench / lathe / multimeter / soldering iron / printing press.
  • Compare workplaces: workshop / factory / building / lab / studio.
  • Compare focus: mechanical / industrial / electrical / digital / visual.
Sentence frames & tips
  • Camila works as an electrician. She installs wiring systems.
  • Andres is a graphic designer. He designs logos and posters.
  • Use simple Present and the verb of the specialty.

📖 Actividad 4 — Reading Text — Five Careers, Five Futures

1. Andres — Automotive: I work at a small automotive workshop. Every day I check engines, change oil and inspect brakes. I use wrenches, screwdrivers and a multimeter. I like the satisfaction of solving a mechanical problem.

2. Sofia — Industrial: I work at a metal structures factory. I cut and weld steel pieces using a welding torch and a grinding wheel. I always wear my PPE. I like the precision and the strength of the metal.

3. Camila — Electricity: I install wiring systems in new buildings. I work with cables, switches, outlets and circuit breakers. I use a multimeter every day. I like making sure that everything works safely.

4. Matias — Electronics: I build small electronic devices in a lab. I solder resistors and microcontrollers on PCBs. I use a soldering iron and a oscilloscope. I like the challenge of programming and the beauty of a clean circuit.

5. Valentina — Graphic Arts: I design posters and books in a small studio. I prepare digital files and check proofs before printing. I work with offset printing and CMYK. I like creating something visual that people will see and use.

📝 Actividad 5 — Reading comprehension

Responde regresando al texto. Busca evidencia concreta antes de revisar la respuesta sugerida.

1. Who works with a welding torch?

Sofia (industrial).

2. Which two professionals use a multimeter?

Andres (automotive) and Camila (electricity).

3. What does Valentina prepare before printing?

Digital files and proofs.

4. Where does Matias work?

In a lab, building small electronic devices.

5. Which professional mentions wearing PPE?

Sofia (industrial).

6. Infer: which two specialties seem closest to each other and why?

Electricity and electronics, because both work with circuits and use a multimeter, but electricity focuses on energy distribution while electronics focuses on signal control.

🚀 Actividad 6 — Apply & Extend: tu perfil profesional FUTURO

Hoy escribes el mini-perfil de la persona que quieres ser. Lo necesitaras de base para la Nota 1 (presentacion oral).
  1. Elige UNA de las 5 especialidades como tu vocacion principal.
  2. Escribe tu perfil siguiendo la estructura del texto: name + specialty + daily tasks + tools you use + workplace + what you like.
  3. Usa al menos 2 verbos tecnicos de tu especialidad y 3 herramientas/equipos nombrados en ingles.
  4. Cierra con UNA oracion mostrando proyeccion: In 5 years, I want to ___.
✅ Auto-chequeo antes de entregar
  • Mi perfil cabe en 5-7 oraciones, no es una lista.
  • Mis herramientas estan correctamente nombradas en ingles.
  • Mi proyeccion es realista y concreta.
  • Mi texto se puede leer en voz alta sin trabarse: ya estoy preparando la Nota 1.
