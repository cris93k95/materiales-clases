🧠 Actividad 2 — Language Focus

Una buena descripcion oral combina: orden claro (conectores), vocabulario tecnico, voz audible y contacto visual.

Oral description of a technical process
Examples
  • First, you connect the negative cable to the battery.
  • Then, you check the voltage with a multimeter.
  • After that, you turn on the switch.
  • Finally, you verify that the system is working.
Sentence frames & tips
  • You + verb in present simple for general instructions.
  • Make sure to ___ for warnings.
  • Slow down, articulate technical words.

📖 Actividad 4 — Reading Text — Sample Script: How to Measure Voltage with a Multimeter

Step 1. First, turn on the multimeter and select the V (voltage) setting. Make sure to choose AC for outlets or DC for batteries.

Step 2. Then, take the red probe and the black probe. The black probe always goes to the COM port; the red probe goes to the V port.

Step 3. After that, place the black probe on the negative terminal and the red probe on the positive terminal. For an outlet, place the probes carefully in each hole.

Step 4. Next, read the value on the screen. A 9V battery should show approximately 9 volts; a wall outlet should show approximately 220 volts in Chile.

Step 5. Finally, remove the probes carefully. If the reading is much lower than expected, the battery is weak or the connection has a problem.

📝 Actividad 5 — Reading comprehension

Responde regresando al texto. Busca evidencia concreta antes de revisar la respuesta sugerida.

1. What is the first step in measuring voltage?

Turn on the multimeter and select the V setting.

2. What is the difference between AC and DC?

AC is for outlets, DC is for batteries.

3. Where does the black probe always go?

To the COM port of the multimeter.

4. What value should a 9V battery show?

Approximately 9 volts.

5. What might it mean if the reading is much lower than expected?

The battery is weak or the connection has a problem.

6. Infer: why do we use sequence connectors in spoken instructions?

Because they help the listener follow the order; without them, instructions sound confusing and steps may be missed.
