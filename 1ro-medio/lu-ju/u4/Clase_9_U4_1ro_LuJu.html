Actividad 2 - Language Focus

Los textos sobre innovacion combinan descripcion, posibilidad, opinion y proyeccion. No basta entender la tecnologia: hay que entender como cambia el trabajo tecnico.

Innovation language in Graphics and printing
Examples
  • Main change: Printing has moved from manual presses to digital workflows, variable data and even 3D output for specialized needs.
  • New tools: digital prepress, color management software, automated cutters and 3D printers extend what print professionals can produce.
  • Benefit: production becomes more flexible, personalized and efficient for different clients and formats.
  • Risk or challenge: professionals who stay only with old routines may miss new workflows, while those who rush into technology may ignore design quality and process control.
Sentence frames and tips
  • This innovation can ___.
  • Technicians will need to ___.
  • Although the technology is useful, ___.
  • The main benefit is ___ because ___.

Actividad 4 - Reading Text - From Gutenberg to 3D: The Evolution of Printing

Printing has moved from manual presses to digital workflows, variable data and even 3D output for specialized needs is changing the world of graphics and printing. In the past, many tasks depended mostly on observation and manual routine. Today, technicians work with systems that collect, compare and share information in real time.

In this new context, digital prepress, color management software, automated cutters and 3D printers extend what print professionals can produce. These changes create clear advantages: production becomes more flexible, personalized and efficient for different clients and formats. Companies and schools that adapt quickly can work with better precision, faster decisions and more preventive maintenance.

However, innovation also creates pressure. professionals who stay only with old routines may miss new workflows, while those who rush into technology may ignore design quality and process control. That is why future technicians will need more than vocabulary. They will need judgment, communication and the ability to keep learning as tools evolve.

The text suggests one key idea: technology does not remove the human role. It changes the role. The most valuable worker will be the technician who understands the system, explains decisions clearly and keeps improving their ability to combine design judgment with digital production tools.
