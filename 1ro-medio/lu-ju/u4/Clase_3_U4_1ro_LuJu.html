Actividad 2 - Language Focus

Las tag questions se usan para confirmar informacion compartida, pedir acuerdo rapido o verificar que una accion de seguridad ya se hizo.

Tag questions for safety checks
Examples
  • You checked the cable, didn't you?
  • The emergency exit is clear, isn't it?
  • We must wear goggles here, mustn't we?
  • This machine is off, isn't it?
Sentence frames and tips
  • You ___, didn't you?
  • This ___, isn't it?
  • We must ___, mustn't we?
  • Positive statement -> negative tag. Negative statement -> positive tag.

Actividad 4 - Reading Text - Checklist Questions That Prevent Accidents

Before starting a practical class, a supervisor may say: The machine is unplugged, isn't it? You are wearing gloves, aren't you? The floor is dry, isn't it? These questions are short, but they force students to pay attention to details that are easy to ignore.

Tag questions are useful because they sound natural and quick. They invite confirmation, but they also remind the listener that safety checks are expected, not optional.

When students use them correctly, they develop a habit of verifying conditions before touching tools or equipment. This habit can prevent incidents that begin with small assumptions.

Actividad 5 - Comprehension and evidence

Responde con evidencia. Vuelve al texto, al transcript o al modelo antes de revisar la respuesta sugerida.

1. Why does a supervisor use short confirmation questions?

Because they are fast, natural and force attention to important safety details.

2. Give one example of a tag question from the text.

Possible answers: The machine is unplugged, isn't it? / You are wearing gloves, aren't you? / The floor is dry, isn't it?

3. Why are tag questions linked to safety habits?

Because they train students to verify conditions before using tools or equipment.

4. What can happen when people make small assumptions?

Those assumptions can lead to incidents.

5. How do tag questions sound in a checklist context?

Quick, natural and focused on confirmation.

6. Infer: why is confirmation language especially valuable in technical environments?

Because technical work depends on small conditions being correct, and confirming them reduces preventable mistakes.

Actividad 6 - Apply & Extend: cuestionario de inspeccion

Disenaras un safety check que sirva de verdad antes de una actividad practica.
  1. Crea una checklist de 8 puntos para un espacio tecnico.
  2. Escribe cada punto como afirmacion + tag question.
  3. Prueba la checklist con un companero y detecta si alguna pregunta suena rara o poco natural.
  4. Corrige 2 items para que sean mas rapidos y mas utiles.
Auto-chequeo antes de entregar
  • Todas mis tag questions concuerdan con el verbo principal.
  • Los puntos de inspeccion son concretos.
  • La checklist puede usarse en menos de 1 minuto.
  • Hice mejoras despues de probarla oralmente.
