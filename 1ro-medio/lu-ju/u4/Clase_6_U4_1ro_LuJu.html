Actividad 2 - Language Focus

Los textos sobre innovacion combinan descripcion, posibilidad, opinion y proyeccion. No basta entender la tecnologia: hay que entender como cambia el trabajo tecnico.

Innovation language in Automotive repair
Examples
  • Main change: Electric vehicles, sensors and diagnostic software are transforming automotive repair.
  • New tools: scan tools, battery management systems, software updates and sensor calibration now matter as much as traditional hand tools.
  • Benefit: technicians can find faults faster and perform more preventive maintenance.
  • Risk or challenge: workers who never update their skills may understand the engine but fail to interpret digital systems.
Sentence frames and tips
  • This innovation can ___.
  • Technicians will need to ___.
  • Although the technology is useful, ___.
  • The main benefit is ___ because ___.

Actividad 4 - Reading Text - The Future of Automotive Repair

Electric vehicles, sensors and diagnostic software are transforming automotive repair is changing the world of automotive repair. In the past, many tasks depended mostly on observation and manual routine. Today, technicians work with systems that collect, compare and share information in real time.

In this new context, scan tools, battery management systems, software updates and sensor calibration now matter as much as traditional hand tools. These changes create clear advantages: technicians can find faults faster and perform more preventive maintenance. Companies and schools that adapt quickly can work with better precision, faster decisions and more preventive maintenance.

However, innovation also creates pressure. workers who never update their skills may understand the engine but fail to interpret digital systems. That is why future technicians will need more than vocabulary. They will need judgment, communication and the ability to keep learning as tools evolve.

The text suggests one key idea: technology does not remove the human role. It changes the role. The most valuable worker will be the technician who understands the system, explains decisions clearly and keeps improving their ability to learn new digital tools.
