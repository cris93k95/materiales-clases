Desarrollo

Actividad 2 - Language Focus

Una buena presentacion no lee el planner; lo transforma en una historia breve y clara sobre intereses, habilidades y proyeccion.

Turning a planner into a spoken presentation
Examples
  • Opening: Today I want to talk about the career path that interests me most.
  • Body: The main skills I need are ___ because ___.
  • Action step: This year I plan to ___ so that I can improve.
  • Closing: My goal is realistic because ___ .
Sentence frames and tips
  • I am interested in ___ because ___.
  • One strength I already have is ___.
  • One skill I still need is ___.
  • This year I will ___ so that ___.

Actividad 4 - Reading Text - Presentation Blueprint: Introducing My Career Path

Today I want to talk about the career path that interests me most. I am interested in electricity because I enjoy practical problem-solving and I like the idea of working with systems that help people and buildings function safely.

One strength I already have is responsibility, but I still need to improve my technical English and my confidence when speaking in front of others. This year I plan to watch tutorials, review vocabulary and participate more actively in practical tasks so that I can prepare better.

My goal is realistic because I know that progress does not happen in one step. It happens through practice, feedback and consistency.

Actividad 5 - Comprehension and evidence

Responde con evidencia. Vuelve al texto, al transcript o al modelo antes de revisar la respuesta sugerida.

1. What career path does the model student choose?

Electricity.

2. What strength does the student already have?

Responsibility.

3. What two areas still need improvement?

Technical English and confidence when speaking in front of others.

4. What action plan is mentioned?

Watching tutorials, reviewing vocabulary and participating more actively in practical tasks.

5. Why does the student say the goal is realistic?

Because progress happens through practice, feedback and consistency.

6. Infer: why are cue cards better than full paragraphs in an oral presentation?

Because they support natural speaking without pushing the student to read word for word.

Actividad 6 - Apply & Extend: ensayo con retroalimentacion

Hoy ensayas para mejorar, no para demostrar perfeccion. Tu companero te ayudara a identificar un siguiente paso claro.
  1. Prepara cue cards con palabras clave, no frases completas.
  2. Presenta tu proyecto en 1-2 minutos a una pareja.
  3. Recibe peer feedback en 3 criterios: clarity, confidence, useful vocabulary.
  4. Escribe un revision target concreto para la clase 16.
Auto-chequeo antes de entregar

  • La presentacion tuvo inicio, cuerpo y cierre.
