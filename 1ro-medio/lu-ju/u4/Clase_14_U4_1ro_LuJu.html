Objetivo de la clase

Planificar un proyecto personal titulado My Career Path, organizando ideas sobre intereses, habilidades, formacion y metas futuras.

Inicio

Actividad 1 - Activate and Predict

  1. Piensa en una especialidad, oficio o area tecnica que te interese hoy.
  2. Responde tres preguntas rapidas: what attracts you, what worries you, what do you need to learn?
  3. Observa la estructura del proyecto final antes de escribir cualquier detalle.

Repaso rapido de la clase anterior

Las clases anteriores ofrecieron contenido sobre innovacion y empleabilidad; ahora todo se transforma en un proyecto personal guiado.

Desarrollo

Actividad 2 - Language Focus

Un proyecto fuerte no nace de escribir mucho, sino de organizar bien: interes, habilidades, formacion, desafios y proyeccion.

Planning a personal technical project
Examples
  • Section 1: My area of interest is ___.
  • Section 2: The skills that I need are ___.
  • Section 3: To prepare better, I should ___.
  • Section 4: In the future, I would like to ___ because ___.
Sentence frames and tips
  • I am interested in ___ because ___.
  • The skills I need include ___.
  • To prepare, I will ___.
  • My future goal is to ___.

Actividad 4 - Reading Text - Model Planner: My Career Path

My area of interest is electronics because I enjoy solving problems that involve systems, devices and precision. I like the idea of understanding both hardware and digital control.

The skills I need include patience, careful observation, teamwork and stronger technical English. I also need to keep improving digital literacy because future systems will be more connected.

To prepare, I can watch tutorials, practice vocabulary, ask questions during practical classes and build a habit of reading about new technologies. My goal is not only to choose a field, but to become ready for it step by step.

Actividad 5 - Comprehension and evidence

Responde con evidencia. Vuelve al texto, al transcript o al modelo antes de revisar la respuesta sugerida.

1. What area of interest does the model student choose?

Electronics.

2. Why does that area attract the student?

Because they enjoy solving problems involving systems, devices and precision.

3. What skills are mentioned as necessary?

Patience, careful observation, teamwork, stronger technical English and digital literacy.

4. What preparation actions are suggested?

Watching tutorials, practicing vocabulary, asking questions and reading about new technologies.

5. What is the final goal according to the text?

Not only to choose a field, but to become ready for it step by step.

6. Infer: why is self-assessment important before starting the final project?

Because students need to know both their interests and their gaps in order to plan realistic goals.

Actividad 6 - Apply & Extend: plan maestro del proyecto

Hoy sales con la estructura completa de tu proyecto, no con frases sueltas.
  1. Completa un planner con 5 secciones: interest - current strengths - skills to develop - actions this year - future goal.
  2. Escribe al menos 2 ideas por seccion.
  3. Marca con color las ideas que ya puedes explicar en ingles y subraya las que aun necesitas preparar.
  4. Comparte tu planner con un companero y recibe una pregunta desafiante para mejorar tu foco.
Auto-chequeo antes de entregar
  • Tengo las 5 secciones completas.
  • Mis ideas son personales y concretas.
  • Se nota una relacion entre habilidades y metas.
  • La pregunta del companero me ayudo a precisar algo.
