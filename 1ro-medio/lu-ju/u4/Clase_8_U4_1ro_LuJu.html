Actividad 2 - Language Focus

Los textos sobre innovacion combinan descripcion, posibilidad, opinion y proyeccion. No basta entender la tecnologia: hay que entender como cambia el trabajo tecnico.

Innovation language in Electricity and electronics
Examples
  • Main change: Smart homes combine sensors, remote control systems and connected electronics to manage light, temperature, access and security.
  • New tools: app-controlled switches, programmable controllers, security sensors and networked devices are increasingly common.
  • Benefit: users gain comfort, energy efficiency and better control of their spaces.
  • Risk or challenge: poor installation, weak cybersecurity or misunderstanding of the system can create new technical and safety problems.
Sentence frames and tips
  • This innovation can ___.
  • Technicians will need to ___.
  • Although the technology is useful, ___.
  • The main benefit is ___ because ___.

Actividad 4 - Reading Text - Smart Homes and Connected Electronics

Smart homes combine sensors, remote control systems and connected electronics to manage light, temperature, access and security is changing the world of electricity and electronics. In the past, many tasks depended mostly on observation and manual routine. Today, technicians work with systems that collect, compare and share information in real time.

In this new context, app-controlled switches, programmable controllers, security sensors and networked devices are increasingly common. These changes create clear advantages: users gain comfort, energy efficiency and better control of their spaces. Companies and schools that adapt quickly can work with better precision, faster decisions and more preventive maintenance.

However, innovation also creates pressure. poor installation, weak cybersecurity or misunderstanding of the system can create new technical and safety problems. That is why future technicians will need more than vocabulary. They will need judgment, communication and the ability to keep learning as tools evolve.

The text suggests one key idea: technology does not remove the human role. It changes the role. The most valuable worker will be the technician who understands the system, explains decisions clearly and keeps improving their ability to combine technical installation with responsible system design.
