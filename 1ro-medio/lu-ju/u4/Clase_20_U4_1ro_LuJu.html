Objetivo de la clase

Reflexionar sobre los aprendizajes del anio, reconociendo avances en ingles tecnico, desafios superados y metas futuras.

Inicio

  1. Mira hacia atras: que palabra, clase o actividad recuerdas mas del anio?
  2. Completa una linea de tiempo personal con 3 momentos importantes.
  3. Piensa que version de ti llego al inicio del anio y que version se va ahora.

Repaso rapido de la clase anterior

Desarrollo

Actividad 2 - Language Focus

Reflexionar no es repetir todo lo hecho. Es seleccionar evidencias de crecimiento, reconocer dificultades reales y proyectar un siguiente paso con sentido.

Closing the year with reflection and projection
Examples
  • Then: At the beginning of the year, I felt ___.
  • Now: Now I can ___ in technical English.
  • Challenge: One difficult point was ___, but I improved by ___.
  • Next step: Next year I want to ___ .
Sentence frames and tips
  • At the beginning of the year, I ___.
  • Now I can ___ better than before.
  • One challenge was ___, but I learned to ___.
  • Next year I want to ___.

Actividad 4 - Reading Text - A Letter to My Future Self

At the beginning of the year, technical English felt difficult and distant. Many words were new, and speaking in front of others felt uncomfortable. However, little by little, practice, reading, listening and feedback made a difference.

Now I can understand more vocabulary, follow technical cases better and explain some ideas with more confidence. I still have things to improve, especially speaking and speed, but I am not in the same place as before.

For next year, I want to keep learning because technical English is not only a school subject. It is a tool that can open future opportunities if I continue to use it with intention.

Actividad 5 - Comprehension and evidence

Responde con evidencia. Vuelve al texto, al transcript o al modelo antes de revisar la respuesta sugerida.

1. How did the writer feel at the beginning of the year?

Technical English felt difficult and distant, and speaking felt uncomfortable.

2. What changed over time?

Practice, reading, listening and feedback made a difference.

3. What can the writer do better now?

Understand more vocabulary, follow technical cases and explain ideas with more confidence.

4. What still needs improvement?

Speaking and speed.

5. Why does the writer want to keep learning next year?

Because technical English is a tool that can open future opportunities.

6. Infer: why is it important to name unfinished challenges at the end of the year?

Because honest reflection helps set realistic next steps and shows growth is still in progress.

Actividad 6 - Apply & Extend: portafolio de cierre anual

Tu tarea final es dejar evidencia de tu proceso y proyectar el siguiente paso.
  1. Completa una hoja de cierre con 4 bloques: what I learned - what was hard - what I improved - what I want next year.
  2. Elige 2 evidencias concretas del anio: una clase, una actividad, una evaluacion o una palabra que muestre progreso.
  3. Escribe una carta corta a tu yo del futuro usando al menos 3 sentence frames del focus.
  4. Si quieres, comparte una linea final con el curso como cierre colectivo.
Auto-chequeo antes de entregar
  • Mi reflexion incluye evidencias concretas.
  • Reconozco una dificultad real y una mejora real.
  • Mi carta mira al futuro, no solo al pasado.
  • Puedo sentir que esta hoja me representa.
