Actividad 2 - Language Focus

La seguridad no es un detalle administrativo. Es una forma de pensar antes de actuar, mientras se trabaja y al cerrar una tarea.

Safety as a professional habit
Examples
  • Obligation: You must inspect the area before starting.
  • Prohibition: You must not ignore damaged equipment.
  • Prevention: PPE reduces risk before the problem appears.
  • Universal idea: The same safety logic applies across specialties.
Sentence frames and tips
  • You must ___ before ___.
  • You must not ___ in the workshop.
  • This rule matters because ___.
  • The risk appears when ___.

Actividad 4 - Reading Text - Safety First: A Universal Rule

In an auto workshop, a forgotten tool can damage an engine. In an electrical room, a wet floor can become a serious danger. In electronics, static discharge can ruin a board. In graphics, moving parts and chemical products also require attention. Different specialties use different tools, but all of them depend on safe habits.

Professionals do not wait for accidents to remember safety. They inspect their space, use protective equipment, follow protocols and report irregular situations early. These actions may seem small, but they prevent expensive errors and protect people from harm.

The best workers are not only fast or skilled. They are also reliable. A reliable technician understands that safety is part of quality, not separate from it.

Actividad 5 - Comprehension and evidence

Responde con evidencia. Vuelve al texto, al transcript o al modelo antes de revisar la respuesta sugerida.

1. Name two specialty-specific risks mentioned in the text.

Possible answers: a forgotten tool damaging an engine, a wet floor in an electrical room, static discharge in electronics, moving parts or chemicals in graphics.

2. What do professionals do instead of waiting for accidents?

They inspect spaces, use protective equipment, follow protocols and report irregular situations early.

3. Why do small actions matter according to the text?

Because they prevent expensive errors and protect people from harm.

4. What makes a technician reliable in this text?

Understanding that safety is part of quality, not separate from it.

5. Why is safety called a universal rule?

Because the same preventive logic applies in all specialties even if the tools and risks are different.

6. Infer: why does the text connect safety with quality?

Because unsafe work can damage products, systems and people, so quality cannot exist without safe processes.

Actividad 6 - Apply & Extend: mapa de riesgos del taller
