Desarrollo

Actividad 2 - Language Focus

Un buen safety briefing es corto, claro y accionable. Debe priorizar riesgos reales y dejar a la audiencia con una accion concreta.

Structure of a short safety briefing
Examples
  • Opening: Today we will focus on two risks before we begin.
  • Main rule: You must check both the cables and the floor condition.
  • Confirmation: The emergency exit is clear, isn't it?
  • Closing: If you see something unusual, report it immediately.
Sentence frames and tips
  • Today we need to focus on ___.
  • You must / must not ___.
  • The ___ is ready, isn't it?
  • If you see ___, report it immediately.

Actividad 4 - Reading Text - Model Safety Briefing Before Workshop Practice

Good morning, everyone. Before we begin, we need to focus on two risks in today's activity: loose cables near the workbench and missing eye protection. Both issues can cause preventable accidents.

Please check your area before you start. The floor is dry, isn't it? Your goggles are on, aren't they? If something is missing or damaged, stop and report it before using any equipment.

Remember: working fast is never more important than working safely. One small report now can prevent a bigger problem later.

Actividad 5 - Comprehension and evidence

Responde con evidencia. Vuelve al texto, al transcript o al modelo antes de revisar la respuesta sugerida.

1. What are the two risks mentioned in the briefing?

Loose cables near the workbench and missing eye protection.

2. What two tag questions are used?

The floor is dry, isn't it? / Your goggles are on, aren't they?

3. What should students do if something is missing or damaged?

Stop and report it before using any equipment.

4. What final principle closes the briefing?

Working fast is never more important than working safely.

5. Why is the message effective?

Because it is short, concrete and leaves the audience with a clear action.

6. Infer: why do briefings mention only a few priorities instead of every possible danger?

Because listeners remember short, focused messages better and can act on them immediately.

Actividad 6 - Apply & Extend: tu safety briefing de 1 minuto

Haras un briefing breve para una actividad tecnica real o probable. Debe sonar util, no como lectura de diapositiva.
  1. Elige una actividad tecnica y detecta 2 riesgos prioritarios.
  2. Escribe un guion de 6-8 lineas con apertura, riesgos, instruccion, confirmacion y cierre.
  3. Incluye al menos 1 tag question y 1 regla con must/must not.
  4. Ensaya con un companero y mejora tono, claridad y tiempo.
Auto-chequeo antes de entregar
  • Mi mensaje dura cerca de 1 minuto.
  • Incluyo 2 riesgos reales y accionables.
  • Uso al menos 1 tag question correcta.
  • Mi cierre deja una accion clara a la audiencia.
