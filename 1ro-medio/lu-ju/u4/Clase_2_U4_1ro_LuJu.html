Desarrollo

Actividad 2 - Language Focus

Un protocolo bien leido muestra orden, prioridad y responsabilidad. No es una lista al azar: es una secuencia para reducir riesgo.

Reading protocols and sequences
Examples
  • Before: Inspect PPE and work area.
  • During: Follow machine instructions and stay alert.
  • After: Clean tools, disconnect power and report issues.
  • Emergency: Stop work, alert others and follow evacuation routes.
Sentence frames and tips
  • Before starting, students must ___.
  • During the task, they must not ___.
  • After finishing, they should ___.
  • In an emergency, the first action is ___.

Actividad 4 - Reading Text - Workshop Safety Protocol - Technical School

Before starting, each student must inspect personal protective equipment, tie back loose hair, and check that the workstation is clean and ready. No machine should be used without teacher authorization.

During practical work, students must follow instructions, avoid distractions and report any unusual sound, smell or movement immediately. Tools must be used only for their intended function, and protective barriers must remain in place.

After finishing, equipment must be shut down correctly, tools returned, surfaces cleaned and incidents reported. In an emergency, the class must stop, alert the supervisor and follow the evacuation route without panic.

Actividad 5 - Comprehension and evidence

Responde con evidencia. Vuelve al texto, al transcript o al modelo antes de revisar la respuesta sugerida.

1. What must students inspect before starting?

Their personal protective equipment and the workstation.

2. Can a machine be used without teacher authorization?

No, it cannot.

3. What should students report during practical work?

Any unusual sound, smell or movement.

4. What must happen after finishing?

Equipment is shut down safely, tools are returned, surfaces are cleaned and incidents are reported.

5. What is the first reaction in an emergency?

Stop, alert the supervisor and follow the evacuation route without panic.

6. Infer: why does the protocol mention unusual smell and sound, not only visible problems?

Because danger signs are not always visual; noticing sound or smell early can prevent accidents.

Actividad 6 - Apply & Extend: protocolo adaptado

Vas a adaptar el protocolo a un espacio tecnico especifico. La meta es volverlo aplicable, no copiarlo literal.
  1. Elige un espacio: auto bay, electrical room, electronics lab, print room or computer lab.
  2. Escribe un mini-protocol with 4 sections: before - during - after - emergency.
  3. Incluye 2 reglas universales y 2 reglas propias del espacio elegido.
  4. Cierra con una linea sobre el error mas comun que tu protocolo quiere prevenir.
Auto-chequeo antes de entregar
  • Mi protocolo esta organizado por momentos.
  • Incluyo reglas universales y reglas especificas.
  • El error final es realista para ese espacio.
  • Otra persona podria seguir este protocolo sin preguntarme nada.
