Desarrollo

Actividad 2 - Language Focus

Los reportes de accidente no buscan solo culpar. Buscan reconstruir hechos, detectar acciones inseguras y proponer cambios para evitar repeticion.

Accident reports and preventive thinking
Examples
  • Event: A worker received an electric shock.
  • Unsafe action: The panel was not fully isolated.
  • Consequence: The task stopped and emergency protocol was activated.
  • Prevention: Verification should happen before touching the system.
Sentence frames and tips
  • The incident happened when ___.
  • The unsafe action was ___.
  • This could have been prevented if ___.
  • The main lesson is ___.

Actividad 4 - Reading Text - Workplace Accident Report: Electric Shock at the Assembly Line

At 10:15 a.m., a maintenance worker at an assembly line received an electric shock while checking a control panel. The worker stepped back immediately, and a nearby colleague activated the emergency stop and called for help.

The incident investigation showed that the panel had not been fully isolated from the power source. A warning sign was present, but the final verification step had been rushed because the line was under production pressure.

The worker received first aid and recovered, but the event stopped production for the rest of the shift. The final report recommended stricter lockout procedures, verbal confirmation between workers, and a rule that no technician may bypass the final safety check.

Actividad 5 - Comprehension and evidence

Responde con evidencia. Vuelve al texto, al transcript o al modelo antes de revisar la respuesta sugerida.

1. When did the accident happen?

At 10:15 a.m. during a control panel check.

2. What did the nearby colleague do?

Activated the emergency stop and called for help.

3. What unsafe condition or action did the investigation find?

The panel had not been fully isolated and the final verification step had been rushed.

4. Why was the final safety check rushed?

Because the line was under production pressure.

5. What corrective measures were recommended?

Stricter lockout procedures, verbal confirmation and no bypassing of the final safety check.

6. Infer: why is production pressure dangerous in safety contexts?

Because it can push workers to skip or rush critical verification steps, increasing the risk of accidents.

Actividad 6 - Apply & Extend: investigacion y prevencion

Tu tarea no es repetir el accidente. Es pensar como un equipo que investiga y mejora el sistema.
  1. Completa una ficha con 4 bloques: event - unsafe action - immediate response - preventive measure.
  2. Imagina un accidente distinto de tu especialidad y escribe un mini-report de 6-8 lineas.
  3. Incluye una leccion final para el equipo.
  4. Compara con el caso de hoy: que se parece y que cambia?
Auto-chequeo antes de entregar
  • Mi nuevo accidente es distinto al shock electrico.
  • La medida preventiva ataca la causa, no solo la consecuencia.
  • Mi mini-report tiene orden claro.
  • La comparacion final muestra criterio y no solo lista diferencias.
