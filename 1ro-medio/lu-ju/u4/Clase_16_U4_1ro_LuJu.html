Desarrollo

Actividad 2 - Language Focus

La presentacion final no termina cuando dejas de hablar. Tambien cuenta como respondes una pregunta y como sostienes tu idea central.

Final rehearsal and question handling
Examples
  • Question: Why does this career interest you?
  • Answer: It interests me because I enjoy practical work and I want to develop technical problem-solving.
  • Follow-up: What skill do you still need to improve?
  • Closing: This path motivates me because it connects my interests with real future opportunities.
Sentence frames and tips
  • This career interests me because ___.
  • I still need to improve ___.
  • This year I will ___ to prepare better.
  • My final idea is that ___.

Actividad 4 - Reading Text - Model Q&A: Answering Questions About Your Career Plan

Teacher: Why are you interested in graphics? Student: I am interested in graphics because I enjoy visual communication and I want to learn how design and technology work together.

Teacher: What skill do you still need to improve? Student: I still need to improve my confidence when speaking and my digital software skills.

Teacher: What will you do this year to prepare? Student: I will practice more, ask for feedback and explore new tools so that I can build a stronger base.

Actividad 5 - Comprehension and evidence

Responde con evidencia. Vuelve al texto, al transcript o al modelo antes de revisar la respuesta sugerida.

1. Why is the student interested in graphics?

Because they enjoy visual communication and want to learn how design and technology work together.

2. What skills still need improvement?

Confidence when speaking and digital software skills.

3. What action plan does the student mention?

Practice more, ask for feedback and explore new tools.

4. What does the phrase so that express in the last answer?

Purpose.

5. Why are Q&A skills important in a presentation?

Because they show understanding, flexibility and confidence beyond memorized speech.

6. Infer: why does final rehearsal include questions and not only the main talk?

Because real evaluation often tests how well students sustain their ideas when something unexpected appears.

Actividad 6 - Apply & Extend: simulacion final

Haras una simulacion casi completa del formato evaluado.
  1. Presenta tu proyecto en el tiempo acordado.
  2. Responde 2 preguntas de un companero o del docente.
  3. Registra en una tarjeta final: opening, strongest point, weak point, next fix.
  4. Haz un ultimo ajuste a tu guion o cue cards antes de la evaluacion.
Auto-chequeo antes de entregar
  • Respete el tiempo.
  • Pude responder preguntas sin bloquearme.
  • Identifique un punto fuerte y uno debil real.
  • Hice un ajuste final concreto.
