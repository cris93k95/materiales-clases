Desarrollo

Actividad 2 - Language Focus

La gramatica final no se revisa como lista de reglas aisladas. Se revisa donde se rompe el mensaje tecnico: confirmaciones, relaciones y secuencia.

Grammar clinic before the final project
Examples
  • The printer is off, isn't it?
  • The sensor that controls the system needs calibration.
  • Although the line is fast, safety checks still matter.
  • We changed both the cable and the connector.
Sentence frames and tips
  • This rule is clear, isn't it?
  • The tool that ___ is ___.
  • Although ___, ___.
  • Both ___ and ___ ___.

Actividad 4 - Reading Text - Why Grammar Still Matters in Technical English

Students sometimes believe grammar is separate from technical content. In reality, bad grammar can change instructions, weaken a diagnosis or confuse a recommendation. A wrong tag question can make a safety check sound strange. A missing connector can hide the logic of a solution.

Before a final project, a grammar clinic helps students notice patterns in their own mistakes. The goal is not perfection; it is clearer professional communication.

Actividad 5 - Comprehension and evidence

Responde con evidencia. Vuelve al texto, al transcript o al modelo antes de revisar la respuesta sugerida.

1. Why can bad grammar be a technical problem?

Because it can change instructions, weaken a diagnosis or confuse a recommendation.

2. What example does the text give about tag questions?

That a wrong tag question can make a safety check sound strange.

3. What can a missing connector hide?

The logic of a solution.

4. What is the goal of a grammar clinic?

Clearer professional communication, not perfection.

5. Why is this class placed before the final project?

Because students need to notice and correct their own recurring mistakes before presenting or writing final work.

6. Infer: why is self-correction valuable in a workplace?

Because technicians do not always have a teacher next to them, so noticing and fixing mistakes independently matters.

Actividad 6 - Apply & Extend: hoja de correccion personal

Saldras con un mini-manual de tus errores frecuentes y como corregirlos.
  1. Completa una tabla con 4 filas: my error - corrected version - rule - new example.
  2. Incluye al menos un error de tag question y uno de otra estructura del anio.
  3. Escribe dos repair sentences vinculadas a seguridad o innovacion.
  4. Guarda la hoja para usarla en las clases 14-18.
Auto-chequeo antes de entregar
  • Mis errores son reales y propios.
  • Cada correccion incluye una regla breve.
  • Mis nuevas oraciones estan en contexto tecnico.
  • Podre revisar esta hoja antes del proyecto.
