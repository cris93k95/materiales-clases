📖 Actividad 4 — Reading Text — Process and Description

Texto graduado A2: ideas claras, detalle tecnico suficiente y vocabulario reutilizable para hablar y escribir.

In the school technical workshop, a student team is working on a technical process that must be explained clearly linked to Innovation, Safety & My Future. Their task is to understand how a practice project is prepared, checked, and explained in English.

Before the main procedure begins, the tool kit is prepared and the class reads a short note about process. The teacher asks students to notice the technical verbs and the words that help the reader follow the order of the task.

During the activity, the checklist is used to complete an important stage of the job. Each action is recorded in clear sentences so the next person can understand what is done, what is checked, and what must happen next.

A small difficulty appears when one step is not fully clear, so the students compare evidence, review the glossary, and revisit the example box for Technical English. This helps them decide which detail is essential and which detail only gives extra context.

In the final stage, the group writes a short conclusion, explains the result, and suggests one safety action. The reading shows that technical English is useful because it supports accuracy, teamwork, and better decisions in the workshop.
