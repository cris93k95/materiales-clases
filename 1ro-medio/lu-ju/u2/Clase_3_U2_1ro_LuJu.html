Objetivo de la clase

Analizar un estudio de caso sobre diagnóstico de falla automotriz, aplicando estrategias de lectura para identificar causa, efecto y solución, y usando conectores en inglés.

🟢 Inicio (15 min)

🔎 Actividad 1 — Conectores de causa-efecto + brainstorm

El docente escribe en la pizarra los siguientes conectores y un ejemplo de cada uno:

Causa Conector Efecto
  • The engine overheated because the coolant was low.
  • The battery was dead, so the car wouldn't start.
  • The oil leaked. As a result, the engine was damaged.
  • The brakes were worn out; therefore, the car couldn't stop quickly.
  • The mechanic replaced the part so that the car could work again.

Pregunta detonante: el docente lanza al curso: "A car won't start. What could be the problem?" y anota en la pizarra las posibles fallas que aparezcan (dead battery, no fuel, faulty starter, broken wires…).

🔠 Actividad 3 — Connectors focus

Antes de leer el caso, fijémonos en cómo se usan los conectores de causa-efecto.

Connectors that introduce a CAUSE
  • because → seguido de una oración con sujeto y verbo: "The engine stopped because the oil leaked."
  • because of → seguido de un sustantivo: "The engine stopped because of the oil leak."
  • since → equivalente a because, más formal.
Connectors that introduce an EFFECT
  • so → conecta dos oraciones: "It was hot, so the engine overheated."
  • therefore / as a result → más formal, va al inicio de la 2.ª oración: "The thermostat failed. As a result, the coolant didn't circulate."
  • so that → introduce un propósito: "The mechanic replaced the part so that the engine could work again."

📖 Actividad 4 — Reading: The Mystery of the Overheating Engine

Pre-reading: solo con el título, ¿de qué crees que tratará el texto? Comparte 1 predicción con tu compañero antes de leer.

The Mystery of the Overheating Engine

One Monday morning, Mr. Pérez drove to a small auto repair shop. He was worried because his car was overheating every time he drove for more than ten minutes. "There must be a problem with the engine," he told the mechanic. "The warning light is always on."

The mechanic, Diego, opened the hood and started the diagnosis. "Let's see what's wrong," he said.

First, the coolant level was checked. It was a little low, so Diego added more coolant. He started the engine again, but after a few minutes the temperature was still high. "It's not just the coolant," he thought.

Then, the radiator was inspected. There were no leaks and the fan worked correctly. The water pump was tested too: it pushed coolant normally through the system. "Something is blocking the circulation," Diego said.

Finally, Diego removed the thermostat and put it in hot water. The thermostat didn't open. "Here's the problem!" he said. The thermostat was stuck closed, so the coolant couldn't reach the radiator. As a result, the engine kept overheating.

The thermostat was replaced with a new one. The engine was started again and the temperature stayed normal. The warning light went off. "Now your car is safe," Diego said with a smile.

Mr. Pérez thanked him. "I never imagined that such a small part could cause such a big problem," he said. Diego laughed: "In an engine, every small part is important."

📝 Actividad 6 — Comprehension & connectors

1. ¿Cuál era el síntoma que tenía el auto del Sr. Pérez?

Se sobrecalentaba después de 10 minutos y la luz testigo siempre estaba encendida: "his car was overheating… The warning light is always on."

2. ¿Qué fue lo primero que revisó Diego y por qué no resolvió el problema?

Revisó el nivel de refrigerante y agregó más, pero la temperatura siguió alta, así que entendió que no era solo el refrigerante.

3. ¿Cuál fue la causa real del problema?

El termostato estaba trabado en posición cerrada (stuck closed), por lo que el refrigerante no podía llegar al radiador.

4. Subraya 3 conectores de causa-efecto del texto y escríbelos aquí.

Ejemplos del texto: because (línea 1), so (varias veces), as a result (final del diagnóstico). También aparecen Passive Voice: was checked, was inspected, was tested, was replaced.

5. Une cada causa con su efecto usando un conector (because / so / as a result):

  • a) The thermostat was stuck closed ____ the coolant couldn't circulate.
  • b) The engine overheated ____ the warning light turned on.
  • c) Mr. Pérez went to the shop ____ his car was overheating.
a) so / as a result · b) so / as a result · c) because

6. Inferencia: ¿qué habría pasado si Diego no hubiera revisado el termostato?

El motor habría seguido sobrecalentándose y, como consecuencia, podría haberse dañado seriamente (deformación del block, cabeza del motor, etc.). El texto sugiere esa idea cuando dice "In an engine, every small part is important."

🗣 Actividad 7 — Discussion en grupos de 4

Cada grupo discute las 3 preguntas (10-12 min) y luego comparte su respuesta más interesante con el curso.

  1. "Was the mechanic's diagnostic process logical? Why?"
  2. "What would have happened if the mechanic hadn't checked the thermostat?"
  3. "Have you ever seen a car overheat (in your family, on the street, in a video)? What happened?"

📋 Seguimiento de la clase

Formativa: el docente circula durante la lectura y la discusión. Criterios: (1) identificación correcta de causa-efecto-solución, (2) uso de al menos 2 conectores diferentes al hablar, (3) referencia al texto al responder, (4) participación equitativa en el grupo.

📦 Recursos sugeridos

Materiales: texto "The Mystery of the Overheating Engine" impreso (1 por estudiante o pareja), diagrama causa-efecto en blanco impreso, flashcards de vocabulario de fallas (overheating, leak, thermostat, coolant, etc.), tarjetas grandes con los conectores (because, so, as a result, therefore, so that) para usar en la pizarra.
