🧠 Actividad 2 — Language Focus

Cuando explicamos una falla, no basta con decir que ocurrio: debemos explicar por que ocurrio y que sucede como consecuencia.

Conectores de causa y efecto
Examples
  • The engine overheated because the thermostat was stuck.
  • The thermostat was stuck, so the coolant could not circulate.
  • As a result, the engine temperature increased very quickly.
  • The mechanic checked the cooling system so that the cause could be identified.
Sentence frames & tips
  • Cause + because + reason | Effect + so / as a result + consequence.
  • Always put a comma before so and after As a result.
  • Use so that + Subject + can/could + verb.

📖 Actividad 4 — Reading Text — Case Study: The Mystery of the Overheating Engine

A customer arrived at the workshop with a serious problem: his car was overheating after only ten minutes of driving. The warning light on the dashboard was on and steam was coming out of the engine.

The mechanic listened to the customer and made a first hypothesis: the cooling system was failing. He decided to follow a logical diagnostic process step by step, instead of replacing parts randomly.

First, the coolant level was checked. The level was normal, so a simple lack of liquid was discarded. Next, the water pump was inspected because a broken pump can stop the circulation. The pump was working correctly.

Then, the thermostat was tested. The mechanic discovered that the thermostat was stuck in the closed position. As a result, the coolant could not circulate through the radiator and the engine got hot very quickly.

The faulty thermostat was replaced with a new one and the system was filled again. The car was tested for thirty minutes and the temperature stayed normal. The problem was solved because the mechanic followed a clear diagnostic order.

📝 Actividad 5 — Reading comprehension

Responde regresando al texto. Busca evidencia concreta antes de revisar la respuesta sugerida.

1. What was the initial symptom reported by the customer?

The car was overheating after only ten minutes of driving, with a warning light on and steam from the engine.

2. Why did the mechanic decide not to replace parts randomly?

Because a logical diagnostic process is more reliable than guessing.

3. What was the first thing checked, and what was the result?

The coolant level was checked first; it was normal, so lack of liquid was discarded.

4. What was the real cause of the overheating?

The thermostat was stuck in the closed position, so the coolant could not circulate.

5. How did the mechanic confirm that the problem was solved?

He tested the car for thirty minutes and the temperature stayed normal.

6. Infer: what could have happened if the mechanic had replaced the water pump first?

He would have spent money on an unnecessary part and the real problem (the thermostat) would have remained, so the car would still overheat.
