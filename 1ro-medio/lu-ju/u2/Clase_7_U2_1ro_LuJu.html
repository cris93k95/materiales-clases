🧠 Actividad 2 — Language Focus

Un buen reporte tecnico combina conectores de causa-efecto (por que paso algo) y de contraste (aunque algo no fuera lo esperado).

Conectores integrados: causa-efecto + contraste
Examples
  • The line was stopped because a sensor failed.
  • However, production continued the next shift.
  • Although the part was inspected, a defect was missed.
  • The pump was replaced, so the system worked again.
Sentence frames & tips
  • Causa-efecto: because, so, as a result, therefore, due to, so that.
  • Contraste: although, however, but, on the other hand, even though, while.
  • Position: However, usually at the start of a sentence; although joins two clauses.

📖 Actividad 4 — Reading Text — Technical Incident Reports

Incident 1 — Automotive: The lift in bay 3 was lowered too fast because a hydraulic valve was leaking. As a result, a car bumper was damaged. However, no operator was injured. The valve was replaced and a daily check was added.

Incident 2 — Electrical: The main panel of a workshop tripped at 9 a.m. The cause was an overload due to several heaters connected at the same time. Although the breakers worked correctly, the office was left without power for one hour. The load was redistributed across two circuits.

Incident 3 — Industrial: A CNC machine stopped during a milling job because the cooling fluid was below the minimum level. The operator refilled the tank, so the job could continue. However, ten minutes of production were lost.

All three reports follow the same logic: a problem appears, a cause is identified, a response is given, and a result is reported. The connectors are essential to show the reader the relationship between events.

📝 Actividad 5 — Reading comprehension

Responde regresando al texto. Busca evidencia concreta antes de revisar la respuesta sugerida.

1. In the first incident, why was the bumper damaged?

Because the lift was lowered too fast due to a leaking hydraulic valve.

2. Why was the electrical incident considered controlled even though the office was without power?

Because the breakers worked correctly and protected the system; the load was then redistributed.

3. What was the immediate response of the CNC operator?

He refilled the cooling fluid tank so the milling job could continue.

4. Which contrast connector is used in the first and second incidents?

However in the first and although in the second.

5. What logical structure do the three reports share?

Problem, cause, response, result, all linked with cause-effect and contrast connectors.

6. Infer: why is the position of the connector important in a technical report?

Because the position changes the focus of the reader: because highlights the cause, while however highlights the contrast or limitation of the result.

🚀 Actividad 6 — Apply & Extend: tu propio incident report

Escribe un reporte profesional de 5-6 oraciones. La nota se la juega la logica y los conectores, no la cantidad de texto.
  1. Inventa un incidente tecnico breve en una de estas areas: workshop, lab, kitchen of a restaurant, hardware store, server room.
  2. Sigue esta estructura obligatoria: Problem — Trigger / Cause — Response — Result — Preventive recommendation.
  3. Usa al menos 3 conectores diferentes: 2 de causa-efecto y 1 de contraste.
  4. Marca con colores: azul = causa-efecto, rojo = contraste. Subraya las formas pasivas.
