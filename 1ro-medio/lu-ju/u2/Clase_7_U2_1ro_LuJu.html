🎯 Describir problemas y soluciones técnicas con conectores 📘 OA8 · OA9 🗂 Clase 7 — Unidad 2 🗓 Semana 13 · mayo 🧩 Grammar focus + 3 reportes + escritura en parejas

Objetivo de la clase

Comprender y utilizar conectores de causa-efecto y contraste para describir problemas, causas, acciones y resultados en informes técnicos.

🟢 Inicio (15 min)

🔎 Actividad 1 — ¿Por qué? y ¿con qué resultado?

El docente escribe en la pizarra una oración corta y la usa como detonante:

"The machine stopped."

Pregunta al curso (rápido, voluntarios):

  • "WHY did it stop?" (recoge causas)
  • "What was the RESULT?" (recoge efectos)

Luego presenta las dos categorías de conectores que se trabajarán hoy:

Causa-efecto Contraste
  • Causa-efecto: because, so, therefore, as a result, so that, due to.
  • Contraste: although, however, but, on the other hand, even though, while.

Ejemplo modelo combinado:
"The machine stopped because the motor overheated. However, the operator restarted it after 30 minutes."

🔵 Desarrollo (60 min)

🔠 Actividad 2 — Grammar focus: posición y uso

🔊 Sounds in focus

Conectores con sonidos que cuesta pronunciar. Practícalos en voz alta antes del reading.

/ð/
"th" sonoro
therefore, although, other
Punta de la lengua entre los dientes, vibra (con voz). Parecido a la "d" suave del español "lado".
/aʊ/
diptongo "au"
although, however, about, out
Como "au" en español ("auto"): empieza en "a" y termina en "u".
/w/
"w" inicial
while, was, worker, warning
Labios redondos como para soplar. No es "v" ni "g". Empieza con sonido de "u".
Causa-efecto
  • because + oración con sujeto y verbo: "It stopped because the motor overheated."
  • due to + sustantivo: "It stopped due to overheating."
  • so conecta dos oraciones: "The motor overheated, so the machine stopped."
  • therefore / as a result: van al inicio de la 2.ª oración, con coma. "The motor overheated. As a result, the machine stopped."
  • so that = propósito: "We replaced the part so that the machine could restart."
Contraste
  • but: conecta dos ideas opuestas en una oración. "It was repaired but it failed again."
  • however: inicio de la 2.ª oración, con coma. "It was repaired. However, it failed again."
  • although / even though: al inicio o al medio. "Although the system was new, it failed."
  • while: contraste paralelo. "While offset prints in batches, digital prints one by one."
  • on the other hand: presenta el otro lado. "Digital is fast. On the other hand, offset is cheaper for large runs."

✍️ Actividad 3 — Une las oraciones con el conector correcto

Reescribe cada par usando un conector apropiado (causa-efecto o contraste).

  1. The fuse blew. The lights went off.
  2. The mechanic checked the brakes. He didn't find the problem.
  3. The water pump was new. It failed after a week.
  4. The thermostat was stuck. The engine overheated.
  5. We added more coolant. The temperature stayed high.
  6. The cable was damaged. We replaced it.
  7. Offset is slow to set up. It is cheaper for large runs.
  8. The breaker tripped. The microwave and the kettle were used together.
1. The fuse blew, so the lights went off.
2. The mechanic checked the brakes, but he didn't find the problem.
3. Although the water pump was new, it failed after a week.
4. The engine overheated because the thermostat was stuck.
5. We added more coolant. However, the temperature stayed high.
6. The cable was damaged, so we replaced it.
7. Offset is slow to set up. On the other hand, it is cheaper for large runs.
8. The breaker tripped because the microwave and the kettle were used together.
Otras combinaciones también pueden ser correctas. Lo importante es que el conector exprese bien la relación.

📖 Actividad 4 — Reading: Technical Incident Reports

Tres informes breves. Mientras lees, subraya con AZUL los conectores de causa-efecto y con ROJO los de contraste.

Report #1 · Automotive workshop
Engine failure during routine inspection

A Toyota Hilux was brought to the workshop for a routine check. The mechanic noticed an oil leak under the engine. The vehicle was tested, and the oil pressure dropped quickly because the seal of the oil pan was broken. As a result, the engine could not be lubricated correctly. The seal was replaced and the oil was changed. However, the customer was advised to monitor the oil level for the next two weeks, since the engine had already worked without enough lubrication for several days.

Report #2 · Residential electrical installation
Tripped breaker in a kitchen circuit

An electrician was called to a house in Maipú. The main breaker in the kitchen tripped every morning when the family used the microwave and the kettle at the same time. The circuit was inspected with a multimeter. Although the wiring was in good condition, the breaker was rated only at 16 A, which was not enough for that load. Therefore, the breaker was replaced by a 20 A one and a new branch circuit was added for the microwave. However, the family was reminded not to connect a heater to the same line.

Report #3 · Industrial printing press
Color registration failure during a magazine run

During a magazine print run, the operator noticed that the cyan and magenta colors did not align correctly on the paper. The press was stopped and the registration system was checked. The problem was caused by a small misalignment of the blanket cylinder, due to a worn bearing. Even though the misalignment was less than 1 mm, the final image looked blurry. The bearing was replaced and the press was re-calibrated. As a result, the next 5,000 copies were printed without defects. However, 800 copies had been wasted before the issue was detected.

📝 Actividad 6 — Comprehension & connectors

1. En el Reporte #1, ¿cuál fue la causa real de la caída de presión de aceite?

El sello (seal) del cárter (oil pan) estaba roto, así que el motor perdía aceite y no podía lubricarse correctamente.

2. En el Reporte #2, ¿por qué se cambió el breaker, si el cableado estaba en buen estado?

Porque el breaker original era de solo 16 A y no soportaba la carga combinada del microondas y el hervidor. Se reemplazó por uno de 20 A.

3. En el Reporte #3, ¿cuántas copias se perdieron antes de detectar el problema?

800 copias se desperdiciaron antes de detectar el desalineamiento.

4. Identifica 3 conectores de causa-efecto y 3 de contraste que aparecen en los reportes.

Causa-efecto: because, as a result, since, therefore, due to. Contraste: however, although, even though.

5. Completa con el conector más apropiado (because / however / although / so / as a result):

  • a) The seal was broken, ____ the oil leaked out.
  • b) ____ the wiring was OK, the breaker was too small.
  • c) The press was re-calibrated. ____ , 800 copies had already been wasted.
  • d) The bearing was worn ____ it had not been replaced for years.
a) so / as a result · b) Although · c) However · d) because.

6. Inferencia: ¿qué tienen en común los 3 reportes en cuanto a cómo se redactan?

Los 3 siguen la misma estructura: descripción del problema → diagnóstico (causa) → acción tomada → resultado. Todos usan Passive Voice (was brought, was called, was inspected, was replaced) y conectores de causa-efecto y contraste para enlazar las ideas.

✍️ Actividad 7 — Writing + Oral: tu propio incidente técnico

En parejas, inventen un incidente técnico ficticio (5-6 oraciones) usando al menos 3 conectores diferentes (mínimo 1 de causa-efecto y 1 de contraste).

  1. Elijan un contexto: taller automotriz, instalación eléctrica, imprenta, fábrica…
  2. Describan: (a) Problema observado, (b) Diagnóstico/causa, (c) Acción tomada, (d) Resultado.
  3. Lean su reporte oralmente a otra pareja, como si fuera un informe formal.
  4. La otra pareja debe identificar los conectores usados y clasificarlos (causa-efecto / contraste).

Modelo de apertura: "On Monday morning, a __________ was brought / called to __________. The technician noticed that __________."

📋 Seguimiento de la clase

Formativa: el docente revisa los reportes escritos. Criterios: (1) uso correcto de al menos 3 conectores diferentes, (2) estructura Problem / Cause / Action / Result clara, (3) al menos 2 oraciones en Passive Voice, (4) coherencia técnica.

🟣 Cierre (15 min)

🟣 Cierre — Reportes orales + poster final

  1. Presentaciones (8 min): 3-4 parejas leen su reporte ante el curso. La clase identifica los conectores y los clasifica.
  2. Poster de conectores (5 min): el docente arma con los estudiantes un poster que queda en la sala, con los conectores clasificados en dos columnas (causa-efecto / contraste). Sirve de referencia para las próximas clases.
  3. Cierre conceptual (2 min): "In technical English, connectors make your description sound professional. Every good incident report uses them."

📦 Recursos sugeridos

Materiales: poster de conectores (causa-efecto vs. contraste), 3 informes de incidentes impresos (1 set por pareja), hoja de ejercicios de conectores impresa, tabla Problem/Cause/Action/Result en blanco, 2 colores de marcadores (azul y rojo) para subrayar.

Apoyos visuales opcionales: diagrama flecha (causa → efecto) y diagrama de balanza (idea ↔ contraste). Tarjetas grandes con cada conector para usar en la pizarra.

Diferenciación: estudiantes avanzados escriben 8 oraciones y usan 5 conectores distintos. Estudiantes con apoyo trabajan con el modelo de apertura y un banco de conectores reducido (because, so, but, however).
