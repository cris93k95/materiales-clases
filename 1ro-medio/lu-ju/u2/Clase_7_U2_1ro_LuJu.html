🔵 Desarrollo

🧠 Actividad 2 — Language Focus

Use these examples before reading. The class text is written at A2 level and the structures below will help you understand, discuss, and write about the topic with more precision.

Connectors for cause, contrast, and result

Use connectors to show why something happens, how two ideas differ, or what result follows from a decision.

Examples
  • The part was replaced because it was damaged.
  • However, the second option required more time.
  • As a result, the final system worked more safely.
Sentence frames
  • ___ because ...
  • However, ...
  • As a result, ...

📖 Actividad 4 — Reading Model — Definir el proceso del trabajo oral

Modelo guiado A2 para la etapa 1/5 del trabajo oral de cierre semestral: Elegir un proceso o mecanismo y fijar el foco de la explicación.

In the school technical workshop, the student chooses one process or mechanism for the final oral task. The topic must be connected to real practice and easy to explain step by step.

The first planning card includes the name of the process, one important material, and one useful tool such as the tool kit. It also explains why the process matters for safe work.

The teacher asks the class to think about audience and purpose: who needs the explanation, and what must that person understand after listening?

A partner checks whether the topic is clear, realistic, and supported by vocabulary from the unit like process and component.

By the end of the lesson, the student has a simple topic plan for the June oral task and knows what information must still be collected.

3. Which tools, parts, or notes are important in the reading?

Important details include one material, one tool like the tool kit, and vocabulary such as process and component.

4. How does the text show the main language focus of the class?

The text uses planning language to show that technical speaking needs order and preparation, not improvisation.

5. What result, decision, or solution appears at the end?

At the end, the student has a clear topic plan and knows what information is still needed.

6. What can you infer about good technical work from the text?

A good technical explanation begins with a realistic topic and a clear idea of why the process matters.
