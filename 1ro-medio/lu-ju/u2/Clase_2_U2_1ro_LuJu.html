🧠 Actividad 2 — Language Focus

Los verbos de manufactura suelen aparecer en pasiva porque describen lo que le ocurre a la pieza, no lo que hace el operario.

Verbos de procesos industriales en Passive Voice
Examples
  • The steel is cast in a mold at high temperature.
  • The piece is forged to give it strength.
  • The edges are ground to remove sharp surfaces.
  • Each gear is inspected before it is packed.
Sentence frames & tips
  • Forms to memorize: cast - cast - cast, forge - forged, grind - ground, weld - welded.
  • Sequence markers: first, next, then, after that, finally.
  • Frame: The ___ is ___ before/after ___ is ___.

📖 Actividad 4 — Reading Text — From Raw Metal to Finished Product: How a Gear Is Made

A gear is a small metal piece with teeth that transfers movement between machines. It looks simple, but it is made in a sequence of careful steps.

First, the metal is melted and the liquid is cast in a steel mold. When the piece is cooled, the rough gear is taken out and the surface is inspected for cracks.

Next, the piece is forged with a hydraulic press to make it stronger. The teeth are then cut with a special machine called a hobbing cutter, and the edges are ground to remove rough surfaces.

After that, the gear is polished and a hole is drilled in the center so it can be installed in an axle. Finally, each gear is measured and tested. If the size is correct, it is packed and sent to the assembly line.

📝 Actividad 5 — Reading comprehension

Responde regresando al texto. Busca evidencia concreta antes de revisar la respuesta sugerida.

1. What is the first action in the process?

The metal is melted and the liquid is cast in a steel mold.

2. Why is the piece forged after casting?

Because forging makes the metal stronger.

3. Which machine is used to cut the teeth of the gear?

A special machine called a hobbing cutter.

4. What is done with the edges of the gear and why?

The edges are ground to remove rough or sharp surfaces.

5. What is the very last step before the gear is sent to assembly?

Each gear is measured and tested for correct size.

6. Infer: what could happen if the inspection step is skipped?

A defective gear could be installed, causing failure or accidents in the final machine.
