🧠 Actividad 2 — Language Focus

Para participar en un debate tecnico no basta con saber el vocabulario: hay que usar frames de opinion y comparar con conectores apropiados.

Lenguaje para opinar y comparar
Examples
  • In my opinion, automation increases efficiency.
  • I believe that humans are still needed for maintenance.
  • Robots work 24 hours a day, while humans need to rest.
  • On the other hand, automation can reduce the number of jobs.
Sentence frames & tips
  • Opinion: In my opinion / I believe / From my point of view / I think.
  • Compare: while / whereas / on the other hand / in contrast.
  • Concede: Although automation is fast, it is not always flexible.

📖 Actividad 4 — Reading Text — The Rise of Automation in Chilean Industry

In the last fifteen years, automation has changed the way many Chilean factories work. Robots, sensors and programmable controllers (PLCs) are now common in automotive plants, food companies, large printing houses and electrical installations.

The advantages are clear. Production is faster, the precision of each piece is higher and the same operation can be repeated thousands of times without errors. Quality control is also improved because sensors detect defects that a human eye could not see.

However, automation is not free. The machines are expensive, the maintenance requires specialized technicians, and any downtime is very costly. In addition, some jobs disappear, while new jobs appear in programming, maintenance and supervision.

Although the change is real, Chile still combines automation with manual work. In many workshops, robots and operators share the line: the robot does the dangerous or repetitive task, while the operator inspects the result and makes decisions. The future of work is, in many cases, a partnership between people and machines.

📝 Actividad 5 — Reading comprehension

Responde regresando al texto. Busca evidencia concreta antes de revisar la respuesta sugerida.

1. Name three sectors in Chile where automation is mentioned.

Automotive plants, food companies, large printing houses (and electrical installations).

2. List two advantages of automation given in the text.

Faster production and higher precision (also better quality control thanks to sensors).

3. List two disadvantages of automation given in the text.

High cost of the machines and loss of some jobs (also expensive maintenance and downtime).

4. Why is the role of the human worker not disappearing completely?

Because operators still inspect results, make decisions and do tasks that need flexibility, while robots do the repetitive or dangerous tasks.

5. Find a sentence with a contrast connector and identify it.

However, automation is not free — the connector is However.

6. Infer: why does the text say the future is a partnership?

Because the most realistic scenario is humans and machines working together, each one doing what it does best, instead of replacing one with the other.

🚀 Actividad 6 — Apply & Extend: prepara tu argumento para el mini-debate

No basta con opinar: hay que sostener la opinion con datos del texto y con vocabulario tecnico.
  1. Elige una postura: PRO-automation o ANTI-automation.
  2. Escribe tu argumento en 3 oraciones usando: 1 frame de opinion + 1 dato del texto + 1 conector de contraste.
  3. Anticipa una contra-argumento de la otra postura y prepara una respuesta breve con although / however.
  4. Cierra con una propuesta concreta para tu especialidad: ¿que tarea se podria automatizar y que tarea no?
✅ Auto-chequeo antes de entregar
  • Cito al menos un dato real del texto (no invento numeros).
  • Mi argumento NO es I think it is good/bad because is good/bad; tiene contenido.
  • Anticipo una objecion real y la respondo.
  • Mi propuesta final es especifica (welding a chassis es especifico; some tasks no lo es).
