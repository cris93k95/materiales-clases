🧠 Actividad 2 — Language Focus

Para definir un componente tecnico usamos relative clauses que se conectan con which, that (para cosas) y who (para personas).

Relative Clauses para definir componentes
Examples
  • A resistor is a component which limits the flow of current.
  • A capacitor is a device that stores electrical energy.
  • An electrician is a person who installs wiring systems.
  • An LED is a small light that only works in one direction.
Sentence frames & tips
  • Thing + which / that + Verb + Complement.
  • Person + who + Verb + Complement.
  • Combine with Passive Voice: A sensor is a device which is used to detect changes.

📖 Actividad 4 — Reading Text — Electronic Components: What They Are and How They Work

Inside almost every modern device there is a small green board called PCB. The PCB is the surface where electronic components are mounted and connected by thin metal tracks.

A resistor is a component which limits the amount of current that passes through a circuit. Without resistors, the current could be too high and other components could be damaged.

A capacitor, on the other hand, is a device that stores energy and releases it quickly when it is needed. Capacitors are very useful in flashes, power supplies and audio circuits.

Diodes and LEDs are components that allow current to flow only in one direction. An LED also produces light when current passes through it, which is why we see LEDs in screens, lamps and indicators.

Finally, transistors and relays are switches. A transistor is a small device which can amplify a signal or open and close a circuit, while a relay is a larger electrical switch that is operated by another smaller circuit.

📝 Actividad 5 — Reading comprehension

Responde regresando al texto. Busca evidencia concreta antes de revisar la respuesta sugerida.

1. What is a PCB and what is it used for?

It is the small green board where electronic components are mounted and connected by metal tracks.

2. Why are resistors necessary in a circuit?

Because they limit the current so other components are not damaged.

3. What is the difference between a resistor and a capacitor?

A resistor limits the current; a capacitor stores energy and releases it quickly.

4. What characteristic do diodes and LEDs share?

Both allow current to flow only in one direction.

5. How are a transistor and a relay similar, and how are they different?

Both work as switches, but a transistor is small and can also amplify signals, while a relay is larger and is operated by another circuit.

6. Infer: why is the same component (a diode) presented in two forms (diode and LED)?

Because an LED is a special kind of diode that, in addition to controlling current direction, also emits light, so it has a different practical use.
