🧠 Actividad 2 — Language Focus

En una ficha tecnica leemos para encontrar un dato (potencia, peso, voltaje), no para entender cada frase. Esa es la habilidad de scanning.

Scanning para extraer especificaciones
Examples
  • Scan for numbers: 220 V, 50 Hz, 2.5 kg.
  • Scan for units: V (voltage), A (current), W (power), kg (weight), mm (size).
  • Scan for key fields: Model, Power supply, Dimensions, Warranty, Safety.
  • Frame to summarize: The ___ operates at ___ V and weighs ___ kg.
Sentence frames & tips
  • First locate the field, then read only its value.
  • Ignore marketing text; focus on the data box.
  • If a value is missing, write not specified instead of guessing.

📚 Actividad 3 — Vocabulary for reading

Revisa estas palabras clave antes de leer. Cada tarjeta incluye una definicion breve en ingles y una guia de pronunciacion para que el vocabulario apoye la comprension del texto.

dimensions
the measurements of an object: length, width, height
Pron.: di-MEN-shons
weight
how heavy an object is, usually in kg
Pron.: WEIT
power supply
the input the device needs to operate (V, Hz)
Pron.: PAU-er su-PLAI
voltage rating
the maximum voltage the device can handle safely
Pron.: VOUL-tedj REI-ting
capacity
the maximum amount or load the device can manage
Pron.: ka-PA-si-ti
warranty
the period during which the maker repairs defects for free
Pron.: WO-ren-ti
model number
the unique code that identifies the product
Pron.: MO-del NAM-ber
safety
the conditions to operate the device without risk
Pron.: SEIF-ti

📖 Actividad 4 — Reading Text — Three Simplified Technical Datasheets

Datasheet 1 — Power Drill TR-450: Power supply: 220 V / 50 Hz. Power: 450 W. Weight: 1.8 kg. Chuck size: 10 mm. Functions: drilling in metal, wood and plastic. Warranty: 12 months. Safety: do not use without protective glasses.

Datasheet 2 — Digital Multimeter DM-900: Power supply: 9 V battery. Functions: DC voltage 0–600 V, AC voltage 0–600 V, current up to 10 A, resistance up to 20 MΩ. Display: 3.5 digits. Weight: 0.4 kg. Warranty: 24 months. Safety: never measure voltage on the resistance setting.

Datasheet 3 — Small Offset Press OP-220: Power supply: 220 V / 50 Hz. Maximum sheet size: 360 x 520 mm. Speed: up to 7,000 sheets per hour. Weight: 1,200 kg. Warranty: 36 months. Safety: keep hands away from the rollers during operation.

All three datasheets follow the same pattern: identification of the equipment, power supply, main specifications, warranty and safety rules. Reading them well is a basic professional skill in any technical job.

📝 Actividad 5 — Reading comprehension

Responde regresando al texto. Busca evidencia concreta antes de revisar la respuesta sugerida.

1. What is the power of the TR-450 drill?

450 W.

2. Up to how many amperes can the DM-900 multimeter measure?

Up to 10 A.

3. How fast can the OP-220 offset press print?

Up to 7,000 sheets per hour.

4. Which device has the longest warranty and how long is it?

The OP-220 offset press, with 36 months of warranty.

5. Which safety rule is given for the multimeter?

Never measure voltage when the device is set to the resistance scale.

6. Infer: why do all three datasheets include a safety section?

Because every technical device has a real risk if it is used incorrectly, and the manufacturer must inform the user to prevent accidents and protect itself legally.

🚀 Actividad 6 — Apply & Extend: tu tabla comparativa profesional

Vas a tomar decisiones tecnicas como un comprador real. Esto exige comparar, no solo describir.
  1. Construye una tabla comparativa de los 3 equipos con estas filas: Power supply, Weight, Main use, Warranty, Most important safety rule.
  2. Imagina que un taller solo puede comprar UNO. Elige UNO y escribe un parrafo de recomendacion de 4 oraciones.
  3. Tu parrafo debe usar al menos 1 frame de opinion (I recommend / In my opinion) y 1 conector de contraste (however, although, on the other hand).
  4. Cierra con una condicion: I would change my recommendation if ___.
✅ Auto-chequeo antes de entregar
  • Mi tabla tiene los datos exactos (con unidades), no aproximaciones.
  • Mi recomendacion incluye al menos un argumento tecnico y uno economico.
  • Uso un conector de contraste correctamente.
  • Mi condicion final es realista, no inventada.
