🧠 Actividad 2 — Language Focus

Use these examples before reading. The class text is written at A2 level and the structures below will help you understand, discuss, and write about the topic with more precision.

Technical English in context

Focus on how the text combines action, evidence, and technical vocabulary to make the task clear and repeatable.

Examples
  • The tool kit is checked before the task begins.
  • The checklist gives evidence for the next decision.
  • The final note explains the result and the safety action.
Sentence frames
  • First, the team ...
  • The key evidence is ...
  • The final result shows that ...

📖 Actividad 4 — Reading Model — Organizar la explicación técnica

Modelo guiado A2 para la etapa 3/5 del trabajo oral de cierre semestral: Ordenar la explicación con inicio, desarrollo y cierre en inglés.

With the evidence ready, the student organizes the oral explanation into a clear opening, a sequence of actions, and a practical conclusion.

Simple connectors help the message sound natural: first, then, after that, finally. Passive voice and relative clauses make the description more technical.

The speaker chooses where to use vocabulary like process and component so the explanation sounds connected to the English of the workshop.

An outline replaces long sentences. Short notes are easier to remember and allow the student to speak instead of reading every line.

A good oral text at this stage sounds ordered, useful, and focused on what another student technician really needs to understand.
