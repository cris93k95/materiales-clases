🧠 Actividad 2 — Language Focus

Una buena descripcion combina 4 capas: material, tamano, funcion y uso. Y usa Passive Voice + Relative Clauses para sonar profesional.

Lenguaje para describir objetos tecnicos
Examples
  • Material: It is made of steel and plastic.
  • Size: It is approximately 30 cm long and weighs about 2 kg.
  • Function (Passive): It is used to measure voltage and current.
  • Extra info (Relative): ...which is commonly found in workshops.
Sentence frames & tips
  • It is made of ___.
  • It is used to ___ / for ___.
  • It is a tool which / that ___.
  • It is designed to ___.

📖 Actividad 4 — Reading Text — Two Model Descriptions for Oral Practice

Model 1 — The Caliper: A caliper is a measuring instrument which is made of stainless steel. It is approximately 20 cm long and very light. It is used to measure internal and external dimensions of small parts with high accuracy. It is commonly used in mechanical workshops.

Model 2 — The Multimeter: A multimeter is a portable electrical device which is made of plastic and metal. It has a digital screen and several scales. It is used to measure voltage, current and resistance. It is designed to be held in one hand while the probes are connected to the circuit.

Both descriptions follow the same logic: a noun is defined with a relative clause (which is made of...), and then specific details are added with Passive Voice (is used to..., is designed to...).

Notice the technical precision: the descriptions do not say it is good; they say with high accuracy, portable, several scales. That is the kind of language expected in Nota 3.

📝 Actividad 5 — Reading comprehension

Responde regresando al texto. Busca evidencia concreta antes de revisar la respuesta sugerida.

1. Which TWO materials are mentioned for the multimeter?

Plastic and metal.

2. What is the caliper used for, according to the model?

To measure internal and external dimensions of small parts with high accuracy.

3. Find ONE relative clause in Model 1.

...which is made of stainless steel.

4. Find ONE sentence in Passive Voice in Model 2.

It is used to measure voltage, current and resistance. (also: It is designed to be held in one hand...)

5. Why does the text criticize phrases like it is good?

Because they are not technical; a professional description uses specific words like accuracy, portable, several scales.

6. Infer: why is it useful to describe an object in this 4-layer order (material, size, function, use)?

Because the listener can build a mental image step by step and understand the object even without seeing it.

🚀 Actividad 6 — Apply & Extend: ensayo guiado para Nota 3

Hoy preparamos la interrogacion oral del proximo dia. Tu descripcion debe cumplir requisitos minimos para alcanzar el nivel Logrado en la rubrica.
  1. Elige un proceso o mecanismo de tu especialidad preferida (NO un objeto generico como pencil).
  2. Prepara una descripcion oral de 2 minutos que incluya: 4+ oraciones en Passive Voice, 2+ Relative Clauses, vocabulario tecnico apropiado, conectores de secuencia y/o causa-efecto.
  3. Practica con una pareja: tu describes, tu pareja marca un checklist (passive 4+? relative 2+? clear order? technical words?).
  4. Intercambien retroalimentacion concreta: ¿que oracion fue la mas clara? ¿cual hay que reformular?
✅ Auto-chequeo antes de entregar
  • Mi descripcion dura entre 90 y 120 segundos al hablarla.
  • Tengo al menos 4 oraciones marcadas como Passive Voice antes de presentar.
  • Tengo al menos 2 oraciones marcadas como Relative Clause antes de presentar.
  • Mi apoyo visual (dibujo o esquema) es legible y ayuda a entender.
