🧠 Actividad 2 — Language Focus

En un manual de imprenta, no leemos cada palabra. Skimming sirve para entender la idea general; scanning para encontrar un dato puntual.

Skimming y scanning + Passive Voice para procesos
Examples
  • Skimming: read the title, the first sentence of each paragraph and the last paragraph to capture the main idea.
  • Scanning: move your eyes quickly to find a specific number, name or part.
  • All the steps are described in Passive Voice: The plate is prepared, the ink is applied, the paper is fed...
  • Sequence markers help you recognize the order: first, after that, in the next stage, finally.
Sentence frames & tips
  • Skim → What is the text about? | Scan → How many colors are used?
  • Frame: In the ___ stage, the ___ is ___.
  • Contrast: however, on the other hand, although, while for comparing offset vs digital.

📖 Actividad 4 — Reading Text — The Offset Printing Process Step by Step

Offset printing is the most common method used to print magazines, books and high quality posters. The image is not transferred directly from the plate to the paper; it is first passed to a rubber cylinder and then to the substrate.

Before printing begins, the digital file is prepared and a metal plate is made for each color. In professional offset, four plates are produced for the four basic inks: cyan, magenta, yellow and black.

In the press, the plate is mounted on a cylinder and ink is applied by a series of rollers. The image is transferred from the plate to the blanket cylinder, and from the blanket the image is finally pressed onto the paper.

Because four colors are printed, the registration is checked carefully. If the colors are not aligned, the image looks blurry and the print is rejected. After the ink is dry, the sheets are trimmed, folded and delivered to the client.

📝 Actividad 5 — Reading comprehension

Responde regresando al texto. Busca evidencia concreta antes de revisar la respuesta sugerida.

1. What products are usually printed with offset?

Magazines, books and high quality posters.

2. Where is the image transferred BEFORE it reaches the paper?

First it is transferred to a rubber blanket cylinder.

3. Why are four plates used in professional offset?

Because four basic inks are printed: cyan, magenta, yellow and black.

4. What happens if the registration is not correct?

The image looks blurry and the print is rejected.

5. What is done with the sheets after the ink is dry?

They are trimmed, folded and delivered to the client.

6. Infer: why is offset preferred over digital for very large quantities?

Because once the plates are made, the printing is fast and cheaper per copy than digital printing.
