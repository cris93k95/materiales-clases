🧠 Actividad 2 — Language Focus

En un informe electrico, el electricista describe lo que se hizo al sistema y lo que se encontro. Casi todo va en pasiva.

Passive Voice + vocabulario electrico residencial
Examples
  • The breaker panel was inspected first.
  • A short circuit was detected in the kitchen line.
  • The damaged wire was replaced with a new copper conductor.
  • The system was tested with a multimeter before the power was restored.
Sentence frames & tips
  • Past Passive: was/were + past participle.
  • Useful electrical verbs (Vp): inspected, tested, measured, replaced, disconnected, grounded.
  • Frame for a fault report: The ___ was found to be ___ and was ___.

📖 Actividad 4 — Reading Text — Case Study: Troubleshooting a Residential Electrical Fault

An electrician was called to a house because several circuits were tripping every evening. The owner had already reset the main breaker many times, but the problem always came back.

Before any tool was used, the breaker panel was inspected visually. No burned marks were found, but one breaker was clearly hotter than the others. The electrician decided to test that branch.

The power was turned off and the cover was opened. The wires of the kitchen line were measured with a multimeter. A drop in insulation resistance was detected near the dishwasher connection.

The damaged section of wire was removed and a new conductor was installed. The connections were tightened and the grounding was checked carefully because a loose ground can be dangerous.

Finally, the power was restored and the kitchen circuit was tested with several appliances. The breaker did not trip again. The owner was informed and a written report was given so the work was traceable.

📝 Actividad 5 — Reading comprehension

Responde regresando al texto. Busca evidencia concreta antes de revisar la respuesta sugerida.

1. What was happening every evening in the house?

Several circuits were tripping and the main breaker had to be reset many times.

2. What did the electrician do BEFORE using any tool?

He inspected the breaker panel visually and noticed that one breaker was hotter than the others.

3. Which instrument was used to measure the kitchen line?

A multimeter was used to measure the wires of the kitchen line.

4. Why was the grounding checked carefully?

Because a loose ground connection can be dangerous.

5. How did the electrician confirm that the repair was successful?

He restored power and tested the kitchen circuit with several appliances; the breaker did not trip again.

6. Infer: why was a written report given to the owner?

So that the work was traceable and the owner had evidence of what was inspected, replaced and tested.

🚀 Actividad 6 — Apply & Extend: redacta un mini-informe electrico

Vas a producir un informe profesional. La idea no es repetir el texto: es aplicar el mismo metodo a una situacion nueva que tu inventes.
  1. Imagina una falla electrica diferente: una toma de corriente que no funciona, una ampolleta que se quema seguido, un interruptor caliente al tacto.
  2. Escribe un informe corto con esta estructura: Problem reported → Visual inspection → Tests performed → Cause identified → Action taken → Final verification.
  3. Cada seccion debe tener al menos 1 oracion en Passive Voice (was/were + past participle).
  4. Cierra con una recomendacion de seguridad de UNA oracion para el dueno de casa.
✅ Auto-chequeo antes de entregar
  • Mi falla NO es la del cable del lavavajillas del texto.
  • Uso al menos 3 verbos electricos del glosario y 3 nuevos.
  • Mi inspeccion visual aparece ANTES de cualquier medicion con instrumento.
  • Mi recomendacion final es preventiva y especifica, no generica.
