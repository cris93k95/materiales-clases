🔠 Actividad 3 — Mini-review de Passive Voice

Antes de leer, recordemos cómo aparece la Passive Voice en este contexto técnico.

Sujeto was / were / is Past participle by + agente (opcional)

  • The wire was damaged by an overload.
  • The circuit is inspected with a multimeter.
  • The faulty cable was replaced.

📖 Actividad 4 — Reading: Troubleshooting a Residential Electrical Fault

Caso real. Lee marcando con un ✓ cada paso del proceso de diagnóstico.

Troubleshooting a Residential Electrical Fault

Last Tuesday, an electrician named Pablo was called to a house in the south of Santiago. The owner, Mrs. Rojas, complained that the breakers in the kitchen circuit kept tripping every time the microwave and the kettle were used at the same time. "There must be something wrong with the wiring," she said.

Pablo started with a visual inspection. The breaker panel was checked first. He noticed that the kitchen breaker was rated only at 16 amps, but the kitchen had a microwave, a kettle, a fridge and a small oven. "The circuit might be overloaded," he thought.

Next, the outlets were inspected. Behind the microwave, one outlet looked slightly burnt. Pablo turned off the main switch and removed the cover. The insulation of the live wire was found to be melted, and the copper was exposed.

Then, the wires were tested with a multimeter. Voltage was measured: 230 V, normal. The continuity of the neutral wire was checked: correct. But the insulation test showed a low resistance between the live wire and the earth — a clear sign of a short to ground.

Pablo explained his diagnosis to Mrs. Rojas: "The damaged wire was the main cause. The circuit was also overloaded. Both problems need to be fixed."

The damaged section of cable was replaced with a new one with proper insulation. The kitchen circuit was upgraded from 16 to 20 amps, and a separate branch was added for the microwave. Finally, everything was tested again: no more tripping, no more burnt smell. The job was completed in three hours.

Before leaving, Pablo gave Mrs. Rojas a recommendation: "Never plug high-power devices into the same outlet. If a breaker trips often, it should be checked. It's not just an annoyance — it's a warning."

📝 Actividad 6 — Comprehension & Passive Voice

1. ¿Cuál era la queja inicial de la Sra. Rojas?

Los automáticos de la cocina saltaban cada vez que usaba el microondas y el hervidor al mismo tiempo.

2. ¿Por qué Pablo midió el aislamiento (insulation test)?

Para detectar si había una fuga de corriente del cable activo hacia tierra. El resultado mostró baja resistencia entre live wire y earth, lo que indica un short to ground.

3. ¿Cuáles fueron las DOS causas del problema?

(1) El cable dañado con aislamiento derretido y (2) la sobrecarga del circuito.

4. ¿Qué pasaría si el cable defectuoso no se reemplazara?

Podría provocar un cortocircuito permanente, descarga eléctrica a quien tocara el aparato o incluso un incendio en la pared. El texto sugiere riesgo cuando dice "It's not just an annoyance — it's a warning."

5. Subraya 5 oraciones en Passive Voice del texto y escríbelas.

Ejemplos: was called, were used, was checked, were inspected, was found, were tested, was measured, was replaced, was upgraded, was added, was completed. Todas siguen la estructura was/were + past participle.

6. Inferencia: ¿qué consejo final dio Pablo y qué quiso decir con "It's not just an annoyance — it's a warning"?

Que no se conecten varios aparatos de alta potencia en el mismo enchufe y que si el breaker salta seguido, hay que revisarlo. No es solo una molestia, es una señal de peligro.

🗣 Actividad 7 — Role-play: electricista & cliente

En parejas, preparen y representen la conversación. Mínimo 4 oraciones en Passive Voice cada uno.

Cliente: describe el síntoma (lo que pasa en la casa) y pregunta qué encontró el electricista.

Electricista: explica el diagnóstico paso a paso usando Passive Voice y propone la solución.

Modelos útiles:

  • "The breaker panel was checked first."
  • "The wire was found to be damaged."
  • "The circuit needs to be replaced."
  • "The breaker panel will be inspected next week."

📋 Seguimiento de la clase

Formativa: el docente circula durante el role-play. Criterios: (1) uso de al menos 4 oraciones en Passive Voice, (2) vocabulario eléctrico correcto, (3) orden lógico del diagnóstico, (4) interacción fluida cliente-electricista.
