🧠 Actividad 2 — Language Focus

Usa esta estructura cuando el proceso o el resultado es mas importante que el agente que realiza la accion.

Passive Voice in Present Simple
Examples
  • Cars are assembled in factories.
  • The oil is changed every 5,000 km.
  • Steel is heated to 1,500 °C before it is shaped.
  • The voltage is measured with a multimeter.
Sentence frames & tips
  • Subject + is/are + past participle (+ by agent).
  • Active: The technician inspects the panel. → Passive: The panel is inspected.
  • Useful verbs: made, used, designed, installed, tested, assembled, repaired, replaced.

📖 Actividad 4 — Reading Text — How a Car Is Assembled

In a modern car factory, a car is assembled in a long production line. Each station is responsible for one specific job. The body of the car is welded first, and then it is painted in a clean cabin.

After the paint is dry, the engine is installed by a team of technicians. The wires, the lights and the brakes are connected in the next stations. Every connection is tested before the car moves forward.

Near the end of the line, the seats, the windows and the wheels are added. The dashboard is checked, the software is updated and the doors are aligned.

Finally, the car is started and a short road test is done. If a defect is found, the car is sent back to the correct station and the problem is repaired before delivery.

📝 Actividad 5 — Reading comprehension

Responde regresando al texto. Busca evidencia concreta antes de revisar la respuesta sugerida.

1. Which part of the car is welded first?

The body of the car is welded first.

2. When is the engine installed?

It is installed after the paint is dry.

3. What is tested at every connection station?

Each electrical and mechanical connection is tested before the car moves forward.

4. What happens at the end of the line if a defect is found?

The car is sent back to the correct station and the problem is repaired.

5. Why does the text use Passive Voice almost always?

Because the focus is on the process and the result, not on the worker who does each action.

6. Infer: why is each station responsible for only one job?

Because it makes the process faster, more reliable and easier to inspect for quality.

🚀 Actividad 6 — Apply & Extend: del texto a tu especialidad

No repitas el glosario ni el texto. Transfiere la estructura del proceso a un mecanismo de tu especialidad preferida.
  1. Elige UN proceso tecnico que conozcas (cambio de aceite, soldadura simple, conexion de un enchufe, impresion de un afiche, prueba de un circuito).
  2. Escribe el proceso en 4 oraciones en Passive Voice, conectadas con first, then, after that, finally.
  3. Agrega UNA oracion donde expliques por que el agente no es importante en ese proceso.
  4. Marca con color: sujeto (azul), verbo to be (verde), past participle (rojo).
✅ Auto-chequeo antes de entregar
  • Mis 4 oraciones describen pasos diferentes, no el mismo paso repetido.
  • Use Passive Voice (is/are + past participle), no Active Voice.
  • Incluyo al menos 2 verbos tecnicos del glosario y 2 verbos nuevos buscados por mi.
  • Mi oracion final justifica el uso de pasiva con un argumento tecnico real.
