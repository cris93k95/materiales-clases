🌈 Passive Voice in Present Simple — color guide

Use the passive voice when the action or process is more important than the person. In this class, the subject is usually a part, a tool, or a product.

subject / pronoun + am / is / are + regular verb + ed

🔵 Pronouns and technical subjects

I you he she it we they

In today's text, the most common subjects are it, they, and technical nouns such as the frame or the cables.

🟢 Verb to be in present simple
  • I + am
  • he / she / it / the frame + is
  • you / we / they / the cables + are
🔴 Regular verbs only in the text

cleaned painted installed connected tested

Only regular verbs are used in the reading so the passive pattern is easier to notice and copy.

Model sentences

  • The frame is cleaned.
  • The doors are installed.
  • The engine is tested.

🧠 Actividad 2 — Language Focus

Use these examples before reading. The text is short, coherent, and written with repeated passive voice so the structure can be understood in context.

Why is passive voice used here?

In technical reading, the process is usually more important than the worker. For that reason, the product or the part becomes the subject of the sentence.

Examples
  • The frame is cleaned before the next stage.
  • The cables are connected after the seats are installed.
  • The engine is tested at the end of the process.
Singular and plural patterns

Use is with singular subjects and are with plural subjects.

Examples
  • The frame is cleaned.
  • The engine is tested.
  • The doors are installed.
  • The cables are connected.
Question frames for this reading

Use these question models to answer with passive voice.

Question stems
  • What is cleaned first?
  • Which parts are installed next?
  • What is tested in the final stage?
Answer stems
  • The frame is cleaned first.
  • The doors are installed next.
  • The engine is tested in the final stage.

📖 Actividad 4 — Reading Text — Process and Description

Texto graduado A2-B1 inicial: secuencia clara, ideas conectadas y Passive Voice repetida con verbos regulares.

How a Training Car Is Assembled

A training car is assembled in the school workshop. The process is organized in six simple steps, so the text is easy to follow.

First, the metal frame is cleaned. Then, blue paint is added, and the frame is painted.

Next, the doors are installed and the seats are installed. After that, the cables are connected inside the car.

In the next stage, the brakes are checked and the engine is tested. Each stage is recorded on a checklist.

At the end, a safety label is added and the final result is presented to the class. The complete process is explained again with short notes.

For that reason, the passive pattern is repeated many times in the text. The action is important, but the person is not named.
