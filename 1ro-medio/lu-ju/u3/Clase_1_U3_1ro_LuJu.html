Actividad 4 - Reading Text - When Things Go Wrong

At the auto shop, a customer says the car engine will not start. At the factory, the CNC machine is cutting unevenly. At the construction site, the power has gone out. At the electronics lab, a circuit board is overheating. At the print shop, the colors look wrong on the page.

Each situation looks different, but all five follow the same logic. First, someone notices a symptom. Then a technician diagnoses the cause. After that, tools and evidence are used to confirm the problem. Finally, a solution is applied and the system is tested again.

Unit 3 is about becoming the kind of technician who does not guess blindly. Instead, you observe, compare, question and decide. Good problem solvers do not only fix things; they explain what failed, why it failed and how to prevent the same problem next time.

Actividad 5 - Comprehension and evidence

Responde con evidencia. Vuelve al texto, al transcript o al modelo antes de revisar la respuesta sugerida.

1. Name two of the five technical situations introduced in the text.

Possible answers: the car engine will not start, the CNC machine is cutting unevenly, the power has gone out, a circuit board is overheating, the print colors look wrong.

2. What common sequence do all the cases follow?

Symptom, diagnosis, confirmation of the cause, solution, and final test.

3. Why is Unit 3 not only about fixing things?

Because good technicians must also explain what failed, why it failed and how to prevent it in the future.

4. What does the text say a good problem solver does instead of guessing?

They observe, compare, question and decide.

5. Why is the final test necessary after a repair?

Because it confirms that the solution really worked and that the problem is resolved.

6. Infer: why are five different specialties presented together on the first day?

Because the same diagnostic logic applies across specialties even when the tools and equipment are different.
