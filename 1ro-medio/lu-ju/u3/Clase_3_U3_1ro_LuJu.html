Actividad 2 - Language Focus

Los estudios de caso tecnicos se entienden mejor cuando separas sintoma, diagnostico, causa y solucion, y cuando reconoces las estructuras que conectan la informacion.

Relative Clauses inside an automotive case study
Examples
  • Relative clause: The technician who arrived first checked the technician first checked the battery and the starter area.
  • Cause: The problem happened because a loose wire near the starter motor and a fuse that had burned out.
  • Solution: The team acted so that the loose wire was reconnected and the fuse was replaced so that the car started normally again.
  • Connector set: who, which, that, and so that.
Sentence frames and tips
  • The problem was that ___.
  • The technician who/which/that ___ first checked ___.
  • Although ___, the real cause was ___.
  • Both ___ and ___ had to be corrected so that ___.

Actividad 4 - Reading Text - The Car That Wouldn't Start

A report from the mecanica automotriz area described a problem that could not be ignored. A 2015 sedan would not start and had made strange noises for two weeks.. The staff member who received the complaint wrote down the exact symptoms before touching any tool.

The first diagnostic step was simple but important: the technician first checked the battery and the starter area. This prevented random replacement of parts and helped the technician focus on evidence instead of guessing.

After the inspection, the team discovered the root cause: a loose wire near the starter motor and a fuse that had burned out. The technician explained why the symptom was misleading and why an early assumption would have wasted time and money.

Finally, the loose wire was reconnected and the fuse was replaced so that the car started normally again. The case ends with a lesson that applies to all technical fields: a clear diagnosis saves resources, improves safety, and builds trust with the client or the work team.

Because it reduces guesswork and allows the technician to collect evidence before replacing parts.

That a structured diagnosis leads to safer, faster and more reliable solutions.
