Desarrollo

Actividad 2 - Language Focus

Un buen parrafo tecnico necesita una entrada clara, sintomas, proceso de diagnostico, causa, solucion y cierre. El orden importa tanto como la gramatica.

Writing a problem-solution paragraph
Examples
  • Opening: At the workshop which serves industrial clients, there was a serious problem with the cooling system.
  • Symptoms: The machine that controlled the line was overheating and stopping unexpectedly.
  • Diagnosis: The technician who inspected it first checked the filter and the hose.
  • Solution: Both parts were replaced so that the system could work again.
Sentence frames and tips
  • At ___, there was a problem with ___.
  • The equipment that ___ showed ___.
  • The technician who ___ first checked ___.
  • Finally, ___ so that ___.

Actividad 4 - Reading Text - Model Paragraph: From Symptom to Solution

At a technical school workshop which repairs electric tools, there was a problem with a drill charger. The charger that students used every day became too hot after only a few minutes.

The technician who inspected the charger first checked the cable, the connector and the ventilation slots. Although the cable looked normal, dust had blocked the ventilation area and the connector had become loose.

Both issues were corrected, and the charger was tested again so that the tool could be used safely. The case shows that small causes can create dangerous symptoms if they are ignored.

Actividad 5 - Comprehension and evidence

Responde con evidencia. Vuelve al texto, al transcript o al modelo antes de revisar la respuesta sugerida.

1. What is the topic sentence of the paragraph?

The opening sentence that introduces the problem with the drill charger at a technical school workshop.

2. What symptom is described?

The charger became too hot after only a few minutes.

3. What did the technician check first?

The cable, the connector and the ventilation slots.

4. What were the two issues discovered?

Dust blocking ventilation and a loose connector.

5. How does the paragraph close?

With a lesson: small causes can create dangerous symptoms if ignored.

6. Infer: why is a model paragraph useful before writing your own?

Because it shows the expected structure and the type of language that makes a technical explanation coherent.

Actividad 6 - Apply & Extend: tu parrafo con scaffolding

Ahora construyes tu propio parrafo tecnico apoyado en una plantilla. La meta es independencia gradual, no rellenar por rellenar.
  1. Elige un problema tecnico de tu especialidad y completa la plantilla: opening, symptoms, diagnosis, cause, solution, lesson.
  2. Escribe un parrafo de 8-12 oraciones usando al menos 2 relative clauses y 2 conectores.
  3. Intercambia con un companero para peer review: estructura, claridad, gramatica, sentido.
  4. Reescribe una version mejorada integrando una sugerencia concreta del companero.
Auto-chequeo antes de entregar
  • Mi parrafo tiene inicio, desarrollo y cierre reconocibles.
  • Uso 2 relative clauses y 2 conectores como minimo.
  • La solucion responde a la causa.
  • Hice al menos una mejora tras el peer review.
