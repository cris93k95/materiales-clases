Actividad 2 - Language Focus

Los conectores no son decoracion. Indican si dos ideas se oponen, si una explica el objetivo de otra o si debes sumar informacion clave.

Connectors that make technical explanations more precise
Examples
  • Although the machine was old, it still worked well.
  • She checked the wires so that the system could be safe.
  • Both the motor and the transmission needed repair.
  • First... then... finally... still matter when you explain the order of actions.
Sentence frames and tips
  • Although ___, ___.
  • ___ so that ___.
  • Both ___ and ___ ___.
  • Ask yourself: am I contrasting, adding, or expressing purpose?

Actividad 4 - Reading Text - Workshop Notes That Need Better Connectors

A student wrote: The machine was old. It worked perfectly. Another wrote: The technician replaced the filter. The system could work safely. A third one wrote: The worker wore gloves. The worker wore goggles.

All three examples have the right information but the wrong shape. Technical English needs relationships between ideas. Although shows contrast. So that shows purpose. Both...and joins two essential details.

When the connector is wrong or missing, the reader understands the facts but not the logic. In diagnostics and maintenance, logic matters as much as vocabulary because it tells the reader how to act next.

Actividad 5 - Comprehension and evidence

Responde con evidencia. Vuelve al texto, al transcript o al modelo antes de revisar la respuesta sugerida.

1. Why are the first three student sentences weak?

Because they contain the right facts but they do not show the logical relationship between those facts.

2. What does although show?

It shows contrast between an expected idea and a surprising reality.

3. What does so that show?

It shows purpose or intended result.

4. What does both...and show?

It adds two important elements in the same structure.

5. Why does the text say logic matters as much as vocabulary?

Because technical decisions depend on understanding how facts connect, not only on naming the parts.

6. Infer: which connector is most useful when writing a preventive recommendation?

So that, because preventive recommendations usually explain the purpose of an action.
