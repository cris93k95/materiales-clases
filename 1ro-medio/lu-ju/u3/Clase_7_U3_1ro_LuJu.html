Desarrollo

Actividad 2 - Language Focus

Un tecnico no habla con frases sueltas. Combina relative clauses, connectors y estructuras vistas antes para que la descripcion sea clara y funcional.

Combining structures to sound professional
Examples
  • The technician who works at the graphics lab repaired the printer so that the delivery could continue.
  • Although the machine looked stable, the bearing which supported the shaft was worn out.
  • Both the cable and the insulation were replaced.
  • The board that overheated has been tested again.
Sentence frames and tips
  • The ___ which/that ___ was ___ because ___.
  • Although ___, ___.
  • Both ___ and ___ were checked.
  • The technician who ___ acted so that ___.

Actividad 4 - Reading Text - A Mini Case That Needs Better Language

A student wrote this case: The printer stopped. The technician worked in the graphics lab. He checked ink. The delivery could continue. The machine was old. It still worked for years.

The facts are useful, but the text is weak because it does not connect the ideas. The technician who worked in the graphics lab checked the ink so that the delivery could continue. Although the machine was old, it had worked for years. The case becomes clearer when the grammar shows the relationships between the details.

Technical writing is not about sounding elegant; it is about reducing confusion. Better structure means fewer mistakes in the next step of the process.

Actividad 5 - Comprehension and evidence

Responde con evidencia. Vuelve al texto, al transcript o al modelo antes de revisar la respuesta sugerida.

1. Why is the first version of the mini-case weak?

Because it lists facts without showing how those facts are related.

2. What connector shows purpose in the improved version?

So that.

3. What connector shows contrast in the improved version?

Although.

4. What relative clause describes the technician?

who worked in the graphics lab.

5. Why is technical writing linked to fewer mistakes?

Because a clear structure reduces confusion and helps the next person understand what to do.

6. Infer: why is this class placed after three case studies?

Because students now have enough technical content to stop imitating sentences and start building their own more complex explanations.

Actividad 6 - Apply & Extend: mini-case para gallery walk

Hoy vas a producir un mini-case realista para exponerlo y recibir preguntas. Tu lenguaje debe sostenerse solo.
  1. Escribe un mini-case de 8-10 oraciones sobre cualquier especialidad.
  2. Incluye al menos 3 relative clauses y 2 conectores entre although, so that y both...and.
  3. Respeta la secuencia problem - diagnosis - cause - solution.
  4. Antes de pegarlo, revisa si cada detalle tiene evidencia y si el lector puede seguir la historia sin preguntarte nada.
Auto-chequeo antes de entregar
  • Mi caso tiene la estructura completa.
  • Uso 3 relative clauses y 2 conectores como minimo.
  • Cada oracion aporta informacion nueva.
  • Mi texto soporta una lectura publica.
