Objetivo de la clase

Describir oralmente problemas tecnicos y sus soluciones utilizando la estructura problema-solucion, clausulas relativas y conectores en interacciones de pares.

Inicio

Actividad 1 - Activate and Predict

  1. Escucha tres sintomas orales y adivina el problema mas probable.
  2. Construye una lista colectiva de preguntas de diagnostico utiles: When did it start? What happened before? Have you tried...?
  3. Recuerda que una buena interaccion tecnica no empieza dando la solucion: empieza preguntando bien.

Repaso rapido de la clase anterior

Ya has leido y escrito casos; ahora necesitas sonar natural al explicarlos en voz alta y al hacer preguntas de diagnostico.

Desarrollo

Actividad 2 - Language Focus

En una conversacion tecnica necesitas una estructura clara: sintoma, preguntas, causa probable, solucion propuesta y recomendacion final.

Speaking frame for technician-client interaction
Examples
  • Problem: The problem is that the device which controls the system is not responding.
  • Question: When did the failure start, and what happened before it?
  • Cause: Although the battery seems fine, the connector may be loose.
  • Solution: I would check both the cable and the fuse first.
Sentence frames and tips
  • The problem is that ___.
  • The equipment which/that ___ has shown ___.
  • First, I would check ___.
  • Although ___, the real cause may be ___.
  • The solution is to ___ so that ___.

Actividad 4 - Reading Text - Model Dialogue: Asking the Right Questions

Client: My printer is making a clicking sound and the paper comes out empty. Technician: When did the problem start? Did anything happen before the clicking sound?

Client: It started yesterday after I changed the ink cartridge. Technician: The cartridge that you installed may not be seated correctly. First, I would check both the cartridge and the print head.

Client: I thought the machine was broken. Technician: Although the symptom looks serious, the cause may be simple. If the print head is dirty, we can clean it so that the printer works again.

Actividad 5 - Comprehension and evidence

Responde con evidencia. Vuelve al texto, al transcript o al modelo antes de revisar la respuesta sugerida.

1. What is the client complaint?

The printer makes a clicking sound and the paper comes out empty.

2. What diagnostic question does the technician ask first?

When did the problem start, and what happened before the clicking sound?

3. What possible cause does the technician propose?

That the cartridge may not be seated correctly.

4. What does the technician want to check first?

Both the cartridge and the print head.

5. Why does the technician say the cause may be simple?

Because a serious symptom can come from a small installation or cleaning problem.

6. Infer: why are questions essential before touching the machine?

Because the client history can narrow the cause and prevent unnecessary actions.

Actividad 6 - Apply & Extend: arbol de preguntas diagnosticas

Hoy no solo role-playeas: diseñas la estructura de preguntas que un buen tecnico necesita para no perder tiempo.
  1. Elige un problema tecnico y dibuja un question tree de 5 preguntas maximo.
  2. Cada pregunta debe abrir una decision: si la respuesta es yes/no, hacia donde sigue el diagnostico?
  3. Usa el arbol para hacer un role-play de 2 minutos con tu companero.
  4. Cierra con una recomendacion final usando so that o although.
Auto-chequeo antes de entregar
  • Mi arbol va de preguntas generales a preguntas especificas.
  • No doy la solucion antes de recoger informacion.
  • El role-play dura al menos 2 minutos y no es lectura plana.
  • La recomendacion final nace del diagnostico, no de una suposicion.
