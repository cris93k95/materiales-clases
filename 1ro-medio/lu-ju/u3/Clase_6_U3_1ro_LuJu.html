Actividad 2 - Language Focus

Los estudios de caso tecnicos se entienden mejor cuando separas sintoma, diagnostico, causa y solucion, y cuando reconoces las estructuras que conectan la informacion.

Problem-solution language in electrical reports
Examples
  • Relative clause: The technician who arrived first checked the electrician first checked the main circuit breaker and then inspected the wiring route in the basement.
  • Cause: The problem happened because a basement cable had damaged insulation, and water from the storm caused a short circuit.
  • Solution: The team acted so that the damaged cable and insulation were replaced and a new surge protector was installed so that future surges would not damage the system.
  • Connector set: although, so that, who, which and that.
Sentence frames and tips
  • The problem was that ___.
  • The technician who/which/that ___ first checked ___.
  • Although ___, the real cause was ___.
  • Both ___ and ___ had to be corrected so that ___.

Actividad 4 - Reading Text - The Building That Lost Power

A report from the electricidad area described a problem that could not be ignored. An office building downtown lost power during a storm and the electricity failed again after the breaker was reset.. The staff member who received the complaint wrote down the exact symptoms before touching any tool.

The first diagnostic step was simple but important: the electrician first checked the main circuit breaker and then inspected the wiring route in the basement. This prevented random replacement of parts and helped the technician focus on evidence instead of guessing.

After the inspection, the team discovered the root cause: a basement cable had damaged insulation, and water from the storm caused a short circuit. The technician explained why the symptom was misleading and why an early assumption would have wasted time and money.

Finally, the damaged cable and insulation were replaced and a new surge protector was installed so that future surges would not damage the system. The case ends with a lesson that applies to all technical fields: a clear diagnosis saves resources, improves safety, and builds trust with the client or the work team.

Actividad 6 - Apply & Extend: boletin de corte electrico

Escribe para una administracion de edificio. El tono debe ser claro, preciso y tranquilizador.
  1. Imagina un corte electrico distinto al del texto: overload in the kitchen area, faulty outlet, broken ground connection, transformer problem.
  2. Redacta un bulletin breve con 4 partes: What happened - first check - cause - action taken.
  3. Incluye 2 relative clauses y 1 conector de proposito (so that).
  4. Anade una linea final para usuarios del edificio: que deben hacer o no hacer mientras se resuelve el problema.
