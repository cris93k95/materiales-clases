Objetivo de la clase

Aplicar estrategias de lectura (skimming, scanning, inferencia) en un estudio de caso mas extenso y complejo que integra vocabulario de multiples especialidades.

Inicio

  2. Lee solo el titulo y las primeras dos oraciones y predice cuantas fallas apareceran.
  3. Define una estrategia personal para no perderte en un texto largo.

Repaso rapido de la clase anterior

La Clase 16 trabajo el orden interno de un caso. Hoy enfrentas un caso largo donde varias fallas se encadenan.

Desarrollo

Actividad 2 - Language Focus

Los textos largos no se leen linea por linea desde el inicio. Primero captas la estructura; despues ubicas datos; finalmente infieres relaciones y consecuencias.

Reading strategies for extended technical cases
Examples
  • Skimming: How many problems are there in total?
  • Scanning: Find the cause of the electrical short circuit.
  • Inference: Why were several failures connected?
  • Summary: Explain the chain of events in your own words.
Sentence frames and tips
  • The text describes ___ main problems.
  • The first cause triggered ___.
  • This detail suggests that ___.
  • If the first problem had been prevented, then ___.

Actividad 4 - Reading Text - A Day of Disasters at the Technical School

It started with an electrical short circuit in one of the service rooms. Because the power system became unstable, several machines in the industrial workshop began to stop and restart unexpectedly. The electronics lab then reported overheated equipment, and the print room found that its calibration data had been lost after the unstable shutdown.

At the same time, a practice vehicle in the auto yard showed an oil leak that had been ignored earlier in the day. Although the leak was not caused by the short circuit, the staff now had to divide attention between a safety risk in the building and a safety risk in the vehicle area.

What looked like many separate problems was actually a mixture of linked failures and independent failures. To solve the day effectively, the school needed technicians who could separate urgent causes, map dependencies and communicate across specialties.

Actividad 5 - Comprehension and evidence

Responde con evidencia. Vuelve al texto, al transcript o al modelo antes de revisar la respuesta sugerida.

1. What problem seems to trigger several other failures?

The electrical short circuit in the service room.

2. Which areas are affected after the unstable power appears?

The industrial workshop, the electronics lab and the print room.

3. Why is the oil leak a special case in the text?

Because it is serious but not directly caused by the electrical short circuit.

4. What does the text say technicians must be able to do?

Separate urgent causes, map dependencies and communicate across specialties.

5. What is the difference between linked failures and independent failures here?

Linked failures come from the same trigger; independent failures happen at the same time but for a different cause.

6. Infer: why is cross-specialty communication essential in this case?

Because no single specialty can understand or solve the whole situation alone; decisions depend on shared information.

Actividad 6 - Apply & Extend: bitacora de centro de mando

Actua como equipo de coordinacion. Debes convertir un caos de fallas en un plan visible y ejecutable.
  1. Construye una evidence table con 4 columnas: problem - specialty - cause - next action.
  2. Marca con un color los problemas ligados y con otro los independientes.
  3. Escribe un resumen de 5 lineas explicando la cadena de eventos principal.
  4. Cierra con una preventive action que, de haberse tomado, habria evitado la cadena principal.
Auto-chequeo antes de entregar
  • Diferencio problemas linked e independent.
  • Mi resumen explica una cadena, no una lista desordenada.
  • La preventive action ataca el trigger principal.
  • Mi bitacora puede servir a otro equipo tecnico.
