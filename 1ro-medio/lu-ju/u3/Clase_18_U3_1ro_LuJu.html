Actividad 4 - Reading Text - Final Review: One Last Problem to Solve

A short case describes a cutter that stops every time the operator increases speed. The student must identify symptom, possible cause, and safest first action. The real challenge is not the case itself but the way the student organizes the answer under time pressure.

Before a written test, students often know more than they show. They lose points because they answer too quickly, ignore the wording of the question or forget to look back at the evidence. A good review lesson helps convert knowledge into points.

Actividad 5 - Comprehension and evidence

Responde con evidencia. Vuelve al texto, al transcript o al modelo antes de revisar la respuesta sugerida.

1. What is the symptom in the mini-case?

The cutter stops every time the operator increases speed.

2. What is the real challenge described in the second paragraph?

Organizing the answer under time pressure and using the question format correctly.

3. Why do some students lose points even if they know the content?

Because they answer too quickly, ignore the wording or forget to go back to the evidence.

4. What should a good review lesson help students do?

Convert knowledge into points by improving strategy and execution.

5. What is the safest first action in a case like this?

Stop and diagnose instead of pushing the machine further at a higher speed.

6. Infer: why is time management included in a language test review?

Because even correct knowledge fails if the student cannot distribute time between reading, grammar and writing tasks.

Actividad 6 - Apply & Extend: pasaporte de revision

Hoy sales con un plan de mejora personal, no solo con respuestas corregidas.
  1. Completa un passport con 4 estaciones: grammar, connectors, reading, vocabulary.
  2. En cada estacion anota: un error que cometiste, la correccion, y un truco para no repetirlo.
  3. Despues del practice test, escribe un plan de tiempo para la prueba real: cuanto dedicaras a cada seccion.
  4. Cierra con una promesa operativa: que haras primero apenas recibas la prueba?
Auto-chequeo antes de entregar
  • Mi passport registra errores reales, no vaguedades.
  • Tengo al menos un truco concreto por estacion.
  • Mi plan de tiempo es realista.
  • Mi primera accion al recibir la prueba esta definida.
