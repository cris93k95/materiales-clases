Actividad 2 - Language Focus

Los estudios de caso tecnicos se entienden mejor cuando separas sintoma, diagnostico, causa y solucion, y cuando reconoces las estructuras que conectan la informacion.

Cause and solution in print-quality cases
Examples
  • Relative clause: The technician who arrived first checked the press technician first checked the ink levels and the computer CMYK settings.
  • Cause: The problem happened because the print head was dirty and one cartridge was expired and had lost color quality.
  • Solution: The team acted so that the technician cleaned the print heads, replaced the cartridge and recalibrated the machine so that the printed colors matched the design again.
  • Connector set: although, both...and, so that, which and that.
Sentence frames and tips
  • The problem was that ___.
  • The technician who/which/that ___ first checked ___.
  • Although ___, the real cause was ___.
  • Both ___ and ___ had to be corrected so that ___.

Actividad 4 - Reading Text - The Colors That Didn't Match

A report from the grafica area described a problem that could not be ignored. Magazine photos looked dull and yellowish instead of vibrant and accurate.. The staff member who received the complaint wrote down the exact symptoms before touching any tool.

The first diagnostic step was simple but important: the press technician first checked the ink levels and the computer CMYK settings. This prevented random replacement of parts and helped the technician focus on evidence instead of guessing.

After the inspection, the team discovered the root cause: the print head was dirty and one cartridge was expired and had lost color quality. The technician explained why the symptom was misleading and why an early assumption would have wasted time and money.

Finally, the technician cleaned the print heads, replaced the cartridge and recalibrated the machine so that the printed colors matched the design again. The case ends with a lesson that applies to all technical fields: a clear diagnosis saves resources, improves safety, and builds trust with the client or the work team.

Actividad 6 - Apply & Extend: tarjeta de troubleshooting de impresion

Vas a crear una tarjeta util para una imprenta o taller escolar. Debe ayudar a detectar rapido un defecto visual.
  1. Elige un defecto distinto: blurred text, paper jam, wrong alignment, toner streaks, trim marks visible.
  2. Disena una card con 4 bloques: visible symptom - first check - probable cause - correction.
  3. Incluye una linea final: How to verify the result.
  4. Usa al menos 1 relative clause y 1 conector de proposito o contraste.
Auto-chequeo antes de entregar
  • Mi defecto no repite el caso del color amarillo.
  • Mi first check es rapido y realista.
  • Mi verificacion final confirma la calidad del resultado.
  • La tarjeta sirve a una persona que necesita actuar rapido.
