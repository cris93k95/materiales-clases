Actividad 2 - Language Focus

Los estudios de caso tecnicos se entienden mejor cuando separas sintoma, diagnostico, causa y solucion, y cuando reconoces las estructuras que conectan la informacion.

Inference and evidence in an electronics case
Examples
  • Relative clause: The technician who arrived first checked the technician examined the defective unit and inspected the capacitor area and the solder joints.
  • Cause: The problem happened because a capacitor was placed too close to a heat-producing resistor and the solder joints were poor and cracked.
  • Solution: The team acted so that the component layout and soldering quality were corrected, and better ventilation was recommended so that future boards would be more reliable.
  • Connector set: although, both...and, so that, which and that.
Sentence frames and tips
  • The problem was that ___.
  • The technician who/which/that ___ first checked ___.
  • Although ___, the real cause was ___.
  • Both ___ and ___ had to be corrected so that ___.

Actividad 4 - Reading Text - The Circuit Board That Overheated

A report from the electronica area described a problem that could not be ignored. Several alarm units were failing after only a few months and one defective board showed signs of overheating.. The staff member who received the complaint wrote down the exact symptoms before touching any tool.

The first diagnostic step was simple but important: the technician examined the defective unit and inspected the capacitor area and the solder joints. This prevented random replacement of parts and helped the technician focus on evidence instead of guessing.

After the inspection, the team discovered the root cause: a capacitor was placed too close to a heat-producing resistor and the solder joints were poor and cracked. The technician explained why the symptom was misleading and why an early assumption would have wasted time and money.

Finally, the component layout and soldering quality were corrected, and better ventilation was recommended so that future boards would be more reliable. The case ends with a lesson that applies to all technical fields: a clear diagnosis saves resources, improves safety, and builds trust with the client or the work team.
