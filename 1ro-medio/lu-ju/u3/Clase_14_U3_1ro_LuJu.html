Objetivo de la clase

Analizar un estudio de caso integrado que combina elementos de multiples especialidades, evaluando la gravedad de los problemas y priorizando soluciones.

Inicio

Repaso rapido de la clase anterior

Los casos anteriores fueron individuales por especialidad; ahora el desafio es decidir prioridades cuando todo falla al mismo tiempo.

Desarrollo

Actividad 2 - Language Focus

Cuando los problemas son simultaneos, el trabajo tecnico ya no es solo diagnostico. Tambien implica decidir que resolver primero y defender esa decision con criterios claros.

Prioritizing solutions in integrated technical cases
Examples
  • High priority: The electrical short circuit should be first because it creates a direct safety risk.
  • Medium priority: Although the CNC calibration problem is serious, it is less urgent than a live electrical hazard.
  • Low priority: The print calibration issue matters, but it does not threaten people immediately.
  • Reasoning: Both safety and system dependency must be considered.
Sentence frames and tips
  • The first priority should be ___ because ___.
  • Although ___, it can wait because ___.
  • Both ___ and ___ affect the decision.
  • The team should fix ___ first so that ___.

Actividad 4 - Reading Text - Chaos at the New Technical School

During the first week at a new technical school, the electrical system in the main building suffered a short circuit. At the same time, the CNC machine in the industrial workshop lost calibration, the electronics lab computers began to shut down unexpectedly, the offset printer produced misaligned pages, and a practice car in the auto yard started leaking oil.

Each problem belonged to a different specialty, but the school could not solve everything at once. Teachers and technicians had to ask difficult questions: Which issue threatened safety? Which one stopped learning completely? Which problem could damage other systems if it was ignored?

The first conclusion was clear: electrical risk comes before production quality. After that, the team had to decide whether machine calibration, unstable computers, print quality, or the leaking vehicle should come next. The challenge was not only technical - it was organizational.

Actividad 5 - Comprehension and evidence

Responde con evidencia. Vuelve al texto, al transcript o al modelo antes de revisar la respuesta sugerida.

1. How many simultaneous problems are described?

Five simultaneous problems.

2. Which issue clearly represents the highest safety risk?

The electrical short circuit in the main building.

3. Why is the decision not purely technical?

Because the team must also consider organization, school operation, learning impact and coordination between specialties.

4. What questions must the team ask before deciding?

Which issue threatens safety, which one stops learning completely, and which one can damage other systems if ignored.

5. Why can a print-quality problem wait longer than an electrical problem?

Because it affects output quality, but it does not create the same immediate danger to people and infrastructure.

6. Infer: why is this type of integrated case realistic for a technical school?

Because real institutions and workplaces often have multiple failures at once, and technicians need to coordinate rather than think only within one specialty.

Actividad 6 - Apply & Extend: tablero de prioridades

Tu producto final sera un priority board defendible. No basta elegir: debes argumentar con criterios.
  1. Haz una tabla con 5 filas: problema, especialidad, riesgo, impacto, prioridad (1-5).
  2. Ordena los problemas de mayor a menor prioridad y explica cada puesto en una linea.
  3. Escribe un mini-acta de reunion con 3 decisiones usando although, because y so that.
  4. Cierra con una reflexion: que pasa si el equipo tecnico decide mal la prioridad?
Auto-chequeo antes de entregar
  • Mi ranking usa criterios, no intuicion solamente.
  • Cada prioridad esta defendida con una razon distinta.
  • Mi mini-acta usa conectores con sentido.
  • Mi reflexion final apunta a consecuencias reales.
