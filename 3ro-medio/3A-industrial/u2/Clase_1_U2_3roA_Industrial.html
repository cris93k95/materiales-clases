📖 Reading: The first ten minutes of the shift

At 8:05 the students of 3°A enter the industrial workshop and go directly to the shift board. Each team reads the job sheet before taking any material from the shelf.

Martina and Diego must prepare a small steel bracket. First, they check the PPE checklist, then they clean the bench and place the piece in the bench vise. Before cutting, they measure and mark the line twice.

The teacher stops one group because they want to start fast. He reminds them: in industry, speed without checking creates waste, wrong dimensions and safety problems.

📝 Read and answer

Responde con información explícita y luego explica qué hábito profesional se destaca en la lectura.

1. Where do students go first when they enter the workshop?

They go to the shift board and read the job sheet.

2. What do Martina and Diego do before cutting the bracket?

They check PPE, clean the bench, place the piece in the bench vise, measure and mark the line twice.

3. Why does the teacher stop one group?

Because they want to start fast without checking, which can create waste and safety problems.
