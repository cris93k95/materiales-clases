3°A — Mecánica Industrial · Case Study: Robots in the Factory

🎯 Analizar caso real (Problem → Causes → Effects → Solutions) 📘 OA3 · OA4 🗂 Clase 2 — Unidad 2 (Clase 11 del año) 🗓 Semana 13 · mayo

Objetivo de la clase

Analizar en grupo un caso real de automatización en una fábrica chilena usando el organizador Problem → Causes → Effects → Solutions, y presentar oralmente la mejor solución con conectores de causa-efecto en inglés.

🟢 Inicio (15 min)

🔎 Recap + connectors

Juego de repaso de vocabulario (5 min): el docente muestra una imagen (robot, CNC, brazo robótico, impresora 3D) y los estudiantes dicen la palabra en inglés.

Pre-reading (5 min): "Look at this image of a robotic welding arm. What can it do? What can it NOT do?"

🔗 Conectores de causa-efecto:

because · so · as a result · therefore

🟦 Desarrollo (60 min)

📖 Actividad 1 — Reading the case (25 min): Robots in the Factory

Texto adaptado nivel A2. Lectura silenciosa, luego segunda lectura completando el organizador.

1. Metalúrgica San Joaquín is a medium factory in Maipú, Santiago. It produces metal parts for cars and washing machines. In 2022, the company bought 2 new CNC machines and 1 robotic welding arm. They also installed a 3D printer to make plastic prototypes. The total cost was USD 380,000.

2. After 6 months, the factory had a serious problem. Productivity went up by 45% — that was good. But the robotic welding arm stopped 3 or 4 times every week. Each stop lasted 2-3 hours. The reason? Only one technician knew how to fix it, and the manual was 280 pages long, all in English. The other workers could not read it.

3. As a result, the factory lost USD 12,000 every month in delayed orders. Two important clients (one in Argentina and one in Brazil) cancelled their contracts. Three workers from the welding area lost their jobs because the robot replaced them, but the company could not hire them back as robot operators because they did not have the technical English skills.

4. The owner, Mr. Carlos Vega, took 3 actions. First, he paid for English classes for 8 workers (4 months, twice a week). Second, he hired a young technician from Inacap with English certification. Third, he asked the supplier for a Spanish version of the manual (it took 5 months to arrive). One year later, the welding arm only stops once a month, and 6 of the original workers are now certified robot operators with better salaries.

Problem: El brazo robótico se detiene 3-4 veces por semana, 2-3 horas cada vez.
Causes: Solo 1 técnico sabe arreglarlo. Manual de 280 páginas en inglés que los trabajadores no pueden leer.
Effects: Pérdida de USD 12,000 mensuales. 2 clientes (Argentina, Brasil) cancelaron contratos. 3 trabajadores perdieron sus empleos.
Solutions: Clases de inglés para 8 trabajadores. Contratación de técnico con certificación en inglés. Manual traducido al español. 6 trabajadores ahora son operadores certificados.

📝 Actividad 3 — Comprehension (15 min): 6 preguntas

1. What 3 machines did Metalúrgica San Joaquín buy in 2022?

2 CNC machines, 1 robotic welding arm, 1 3D printer.

2. How much money did the factory lose every month?

USD 12,000 every month.

3. Why did 3 workers lose their jobs?

Because the robot replaced them and they did not have technical English skills to be hired as robot operators.

4. What 3 actions did Mr. Vega take?

(1) Paid English classes for 8 workers, (2) hired a young technician with English certification from Inacap, (3) asked supplier for Spanish manual.

5. (Opinión) Was buying the robot a good decision? Why?

Respuesta personal con razón usando conectores.

6. (Opinión) If you were a worker at this factory, what would you do?

Respuesta personal (estudiar inglés, tomar curso en Inacap, etc.).

🗣 Actividad 4 — Group analysis + oral report (15 min)

En grupos de 3-4, decidan: "Which solution by Mr. Vega is the best? Why?" Después un vocero presenta usando estas oraciones:

  1. "We believe the best solution is __________ because __________."
  2. "As a result, the factory __________."
  3. "Therefore, other factories in Chile should __________."

📋 Seguimiento

Formativa: organizador completo + 3 oraciones del vocero + uso de al menos 2 conectores + 4 palabras del vocabulario.

🟣 Cierre (15 min)

🗣 Reflexión + exit ticket

Pregunta de cierre: "How does automation affect YOUR future as an industrial mechanic in Chile?"

El docente cierra con la idea: "Robots don't replace technicians. Robots replace technicians who don't learn English and don't learn to operate them."

🎫 Exit ticket

"I think the biggest challenge for Chilean workers is __________ because __________."

📦 Recursos sugeridos

Materiales: texto impreso, organizador en hoja A3, ficha de 6 preguntas, ficha de oraciones para el vocero.

Diferenciación: avanzados pueden agregar 2 desventajas de cada solución. Apoyo: vocabulario con imágenes y banco de oraciones.
