📖 Reading: The wet floor near the grinder

During the second block, a student carries coolant from one station to another and a small amount spills near the grinder. The yellow warning sign is still in the storage room, not on the floor.

Another team arrives in a hurry because they want to finish before lunch. One student starts grinding without checking the floor and without using a face shield. His partner notices the risk and stops the job immediately.

The teacher asks the group to report the near miss, clean the area, place the warning sign and restart only when the station is safe. In industry, reporting a hazard is part of the job, not a delay.

📝 Read and answer

Responde usando evidencia concreta del texto.

1. What risk appears near the grinder?

A wet floor caused by spilled coolant.

2. Which two checks are missing before the student starts grinding?

Checking the floor and using a face shield.

3. Why does the teacher insist on reporting the near miss?

Because reporting hazards is part of professional work and prevents real accidents.
