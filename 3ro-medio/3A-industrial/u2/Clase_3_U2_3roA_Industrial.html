📖 Reading: Safety first

Every workshop has rules. Students must wear PPE before they start: helmet on the head, gloves on the hands, goggles on the eyes and safety boots on the feet.

The walls show signs: yellow signs warn about hazards (hot surface, sharp edges), red signs show prohibitions (no entry, no smoking) and green signs show the exit.

If a student sees a hazard, the rule is simple: stop, tell the teacher and never touch a damaged tool.

📝 Read and answer

Responde con información del texto.

1. What four pieces of PPE must students wear?

Helmet, gloves, goggles and safety boots.

2. What do yellow signs warn about?

Hazards like hot surface or sharp edges.

3. What should a student do if he sees a hazard?

Stop, tell the teacher and never touch a damaged tool.
