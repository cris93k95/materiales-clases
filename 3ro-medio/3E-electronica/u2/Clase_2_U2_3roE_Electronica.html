Objetivo de la clase

Analizar un caso real de gestión de basura electrónica en una comuna chilena usando Problem → Causes → Effects → Solutions, y presentar oralmente la mejor solución con conectores de causa-efecto.

🟢 Inicio (15 min)

🔎 Recap + connectors

Juego de vocabulario (5 min): el docente muestra imágenes (placa de circuito, batería de litio, cargador, pantalla rota) y los estudiantes nombran en inglés.

Pre-reading: "How many old electronic devices do you have at home that don't work?"

🔗 Conectores: because · so · as a result · therefore
🟪 Desarrollo (60 min)

📖 Actividad 1 — Reading the case (25 min): The E-Waste Crisis in Quilicura

Texto adaptado nivel A2.

1. Quilicura is a comuna in the north of Santiago with many factories and warehouses. In 2023, the SEREMI de Salud discovered a serious problem: an illegal "deposit" of e-waste on Av. Lo Cruzat, with more than 80 tons of old TVs, computers, batteries and cables. The deposit had been there for 3 years. Local children played near the site, and tests of the soil showed high levels of lead and mercury.

2. The investigation found 3 main causes. First, there was no official drop-off center for e-waste in the comuna — neighbors didn't know what to do with their old devices. Second, an informal "recycler" (a young man with a truck) was collecting devices for free and burning the plastic to recover copper, releasing toxic smoke. Third, electronics stores in Quilicura were not taking back old products, even though the new Ley REP (Extended Producer Responsibility) requires it.

3. The Municipality of Quilicura worked with a certified recycling company called Recycla Chile. They cleaned the site in 6 weeks (cost: CLP 95 million, paid by the original property owner) and treated the contaminated soil. Recycla Chile recovered 4 kg of gold, 18 kg of silver and 220 kg of copper from the boards. The toxic plastic and the batteries were sent to a specialized treatment plant in Antofagasta.

4. The Municipality also took 4 long-term actions. First, they opened an official drop-off center where neighbors can leave their e-waste for free. Second, they trained 12 young people from the comuna in safe e-waste handling — 8 of them now work at Recycla Chile with a fair salary. Third, they signed an agreement with 5 electronics stores to start the take-back program. Fourth, they launched a community campaign in schools, in Spanish AND with international materials in English. Today, Quilicura collects more than 12 tons of e-waste every month for proper recycling.

Problem: Vertedero ilegal de e-waste en Quilicura (80 toneladas, 3 años).
Causes: Sin punto limpio oficial. Reciclador informal que quemaba plástico. Tiendas sin programa de retiro (Ley REP no aplicada).
Effects: Suelo contaminado con plomo y mercurio, riesgo para niños, humo tóxico, costo de limpieza CLP 95M.
Solutions: Limpieza del sitio. Recuperación de oro/plata/cobre. Punto limpio oficial. Capacitación de 12 jóvenes. Convenio con 5 tiendas. Campaña en colegios.

📝 Actividad 3 — Comprehension (15 min): 6 preguntas

1. How many tons of e-waste were in the illegal deposit?

More than 80 tons.

2. What 2 toxic metals were found in the soil?

Lead and mercury.

3. What valuable metals did Recycla Chile recover?

4 kg of gold, 18 kg of silver, 220 kg of copper.

4. What 4 long-term actions did the Municipality take?

(1) Punto limpio oficial gratuito, (2) capacitación de 12 jóvenes, (3) convenio con 5 tiendas (Ley REP), (4) campaña en colegios.

5. (Opinión) Whose responsibility is e-waste — citizens, companies or government? Why?

Respuesta personal con razón.

6. (Opinión) Would you like to work in e-waste recycling? Why or why not?

Respuesta personal.
