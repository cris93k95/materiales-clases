📖 Reading: The note left on the electronics bench

At the end of the block, the prototype works on the breadboard, the resistor value is verified and the solder repair on one terminal is now stable.

One task remains pending: transfer the tested circuit to a cleaner final board tomorrow. The group writes a handover note so the next team knows which component values were already verified.

The teacher highlights the professional habit behind the note: good electronics work is easier when verification and pending steps are visible to others.

📝 Read and answer
