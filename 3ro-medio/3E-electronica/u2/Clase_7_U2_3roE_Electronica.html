📖 Ítem II — Reading comprehension (20 pts)

II.1 — Read the text

In electronics, every prototype starts on a breadboard: a board with rows connected internally and side rails for power and ground.

The student places the components: a resistor to limit the current, an LED with the correct polarity, and the wires from the battery. To find the right resistor he uses Ohm’s law: R = V / I.

When the prototype works, he moves the circuit to a PCB and solders each joint with a soldering iron at about 300–350 °C. Then he tests every joint with a multimeter and documents the work.
