3°D — Gráfica · Debate Preparation (A2)

🎯 Preparar argumentos simples sobre publicidad impresa 📘 OA4 · OA5 (A2) 🗂 Clase 3 — Unidad 2 🗓 Semana 14 · mayo 🧩 Próxima clase: MINI-DEBATE

Objetivo de la clase

Preparar 2 argumentos simples para un mini-debate sobre prohibir la publicidad impresa para salvar el planeta, usando expresiones básicas en inglés (A2).

🟢 Inicio (15 min)

📣 Topic for the debate

"Printed advertising (flyers, posters, magazines) should be BANNED to save the planet."

✅ TEAM BAN

Sí prohibir: usa papel, talas árboles, contamina con tintas y termina en la basura.

❌ TEAM KEEP

No prohibir: hay miles de empleos en imprentas, hay tecnologías verdes y la publicidad digital también contamina.

Curso dividido en 2 equipos, subgrupos de 3-4.

🟦 Desarrollo (60 min)

📚 Activity 1 — Easy debate phrases (10 min)

Opinión"I think that…" · "I believe…"
Razón"Because…" · "For example…"
Acuerdo"I agree."
Desacuerdo"I don't agree."
Añadir"Also,…"
Cerrar"That's why I think…"

🔎 Activity 2 — Facts box (15 min)

✅ BAN facts
  • Chile produces 600,000 tons of paper waste each year.
  • One tree = 8,300 sheets of paper.
  • Printing inks contain heavy metals — bad for water.
  • 90% of flyers end up in the trash in 24 hours.
  • Digital ads can reach more people at lower cost.
  • France banned plastic ad inserts in 2022.
❌ KEEP facts
  • The printing industry employs 20,000 people in Chile.
  • Recycled paper, soy ink and FSC certifications are green options.
  • Digital ads consume electricity and produce e-waste.
  • Some people don't use the internet — they need printed info.
  • Posters and magazines are part of culture (museum posters, art books).
  • A ban would close small printing shops.

✍️ Activity 3 — Argument planner (25 min)

Argument 1
  1. I think that… __________
  2. Because… __________
  3. For example… __________
Argument 2
  1. Also, I think that… __________
  2. Because… __________
Answer to opponents

The other team will probably say: __________

We answer: "I don't agree, because __________."

🎤 Activity 4 — Practice (10 min)

Practica en voz alta. Usa al menos 3 frases de debate.

📋 Seguimiento

Formativa: 2 argumentos + 3 frases + idea clara.

🟣 Cierre (15 min)

🗣 Share + next class

Vocero por equipo lee UN argumento. Próxima clase: mini-debate.

🎫 Exit ticket

"My best argument is: I think that __________ because __________."

📦 Recursos sugeridos

Materiales: facts box, argument planner, ejemplos de flyers y posters, ficha de debate phrases A2.

Diferenciación: con apoyo, planner con pistas; avanzados, 3er argumento.
