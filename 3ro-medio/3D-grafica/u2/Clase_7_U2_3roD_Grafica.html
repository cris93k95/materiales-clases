📖 Ítem II — Reading comprehension (20 pts)

II.1 — Read the text

A graphic design project starts with a brief: who is the audience, what is the message and when is the deadline.

The designer picks the right tools. For screen, the color model is RGB; for print, the color model is CMYK. For long print runs (over 1,000 copies), offset is best; for t-shirts, screen printing; for banners, large format.

Files come in two families: raster (JPG, PNG) made of pixels, and vector (AI, SVG, PDF) made of math curves. Vector keeps quality at any size, so logos are always vector. A proof is a test print before the final run.
