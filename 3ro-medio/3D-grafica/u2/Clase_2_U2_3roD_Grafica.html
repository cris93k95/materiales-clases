Objetivo de la clase

Analizar un caso real de transición a impresión sustentable en una imprenta chilena usando Problem → Causes → Effects → Solutions, y presentar oralmente la mejor solución con conectores de causa-efecto.

🟢 Inicio (15 min)

🔎 Recap + connectors

Juego de vocabulario (5 min): mostrar imágenes (tinta de soya, papel reciclado, certificación FSC, residuos químicos) y nombrar en inglés.

Pre-reading: mostrar 2 botellas de tinta (offset tradicional vs. soya). "Which one is better? Why?"

🔗 Conectores: because · so · as a result · therefore
🟪 Desarrollo (60 min)

📖 Actividad 1 — Reading the case (25 min): Green Printing

Texto adaptado nivel A2.

1. Imprenta Bellavista is a medium print shop in Recoleta, Santiago. It has 18 workers and prints books, magazines and packaging. In 2023, a big client (an organic food brand from Italy) asked for a special service: "We only work with printers who use sustainable materials. Show us your green certification or we will choose another printer."

2. The owner, Ms. Andrea Soto, had a problem. The imprenta used traditional offset inks (with petroleum), normal paper without certification, and cleaning solvents with high VOC. The Italian client represented 35% of annual income. If they lost this client, they would have to fire 4 workers.

3. Andrea worked with the print foreman, Don José (28 years of experience). They identified 3 changes. First, change the inks: soy-based inks cost 18% more but are safer for workers and clients. Second, buy only FSC-certified paper: 12% more expensive, but allows the FSC logo on every product. Third, install a closed solvent recovery system (USD 22,000) that reduces VOC by 85%.

4. The total investment was USD 35,000. They got a 60% subsidy from CORFO ("Sello Pro-Pyme Sustentable"). After 8 months, the imprenta obtained FSC and ISO 14001 certifications. The Italian client renewed the contract and increased orders by 40%. Three new sustainable clients arrived: a winery from Casablanca, a Chilean cosmetics brand, and the Municipality of Providencia. The imprenta hired 2 new workers — one of them, a young graphic designer who reads English fluently and manages all certification documents.

Problem: Imprenta Bellavista perderá un cliente que representa 35% de los ingresos por no tener certificación sustentable.
Causes: Uso de tintas con petróleo. Papel sin certificación. Solventes con altos VOC. No había documentación en inglés.
Effects: Riesgo de despedir 4 trabajadores, pérdida del 35% de ingresos.
Solutions: Cambio a tintas de soya, papel FSC, sistema de recuperación de solventes. Inversión USD 35,000 con 60% subsidio CORFO. Certificaciones FSC + ISO 14001. Contratación de diseñador que maneja inglés.

📝 Actividad 3 — Comprehension (15 min): 6 preguntas

1. What percentage of the imprenta's income came from the Italian client?

35%.

2. What 3 changes did Andrea and Don José make?

(1) Soy-based inks, (2) FSC-certified paper, (3) closed solvent recovery system.

3. What was the total investment and what subsidy did they get?

USD 35,000 with a 60% CORFO subsidy.

4. Name 3 new clients that arrived after the certifications.

Winery from Casablanca, Chilean cosmetics brand, Municipality of Providencia.

5. (Opinión) Was Andrea's decision a good business decision? Why?

Respuesta personal con razón.

6. (Opinión) What is the most important skill for a Chilean graphic designer today?

Respuesta personal: inglés, conocer certificaciones, diseño sustentable, software, etc.
