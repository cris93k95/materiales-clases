3°C — Electricidad · Case Study: Solar vs. Wind Energy

Objetivo de la clase

Comparar energía solar y eólica en un caso real chileno usando el organizador Problem → Causes → Effects → Solutions, y presentar oralmente la mejor solución con conectores de causa-efecto.

🟢 Inicio (15 min)

🔎 Recap + connectors

Juego de vocabulario (5 min): mostrar imágenes (panel solar, turbina eólica, red eléctrica, batería) y nombrarlas en inglés.

Pre-reading: mostrar foto del parque solar Cerro Dominador (Antofagasta) y del parque eólico San Pedro (Chiloé). "Which one is better? Why?"

🔗 Conectores: because · so · as a result · therefore
🟨 Desarrollo (60 min)

📖 Actividad 1 — Reading the case (25 min): Solar vs. Wind Energy

Texto adaptado nivel A2.

1. The Municipality of Calbuco (Región de Los Lagos) wants to produce its own electricity to save money and reduce CO₂. They have CLP 600 million for the project. They must choose between a solar park (10,000 panels) or a wind park (3 turbines). Both projects produce the same amount of energy: 1.5 MW.

2. Engineer María Soto from a consultancy in Puerto Montt explains the differences. The solar park is faster to build (6 months), cheaper to maintain, silent, and the panels last 25 years. However, Calbuco has many clouds and rains 200 days per year. The panels would only work at 35% of their potential — less than in the north of Chile.

3. The wind park is more expensive to build (CLP 750 million if they include 1 extra turbine), takes 18 months, and the turbines need maintenance every 6 months (high cost). But Calbuco has strong winds all year (average 8 m/s), so the wind park would work at 75% of its potential — more than double the solar option.

4. There is also a community problem. Some neighbors don't want the turbines because they say they are noisy and dangerous for birds. Others say solar panels need a lot of land (8 hectares) and the area must be cleared. After 4 community meetings (in Spanish and in Mapudungun), the municipality decided on a hybrid solution: 5,000 solar panels + 1 wind turbine. This combination uses 4 hectares of land, costs CLP 580 million, and produces electricity day and night. The technicians are trained at the Liceo TP de Puerto Montt — and their service manuals are all in English.

Problem: Calbuco debe decidir entre solar o eólica con CLP 600M.
Causes: Solar: muchas nubes (35% potencial). Eólica: cara, ruidosa, peligro para aves, requiere mantención cada 6 meses. Resistencia de la comunidad.
Effects: Decisión difícil, posibles conflictos sociales, presupuesto limitado.
Solutions: Solución híbrida: 5,000 paneles + 1 turbina. CLP 580M, 4 hectáreas, energía día y noche. 4 reuniones comunitarias en español y mapudungun.

📝 Actividad 3 — Comprehension (15 min): 6 preguntas

1. How much money does Calbuco have for the project?

CLP 600 million.

2. At what percentage of potential would solar panels work in Calbuco?

35%, because there are clouds and 200 days of rain.

3. What is the hybrid solution they chose?

5,000 solar panels + 1 wind turbine. Costs CLP 580 million, 4 hectares.

4. Why were some neighbors against the wind turbines?

Because they say they are noisy and dangerous for birds.

5. (Opinión) Was the hybrid solution a good decision? Why?

Respuesta personal con razón.
