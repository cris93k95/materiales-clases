3°C — Electricidad · Reading introduction: Renewable Energy & the Future of Power

🎯 Identificar fuentes renovables de energía (lectura A2) 📘 OA3 🗂 Clase 1 — Unidad 2 (Clase 10 del año) 🗓 Semana 13 · mayo

Objetivo de la clase

Identificar fuentes de energía renovable y su importancia para el futuro eléctrico de Chile, a partir de un texto introductorio en inglés nivel A2, y compartir opiniones simples.

🟢 Inicio (15 min)

🔎 Brainstorm + vocabulario

Brainstorm: "Where does the electricity in your home come from?" (5 min). Sol, viento, agua, carbón…

Vocabulario nuevo:

renewable energy (ri-NIU-a-bul E-ner-yi) — energía renovable
solar panel (SO-lar PA-nel) — panel solar
wind turbine (uind TUR-bain) — turbina eólica
hydropower (JAI-dro-pau-er) — energía hidráulica
grid (grid) — red eléctrica
fossil fuel (FO-sil FIU-el) — combustible fósil
🟨 Desarrollo (60 min)

📖 Actividad 1 — Reading introduction (20 min)

Texto adaptado nivel A2.

1. For more than 100 years, the world has produced electricity from fossil fuels: coal, oil and natural gas. These fuels are cheap and reliable, but they produce a lot of CO₂ and they are running out. Scientists say we must change to renewable energy — energy that comes from natural sources that never end.

2. There are 3 main types of renewable energy. Solar panels use the sun. Wind turbines use the wind. Hydropower plants use the movement of water in rivers. Chile is one of the best countries in the world for solar energy because the Atacama Desert has the strongest sun on Earth. It is also good for wind energy in the south (Magallanes, Coquimbo) and for hydropower in the Andes.

3. In 2023, 60% of Chile's electricity came from renewable sources — one of the highest percentages in Latin America. The Cerro Dominador solar plant in Antofagasta produces enough energy for 380,000 homes. The El Romero solar plant supplies Google's data center in Quilicura. The government wants 100% renewable energy by 2050.

4. But renewable energy has challenges. The sun does not shine at night and the wind is not always strong. To solve this, engineers install batteries and improve the electrical grid. They also need many new technicians who can install, repair and maintain panels, turbines and smart grids. Most technical documentation comes in English — that is why YOU must learn it.

📝 Actividad 2 — Comprehension (15 min): 6 preguntas

1. What are the 3 main types of renewable energy?

Solar, wind, hydropower.

2. Why is Chile good for solar energy?

Because the Atacama Desert has the strongest sun on Earth.

3. What percentage of Chile's electricity came from renewable sources in 2023?

60%.

4. (Opinión) Do you think 100% renewable energy by 2050 is realistic for Chile? Why?

Respuesta personal con razón.

5. (Opinión) Would you like to work installing solar panels? Why?

Respuesta personal.

6. (Opinión) What is the biggest challenge for renewable energy in Chile?

Respuesta personal: almacenamiento (baterías), intermitencia (noche/calma), red eléctrica, falta de técnicos.

✍️ Actividad 3 — Vocabulary in context (10 min)

Escribe 1 oración propia con cada palabra del vocabulario. Ejemplo: "There are many solar panels in the north of Chile."

Discute preguntas 4, 5 y 6 con un compañero usando:

Sentence starters:
