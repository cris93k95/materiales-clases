📖 Actividad 4 — Reading Model — Preparing an Oral Explanation

Texto B1: cinco parrafos con relaciones de causa, secuencia, evidencia y conclusion tecnica.

At the start of the shift in the electrical lab, a team is asked to solve a model explanation prepared for an oral task connected to Technical Communication & Professional Language. The text follows the way technical information is interpreted before any action is taken.

The supervisor first reviews the record of the previous task, the available materials, and the quality criteria expected for the panel connection. This context matters because the team cannot choose a valid response until the evidence is organized.

As the procedure develops, the multimeter and the control panel provide the key evidence for the decision-making process. Notes, measurements, and short explanations are compared in order to distinguish routine data from signs of a real problem or opportunity.

The writer also uses Technical English to connect ideas precisely and to clarify relationships between cause, sequence, contrast, or responsibility. Because of that, the reader can understand not only what happens, but also why a certain action is preferred.

By the end of the text, the team reaches a justified conclusion, records the outcome, and proposes one improvement for future work. The passage suggests that technical English is valuable when evidence must be communicated clearly to classmates, supervisors, or clients.
