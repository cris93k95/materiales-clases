3°B — Mecánica Automotriz · Case Study: The Electric Vehicle Revolution

🎯 Analizar caso real (Problem → Causes → Effects → Solutions) 📘 OA3 · OA4 🗂 Clase 2 — Unidad 2 (Clase 11 del año) 🗓 Semana 13 · mayo

Objetivo de la clase

Comparar autos híbridos, eléctricos e hidrógeno a partir de un caso real chileno, usando el organizador Problem → Causes → Effects → Solutions, y presentar oralmente la mejor solución con conectores de causa-efecto.

🟢 Inicio (15 min)

🔎 Recap + connectors

Juego de repaso (5 min): el docente dice una palabra en español y los estudiantes la dicen en inglés (emisiones, batería, híbrido, eléctrico, contaminación, combustible).

Pre-reading: muestra fotos de un auto a gasolina, uno híbrido (Toyota Prius), uno eléctrico (BYD Dolphin) y uno a hidrógeno (Toyota Mirai). "What's the difference?"

🔗 Conectores de causa-efecto:

because · so · as a result · therefore

🟥 Desarrollo (60 min)

📖 Actividad 1 — Reading the case (25 min): The Electric Vehicle Revolution

Texto adaptado nivel A2.

1. The Pérez family lives in Concepción. They have an old 2010 diesel car. The car has many problems: it consumes a lot of fuel, it produces black smoke and it failed the SEC technical inspection last year. The family wants to buy a new car, but they don't know which option is best: hybrid, electric or hydrogen.

2. They visit three dealers. The hybrid car (Toyota Corolla Hybrid) costs CLP 22 million. It uses gasoline AND a small battery. It is cheaper and easy to refuel anywhere. But it still produces emissions (50% less than a normal car, not zero). The electric car (BYD Dolphin) costs CLP 25 million. It produces zero direct emissions and is very cheap to "refuel" (charging costs 1/4 of gasoline). However, in Concepción there are only 12 public charging stations, and a long trip to Santiago needs 2 charging stops.

3. The hydrogen car (Toyota Mirai) costs CLP 70 million. It is amazing — it produces ONLY water as emission. But there are zero hydrogen stations in Chile. The closest one is in Punta Arenas (a project of HIF Global with Porsche). Therefore, this option is not realistic for Concepción in 2024.

4. Mr. Pérez also visits a mechanic, Don Luis, who has 30 years of experience. Don Luis tells him: "I can fix any old car with my hands and my eyes. But the new electric cars… I cannot work on them. I don't have the diagnostic computer, I don't have the high-voltage gloves, and the service manual is 1,200 pages in English. The family must check that the dealer has a service workshop in Concepción." After 2 weeks, the family chose the hybrid Toyota — middle price, available service, lower emissions than their old car. They plan to buy a full EV in 5-7 years, when there are more charging stations and more trained mechanics in the region.

Problem: La familia Pérez no sabe qué tipo de auto comprar (híbrido, eléctrico o hidrógeno).
Causes: Pocas estaciones de carga en Concepción (solo 12). Cero estaciones de hidrógeno en la zona. Falta de mecánicos capacitados en EV. Manuales de servicio de 1,200 páginas en inglés.
Effects: Decisión difícil, precio alto del hidrógeno (CLP 70M), incertidumbre sobre servicio postventa.
Solutions: Comprar híbrido como paso intermedio. Esperar 5-7 años para EV completo. Capacitar mecánicos locales. Instalar más cargadores públicos.

📝 Actividad 3 — Comprehension (15 min): 6 preguntas

1. What 3 types of car did the Pérez family consider?

Hybrid (Toyota Corolla Hybrid), electric (BYD Dolphin) and hydrogen (Toyota Mirai).

2. How many public charging stations are there in Concepción?

Only 12.

3. What does the hydrogen car produce as emission?

Only water.

4. Why can't Don Luis fix electric cars?

No tiene el computador de diagnóstico, no tiene guantes de alta tensión, y el manual son 1,200 páginas en inglés.

5. (Opinión) Was the family's choice (hybrid) the best decision? Why?

Respuesta personal con razón.

6. (Opinión) What should YOU (a future mechanic) learn to be ready for EVs?

Respuesta personal: inglés técnico, alta tensión, sistemas de batería, diagnóstico computarizado, etc.

🗣 Actividad 4 — Group analysis + oral report (15 min)

En grupos de 3-4: "Which car is the best option for a family in Concepción in 2024? Why?" El vocero presenta:

  1. "We believe the best option is __________ because __________."
  2. "As a result, the family __________."
  3. "Therefore, Chile should __________."

📋 Seguimiento

Formativa: organizador + 3 oraciones + 2 conectores + 4 palabras del vocabulario.

Cierre: "The mechanic who learns EVs in 2024 will own the workshop of 2030."

🎫 Exit ticket

"I think the biggest challenge for Chile is __________ because __________."
