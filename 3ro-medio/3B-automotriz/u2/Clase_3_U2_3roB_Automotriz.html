3°B — Mecánica Automotriz · Debate Preparation (A2)

🎯 Preparar argumentos simples para mini-debate 📘 OA4 · OA5 (A2) 🗂 Clase 3 — Unidad 2 🗓 Semana 14 · mayo 🧩 Próxima clase: MINI-DEBATE

Objetivo de la clase

Preparar 2 argumentos simples para un mini-debate sobre autos eléctricos, usando expresiones básicas en inglés (A2).

🟢 Inicio (15 min)

📣 Topic for the debate

"Every Chilean family should buy an electric car (EV)."

✅ TEAM YES (buy EV)

EVs son el futuro: menos contaminación, más baratos de operar, ciudades más limpias.

❌ TEAM NO (don't buy yet)

EVs son muy caros, faltan cargadores y la electricidad chilena no es 100% limpia.

Curso dividido en 2 equipos, subgrupos de 3-4.

🟦 Desarrollo (60 min)

📚 Activity 1 — Easy debate phrases (10 min)

Opinión"I think that…" · "I believe…"
Razón"Because…" · "For example…"
Acuerdo"I agree." · "That's true."
Desacuerdo"I don't agree."
Añadir"Also,…" · "And…"
Cerrar"That's why I think…"

🔎 Activity 2 — Facts box (15 min)

👍 YES facts
  • EVs produce ZERO emissions while driving.
  • Charging an EV costs 1/3 the price of gasoline.
  • Cities are less polluted with EVs.
  • EVs are quiet — less noise in the streets.
  • Chile has lithium for batteries — local industry!
  • EVs need less maintenance (no oil changes).
👎 NO facts
  • An EV costs USD 28,000 — too expensive for most families.
  • Chile has only 500 public chargers — not enough.
  • Batteries take 6-8 hours to charge at home.
  • If you live in a small town, there is no charger.
  • 40% of Chile's electricity still comes from coal and gas.
  • Used EV batteries are difficult to recycle.

✍️ Activity 3 — Argument planner (25 min)

Argument 1
  1. I think that… __________
  2. Because… __________
  3. For example… __________
Argument 2
  1. Also, I think that… __________
  2. Because… __________
  3. For example… __________
Answer to opponents

The other team will probably say: __________

We answer: "I don't agree, because __________."

🎤 Activity 4 — Practice (10 min)

Practica en voz alta con tu grupo. Usa al menos 3 frases de debate.

📋 Seguimiento

Formativa: 2 argumentos + 3 frases de debate + idea clara.

🟣 Cierre (15 min)

🗣 Share + next class

Vocero por equipo lee UN argumento. La próxima clase: mini-debate.

🎫 Exit ticket

"My best argument is: I think that __________ because __________."

📦 Recursos sugeridos

Materiales: facts box, argument planner, ficha de debate phrases A2.

Diferenciación: con apoyo, planner con pistas; avanzados, 3er argumento.
