📖 Reading: Why is the warning light on?

A customer arrives because a red warning light appeared after a short trip and the engine smelled hot.

The student checks the engine bay with the mechanic. The coolant level is low and there is a small wet area near one hose. The brake fluid is normal and the engine oil is close to the minimum mark but still visible.

The mechanic writes an inspection note: top up coolant only after the system cools down, inspect the hose for a leak and ask the customer when the last oil service was completed.

📝 Read and answer

Responde usando evidencia del texto y una idea propia cuando se te pida.

1. What two clues make the customer worry?

A red warning light and a hot engine smell.

2. Which fluid is clearly low in the inspection?

The coolant.

3. What does the mechanic include in the inspection note?

Top up coolant after cooling, inspect the hose for a leak and ask about the last oil service.
