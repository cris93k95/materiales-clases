Objetivo de la clase: Aplicar inglés técnico para describir sistemas o procesos propios de la especialidad en Mecánica Industrial.

📖 Reading Text (6 paragraphs) — Systems I Understand in Mecánica Industrial

Diego is a 3rd year student in Mecánica Industrial. This unit is called 'My Technical Skills' because students are preparing an individual video presentation in English. In the video, each student must describe three tools or pieces of equipment, explain two systems or processes, and justify why industrial mechanics is important for society.

The class focus today is 'Systems I Understand'. In the industrial workshop, technical English is not decorative; it is a practical tool for understanding manuals, safety labels, diagrams, software menus, and international vocabulary. Students use English to name objects accurately and to explain what they can do with them.

For this specialty, the first technical references are lathe, milling machine, caliper, micrometer. These words appear in workshop routines, teacher explanations, catalogues, short manuals, and safety instructions. When students pronounce them correctly, they sound more professional and can participate in technical conversations with more confidence.

The main processes connected to this lesson include turning a metal shaft on a lathe and checking measurements with a caliper. Students do not need to master every advanced detail yet, but they do need to explain the basic purpose, the main steps, and the safety conditions of each process in simple English.

This content also connects with the year focus for Mecánica Industrial: precision, preventive maintenance, technical drawings. These topics will return during the year in reading tasks, technical vocabulary work, oral activities, and written comprehension. The objective is to build a bridge between English class and real TP learning, not to study isolated words.

By the end of the lesson, students should have evidence for their final video. They should be able to say what they know, what they can do, what they still need to practice, and why their specialty matters. In social terms, factories, hospitals, mining companies, and public services depend on safe machines and precise metal parts. That is why technical skills are also civic skills.

📝 Fill in the Gap (10 items)

Grammar focus: There is / there are + present simple
  1. There  ___  several parts in this system.
  2. There  ___  one main control unit.
  3. The system  ___  energy or information.
  4. Components  ___  together to complete a task.
  5. There  ___  a clear input and output.
  6. A technician  ___  the function of each part.
  7. There  ___  safety risks in the process.
  8. The diagram  ___  the connections.
  9. Students  ___  the system in simple English.
  10. There  ___  a reason for every step.
