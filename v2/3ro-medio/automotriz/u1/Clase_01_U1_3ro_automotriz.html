🎯 Objetivo de Aprendizaje

OA: OA9/OA10/OA13 — Comprender textos técnicos adaptados, usar vocabulario de la especialidad y producir una presentación oral breve sobre habilidades técnicas.

Objetivo de la clase: Aplicar inglés técnico para presentar la especialidad y explicar identidad técnica inicial en Mecánica Automotriz.

📖 Reading Text (6 paragraphs) — My Technical Identity in Mecánica Automotriz

Sofía is a 3rd year student in Mecánica Automotriz. This unit is called 'My Technical Skills' because students are preparing an individual video presentation in English. In the video, each student must describe three tools or pieces of equipment, explain two systems or processes, and justify why automotive mechanics is important for society.

The class focus today is 'My Technical Identity'. In the automotive workshop, technical English is not decorative; it is a practical tool for understanding manuals, safety labels, diagrams, software menus, and international vocabulary. Students use English to name objects accurately and to explain what they can do with them.

For this specialty, the first technical references are scanner, multimeter, torque wrench, hydraulic jack. These words appear in workshop routines, teacher explanations, catalogues, short manuals, and safety instructions. When students pronounce them correctly, they sound more professional and can participate in technical conversations with more confidence.

The main processes connected to this lesson include checking active and passive safety systems and explaining the four-stroke engine cycle. Students do not need to master every advanced detail yet, but they do need to explain the basic purpose, the main steps, and the safety conditions of each process in simple English.

This content also connects with the year focus for Mecánica Automotriz: basic diagnosis, engine parts, four-stroke cycle. These topics will return during the year in reading tasks, technical vocabulary work, oral activities, and written comprehension. The objective is to build a bridge between English class and real TP learning, not to study isolated words.

By the end of the lesson, students should have evidence for their final video. They should be able to say what they know, what they can do, what they still need to practice, and why their specialty matters. In social terms, transport, emergency services, families, and local businesses depend on safe and reliable vehicles. That is why technical skills are also civic skills.
