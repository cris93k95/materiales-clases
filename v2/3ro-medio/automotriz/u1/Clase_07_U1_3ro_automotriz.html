📖 Reading Text (6 paragraphs) — Why My Specialty Matters in Mecánica Automotriz

Sofia is a 3rd year student in Mecánica Automotriz. The lesson 'Why My Specialty Matters' belongs to Unit 1, 'My Technical Skills', a unit focused on technical identity, tools, systems, and social importance. The reading is written as an adapted technical workplace text, similar to the kind of explanation students may find in a manual, training note, or workshop guide.

In the automotive workshop, English is used to identify tools, follow procedures, read labels, and explain decisions. This is especially important for automotive mechanics because many manuals, digital interfaces, catalogues, and safety warnings use international English terms. The language objective today is connected to Because / so / therefore.

The technical vocabulary for this class includes tools and concepts such as scanner, multimeter, torque wrench, hydraulic jack. These terms are not decorative words for a list; they are words that help students describe real work. When a student says the word accurately, writes it correctly, and understands its purpose, the student is closer to professional communication.

The first process connected to this lesson is diagnosing a fault with a scanner. The second process is checking active and passive safety systems. Students are not expected to master every advanced detail yet, but they should understand the purpose of the process, identify the main steps, and explain at least one risk or quality requirement in simple English.

This lesson also connects with active and passive safety systems. A system has parts, inputs, outputs, rules, and possible failures. If students learn to describe those elements in English, they can answer reading questions, prepare subtitles, write short reports, and speak about their technical skills with more confidence.

The final purpose is not only to pass an English activity. By the end of the lesson, students should collect ideas for an oral or written product connected to the unit. In social terms, transport, emergency services, families, agriculture, and local businesses depend on safe and reliable vehicles. For that reason, technical English supports employability, safety, service, and civic responsibility.
