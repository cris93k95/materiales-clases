Objetivo de la clase: Apply technical English to describe what happened in a technical incident in Mecánica Automotriz.

📖 Reading Text (6 paragraphs) — Past Simple in Case Reports in Mecánica Automotriz

The following report was found in the folder of the automotive workshop: 'Yesterday, the team started explaining the four-stroke engine cycle at 9:10. The equipment responded normally at first, but a minor fault appeared after the second verification.' The document is short, direct, and written in past tense because it records finished actions.

The report continues with a timeline. A student checked torque wrench, another student cleaned the area, and the instructor confirmed that the setup had followed the standard sequence. After that, the group noticed that one part was not aligned correctly.

Instead of writing opinions, the author used concrete verbs: inspected, measured, compared, stopped, and reported. Those verbs help the reader understand what happened, in what order, and why the final decision was necessary.

In the second paragraph, the writer mentions active and passive safety systems and explains how the fault affected the full process. Even a small incident can become serious if the report ignores time, sequence, and evidence.

Students underline key verbs and rewrite weak sentences. For example, 'there was a problem' becomes 'the technician measured an unstable value during checking lubrication and cooling.' Better verbs create better reports.

When the class finishes, everyone agrees that a case report is not just school writing. In a real workplace, a past-tense report becomes a record for maintenance, training, and safety decisions.
