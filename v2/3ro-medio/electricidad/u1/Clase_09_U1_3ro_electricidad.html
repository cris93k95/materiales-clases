📖 Reading Text (6 paragraphs) — Script Structure: My Technical Skills in Electricidad

Mateo is a 3rd year student in Electricidad. This unit is called 'My Technical Skills' because students are preparing an individual video presentation in English. In the video, each student must describe three tools or pieces of equipment, explain two systems or processes, and justify why electricity is important for society.

The class focus today is 'Script Structure: My Technical Skills'. In the electrical workshop, technical English is not decorative; it is a practical tool for understanding manuals, safety labels, diagrams, software menus, and international vocabulary. Students use English to name objects accurately and to explain what they can do with them.

For this specialty, the first technical references are multimeter, voltage tester, circuit breaker, wire stripper. These words appear in workshop routines, teacher explanations, catalogues, short manuals, and safety instructions. When students pronounce them correctly, they sound more professional and can participate in technical conversations with more confidence.

The main processes connected to this lesson include explaining how green hydrogen can use renewable electricity and testing voltage before touching a circuit. Students do not need to master every advanced detail yet, but they do need to explain the basic purpose, the main steps, and the safety conditions of each process in simple English.

This content also connects with the year focus for Electricidad: renewable energy, energy transition, green hydrogen. These topics will return during the year in reading tasks, technical vocabulary work, oral activities, and written comprehension. The objective is to build a bridge between English class and real TP learning, not to study isolated words.

By the end of the lesson, students should have evidence for their final video. They should be able to say what they know, what they can do, what they still need to practice, and why their specialty matters. In social terms, homes, schools, hospitals, industry, and renewable energy projects depend on safe electrical installations. That is why technical skills are also civic skills.
