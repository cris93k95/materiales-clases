Objetivo de la clase: Apply technical English to predict consequences of technical failures in Electricidad.

📖 Reading Text (6 paragraphs) — If the System Fails… in Electricidad

A laminated poster in the electrical workshop presents a warning scenario: if the system fails during reading a single-line diagram, the team must react in an ordered way. The document does not try to scare students; it helps them predict consequences before a real fault happens.

The first part of the poster explains that a small error can grow quickly. If a reading on solar panel is ignored, the next stage may produce false data. If a student continues without checking inverter, the fault may affect more than one component.

The second part lists likely consequences: wasted material, unsafe operation, incorrect diagnosis, and poor communication between team members. Each consequence is connected to a preventive action written in simple English.

The instructor adds an example from electrical safety systems. In that system, one wrong decision can change the full sequence of work. Prediction is useful because it trains students to think one step ahead instead of reacting too late.

During pair work, students complete sentences such as 'If the system fails, we will...' and 'If we ignore the evidence, the result will...'. The conditional form becomes part of technical reasoning, not just a grammar exercise.

By the end of the lesson, the class understands that prevention begins in language. When technicians can describe possible consequences clearly, they are better prepared to protect equipment, people, and time.
