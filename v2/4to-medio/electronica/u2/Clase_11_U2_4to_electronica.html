📖 Reading Text (6 paragraphs) — Critical Reflection in Electrónica

Valentina is a 4th year student in Electrónica. The lesson 'Critical Reflection' belongs to Unit 2, 'Advanced Technical Reading & Critical Thinking', and connects English with employability, technical reading, and real transition to work or further training. This text is written as an adapted workplace document for a TP student preparing for graduation.

The interdisciplinary TP plan says that English in 4to Medio must support employability and technical comprehension in Electrónica. For this specialty, the main technical source focus is texts about electric motors, automation with sensors, actuators and PLC, power electronics, servomechanisms, and consumer electronics. Therefore, reading, speaking, and writing activities must use information that sounds useful in the electronics lab and not only in a general English classroom.

The vocabulary from this text includes these ten terms: diode, transistor, PLC, sensor, actuator, servo motor, project defense, critical thinking, employability, CV. Students should not memorize the words as isolated items. They should connect each term with a job task, a document, a safety decision, a procedure, a customer interaction, or a project defense in English.

One process connected to this lesson is troubleshooting a consumer electronics fault. Another related process is measuring signals with an oscilloscope. In both cases, the student needs to identify the purpose, the required materials or information, the safety precautions, and the expected result before explaining the process orally or answering reading questions.

This class also connects with consumer electronics troubleshooting workflow. The language focus is Opinion paragraphs, because students need grammar that helps them describe responsibilities, interpret instructions, justify decisions, and communicate professionally. If the grammar is used with technical content, it becomes a tool for workplace communication instead of a separate rule.

The final product of the unit is Technical reading test + semester integrative assessment. The expected evidence in the TP plan includes English demonstration of assembly, checking, or troubleshooting in electronic systems and technical reading assessment based on automation, power electronics, servomechanisms, electric motors, and consumer electronics. In social terms, automation systems, consumer devices, medical equipment, transport, communication networks, and smart homes depend on precise electronic diagnosis and safe repair decisions. For that reason, English supports employability, safety, technical rigor, and professional confidence.
