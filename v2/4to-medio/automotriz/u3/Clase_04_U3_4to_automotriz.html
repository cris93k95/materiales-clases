📖 Reading Text (6 paragraphs) — Technical Report Structure in Mecánica Automotriz

The workshop manager receives a technical report after a problem appears during checking brakes, suspension, and steering. The first section states the context, the affected equipment, and the exact moment when the team noticed that something was wrong.

The second section records the findings. A trainee inspected fault code, another checked maintenance schedule, and the lead technician compared the result with the normal value shown in the previous report. Those details matter because they separate observation from opinion.

In the findings paragraph, the writer avoids dramatic language. Instead of saying the process was 'a disaster,' the report explains that one stage was incomplete, one measurement was unstable, and one safety check had to be repeated.

The document then refers to vehicle electrical system to explain the wider impact. A report should not only mention the isolated fault; it should show how the problem can affect workflow, safety, or final quality.

When students label the sections, they notice a pattern: context, findings, probable cause, action taken, and recommendation. That structure turns a confusing event into a document that another technician can trust.

By the final line, the report sounds professional because it is organized, factual, and calm. In Mecánica Automotriz, a good report preserves knowledge for the next shift instead of leaving the problem to memory.
