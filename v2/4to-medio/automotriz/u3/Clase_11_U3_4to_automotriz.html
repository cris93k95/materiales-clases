Objetivo de la clase: Apply technical and workplace English to explain a technical procedure in English in Mecánica Automotriz.

📖 Reading Text (6 paragraphs) — Oral Procedure Evaluation in Mecánica Automotriz

Evaluation day begins quietly in the automotive workshop classroom. Each student has chosen a real procedure, organized the sequence, and prepared explanations for the technical decisions behind following a maintenance schedule milestone or a related task.

When the first speaker begins, the audience hears a clear introduction, careful use of torque wrench, and a sequence that moves from preparation to execution without confusion. The strongest presentations do not sound memorized; they sound understood.

One student pauses to explain why a verification step matters before moving to battery test. That brief explanation improves the whole performance because it shows purpose, not just repetition.

The rubric also values connection with brake, suspension, and steering systems. A procedure is stronger when the student shows how one task influences the larger technical process, the final quality, or workplace safety.

After each turn, the teacher asks one or two follow-up questions. The answers reveal whether the speaker truly controls the procedure or only remembers isolated lines from the script.

By the last presentation, the class can hear a real difference between practice and performance. Technical English becomes meaningful when students use it to explain work clearly to another person.
