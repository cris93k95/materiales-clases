📖 Reading Text (6 paragraphs) — Final Project Defense in Mecánica Automotriz

Sofía is a 4th year student in Mecánica Automotriz. The lesson 'Final Project Defense' belongs to Unit 4, 'Innovation, Project Defense & Closure', and connects English with employability, technical reading, and real transition to work or further training. This text is written as an adapted workplace document for a TP student preparing for graduation.

The interdisciplinary TP plan says that English in 4to Medio must support employability and technical comprehension in Mecánica Automotriz. For this specialty, the main technical source focus is vehicle systems, engine maintenance, brake systems, suspension, steering, electrical systems, front train components, fault diagnosis, and maintenance milestones. Therefore, reading, speaking, and writing activities must use information that sounds useful in the automotive workshop and not only in a general English classroom. In this specialty, the text also emphasizes professional customer service, because the student must explain diagnosis and work clearly to a client.

The vocabulary from this text includes these ten terms: torque wrench, battery test, service order, customer complaint, diagnostic scanner, fault code, project defense, critical thinking, employability, CV. Students should not memorize the words as isolated items. They should connect each term with a job task, a document, a safety decision, a procedure, a customer interaction, or a project defense in English.

One process connected to this lesson is following a maintenance schedule milestone. Another related process is preparing a repair estimate for the client. In both cases, the student needs to identify the purpose, the required materials or information, the safety precautions, and the expected result before explaining the process orally or answering reading questions.

This class also connects with engine and vehicle systems. The language focus is Integrated oral defense, because students need grammar that helps them describe responsibilities, interpret instructions, justify decisions, and communicate professionally. If the grammar is used with technical content, it becomes a tool for workplace communication instead of a separate rule.

The final product of the unit is Advanced reading test + final project defense in English. The expected evidence in the TP plan includes customer service interaction explaining the diagnosis and the work that was done or will be done and technical reading assessment based on work guides, automotive systems, fault diagnosis, and vehicle maintenance milestones. In social terms, families, emergency services, companies, public transport, and local businesses depend on safe vehicles and clear communication between the workshop and the client. For that reason, English supports employability, safety, technical rigor, and professional confidence.
