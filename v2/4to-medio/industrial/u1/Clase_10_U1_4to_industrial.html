📖 Reading Text (6 paragraphs) — Video Interview Script in Mecánica Industrial

Diego is a 4th year student in Mecánica Industrial. The lesson 'Video Interview Script' belongs to Unit 1, 'Professional Profile & Workplace English', and connects English with employability, technical reading, and real transition to work or further training. This text is written as an adapted workplace document for a TP student preparing for graduation.

The interdisciplinary TP plan says that English in 4to Medio must support employability and technical comprehension in Mecánica Industrial. For this specialty, the main technical source focus is CNC and MasterCAM documents consulted with the specialty teachers. Therefore, reading, speaking, and writing activities must use information that sounds useful in the industrial workshop and not only in a general English classroom.

The vocabulary from this text includes these ten terms: tolerance, machining center, simulation, CNC machine, MasterCAM, toolpath, technical report, project defense, critical thinking, employability. Students should not memorize the words as isolated items. They should connect each term with a job task, a document, a safety decision, a procedure, a customer interaction, or a project defense in English.

One process connected to this lesson is selecting feed rate and spindle speed. Another related process is simulating a machining operation. In both cases, the student needs to identify the purpose, the required materials or information, the safety precautions, and the expected result before explaining the process orally or answering reading questions.

This class also connects with CAD/CAM production chain. The language focus is Dialogue structure + turn-taking, because students need grammar that helps them describe responsibilities, interpret instructions, justify decisions, and communicate professionally. If the grammar is used with technical content, it becomes a tool for workplace communication instead of a separate rule.

The final product of the unit is CV in English + Mock Job Interview video. The expected evidence in the TP plan includes written English assessment on CNC and MasterCAM concepts, instructions, and terminology and technical reading assessment based on MasterCAM and CNC materials. In social terms, manufacturing, mining, maintenance companies, hospitals, and public services need technicians who can read CNC instructions and follow safe machining procedures. For that reason, English supports employability, safety, technical rigor, and professional confidence.
