Writing Clear Steps

4to D · Gráfica · Unit 3

Nivel: 4to Medio
Asignatura: Inglés
Duración: 90 min
Gramática: First / next / after that / finally

🎯 Objetivo de Aprendizaje

OA: OA9/OA10/OA13/OA14 — Comprender textos tecnicos y laborales, utilizar vocabulario TP, producir respuestas escritas y orales para empleabilidad y comunicacion tecnica.

Objetivo de la clase: Apply technical and workplace English to write clear procedural steps in Gráfica.

📖 Reading Text (6 paragraphs) — Writing Clear Steps in Gráfica

A draft instruction sheet on the supervisor's desk describes preflighting a print-ready file, but the writing is messy and difficult to follow. Some steps are too long, two actions are mixed in the same sentence, and the final verification appears before the preparation stage.

The team rewrites the document so that each instruction contains only one clear action. First, the technician checks equipment manual. Next, the technician prepares RIP software. After that, the technician confirms settings or alignment with print queue.

As the sheet improves, the workshop notices how language changes the quality of work. A good procedure does not only tell people what to do; it also reduces hesitation, repeated questions, and preventable errors.

The revised version also mentions equipment documentation. Procedures inside that system must be easy to scan quickly because technicians often consult them while working under time pressure.

To test the new draft, one student reads the instructions aloud while another follows them step by step. Whenever the listener pauses or misunderstands a sequence, the sentence is corrected again.

The final text proves that technical English should be direct and useful. Clear steps create better routines, and better routines create more reliable work in Gráfica.
