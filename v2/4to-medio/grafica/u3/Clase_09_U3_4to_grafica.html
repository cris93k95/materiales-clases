Objetivo de la clase: Apply technical and workplace English to rehearse key terms accurately in Gráfica.

📖 Reading Text (6 paragraphs) — Technical Vocabulary Rehearsal in Gráfica

On rehearsal day, students stand beside their notes and practice key terms before explaining calibrating color before a production run. The instructor does not start with grammar. Instead, the first focus is pronunciation, because unclear terminology can confuse the whole explanation.

A pronunciation sheet groups difficult words such as finishing unit, maintenance log, and preflight. Students mark stressed syllables, repeat the words in short phrases, and connect them with the exact step where each term appears.

One problem appears immediately: some students know the word on paper but cannot say it naturally while describing the procedure. The rehearsal helps them bridge that gap before the final performance.

The vocabulary sheet also refers to digital print workflow so that the terms are not learned in isolation. Each word belongs to an action, a part, or a decision inside a real technical context.

Pairs then test each other. One student says the term, the other explains its role in the procedure. If the explanation sounds vague, both students review the sentence again until the meaning is precise.

By the end of the activity, pronunciation is no longer a separate drill. It becomes part of professional credibility: if the term is clear, the procedure sounds more confident and more teachable.
