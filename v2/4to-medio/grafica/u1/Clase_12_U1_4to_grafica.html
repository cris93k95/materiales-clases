📖 Reading Text (6 paragraphs) — Mock Job Interview in Gráfica

Camila is a 4th year student in Gráfica. The lesson 'Mock Job Interview' belongs to Unit 1, 'Professional Profile & Workplace English', and connects English with employability, technical reading, and real transition to work or further training. This text is written as an adapted workplace document for a TP student preparing for graduation.

The interdisciplinary TP plan says that English in 4to Medio must support employability and technical comprehension in Gráfica. For this specialty, the main technical source focus is documents and materials linked to the recently acquired equipment in the specialty. Therefore, reading, speaking, and writing activities must use information that sounds useful in the graphic production lab and not only in a general English classroom.

The vocabulary from this text includes these ten terms: bleed, digital press, equipment manual, RIP software, print queue, color calibration, critical thinking, employability, CV, job interview. Students should not memorize the words as isolated items. They should connect each term with a job task, a document, a safety decision, a procedure, a customer interaction, or a project defense in English.

One process connected to this lesson is recording equipment maintenance after use. Another related process is reading the equipment manual before operation. In both cases, the student needs to identify the purpose, the required materials or information, the safety precautions, and the expected result before explaining the process orally or answering reading questions.

This class also connects with production and finishing workflow. The language focus is Integrated oral performance, because students need grammar that helps them describe responsibilities, interpret instructions, justify decisions, and communicate professionally. If the grammar is used with technical content, it becomes a tool for workplace communication instead of a separate rule.

The final product of the unit is CV in English + Mock Job Interview video. The expected evidence in the TP plan includes explanatory video with professional vocabulary from the graphic production area and technical reading assessment based on documentation for the recently acquired graphic production equipment. In social terms, schools, companies, public campaigns, local businesses, and community organizations need clear printed and digital communication produced with professional workflows. For that reason, English supports employability, safety, technical rigor, and professional confidence.
