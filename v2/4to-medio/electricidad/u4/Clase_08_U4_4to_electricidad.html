📖 Reading Text (6 paragraphs) — Evidence for My Project in Electricidad

Mateo is a 4th year student in Electricidad. The lesson 'Evidence for My Project' belongs to Unit 4, 'Innovation, Project Defense & Closure', and connects English with employability, technical reading, and real transition to work or further training. This text is written as an adapted workplace document for a TP student preparing for graduation.

The interdisciplinary TP plan says that English in 4to Medio must support employability and technical comprehension in Electricidad. For this specialty, the main technical source focus is texts about electric motors, magnetism, and key technical terminology related to these contents. Therefore, reading, speaking, and writing activities must use information that sounds useful in the electrical workshop and not only in a general English classroom.

The vocabulary from this text includes these ten terms: insulation, winding, nameplate, grounding, current draw, electric motor, manual, evidence, technical report, project defense. Students should not memorize the words as isolated items. They should connect each term with a job task, a document, a safety decision, a procedure, a customer interaction, or a project defense in English.

One process connected to this lesson is checking insulation before connection. Another related process is explaining how a magnetic field moves a rotor. In both cases, the student needs to identify the purpose, the required materials or information, the safety precautions, and the expected result before explaining the process orally or answering reading questions.

This class also connects with three-phase installation. The language focus is Evidence language, because students need grammar that helps them describe responsibilities, interpret instructions, justify decisions, and communicate professionally. If the grammar is used with technical content, it becomes a tool for workplace communication instead of a separate rule.

The final product of the unit is Advanced reading test + final project defense in English. The expected evidence in the TP plan includes audiovisual presentation with precise electrical terminology and safety language and technical reading assessment based on electric motors, magnetism, and key electrical terminology. In social terms, homes, workshops, factories, schools, hospitals, and renewable energy projects require safe electrical installations and technicians who can explain risks clearly. For that reason, English supports employability, safety, technical rigor, and professional confidence.
