📖 Reading Text (6 paragraphs) — Procedure Script in Electricidad

A trainee drafts a procedure script for checking insulation before connection before the oral presentation. The script is not a full paragraph to memorize. It is a structured guide with an opening, a logical sequence, and a final explanation of why the task matters.

In the opening section, the trainee names the purpose of the procedure and the materials required. In the middle section, the script moves through the key stages, including checks related to insulation, winding, and nameplate.

The first version is too dense, so the instructor asks for shorter sentences and clearer transitions. A good script should support speaking, not trap the speaker inside a wall of text.

The class also connects the script to motor protection circuit. When the procedure belongs to a wider system, the conclusion should show the expected result and how the task supports quality or safety in the full workflow.

Students then highlight purpose clauses such as 'to verify,' 'to prevent,' and 'in order to confirm.' Those phrases help the speaker explain not only what happens, but why each action exists.

The final script looks smaller than the first draft, but it communicates more. In technical English, clarity is stronger than quantity when the goal is oral explanation.
