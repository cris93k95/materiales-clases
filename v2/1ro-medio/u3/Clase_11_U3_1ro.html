Writing Your Own Mini-Biography

Past Simple + Past Continuous combined

Nivel: 1ro Medio
Asignatura: Inglés
Duración: 90 min
Gramática: Past Simple + Past Continuous combined — 'When X happened, Y was happening.'

🎯 Objetivo de Aprendizaje

OA: OA14 — Producir un texto biográfico breve combinando estructuras gramaticales del pasado.

Objetivo de la clase: Redactar una mini-biografía (150 palabras) de un/a innovador/a TP elegido/a usando pasado simple y continuo.

📖 Reading Text — Model Bio — María Skłodowska Curie

María Skłodowska was born in Warsaw, Poland, in 1867. Her family was very poor, but her parents were teachers and they valued education. While she was studying at home with her father, she was developing a passion for science.

In 1891, she moved to Paris and studied at the Sorbonne. There, she met Pierre Curie. They got married in 1895 and worked together in a small laboratory. While they were experimenting with uranium, they discovered two new elements: polonium and radium.

Marie Curie won two Nobel Prizes — one in Physics (1903) and one in Chemistry (1911). She was the first woman to win, and the first person to win in two different sciences. She died in 1934, but her research opened the door to modern medicine and electronics.

📝 Fill in the Gap (10 items)

Grammar focus: Past Simple + Past Continuous combined — 'When X happened, Y was happening.'
  1. Marie  ___  (be) born in 1867.
  2. Her parents  ___  (be) teachers.
  3. While she  ___  (study) at home, she  ___  (develop) a passion.
  4. In 1891, she  ___  (move) to Paris.
  5. She  ___  (meet) Pierre Curie.
  6. They  ___  (get) married in 1895.
  7. While they  ___  (experiment) with uranium, they  ___  (discover) two elements.
  8. She  ___  (win) two Nobel Prizes.
  9. She  ___  (die) in 1934.
  10. Her research  ___  (open) the door to modern science.
Answer key:
  1. was
  2. were
  3. was studying / was developing
  4. moved
  5. met
  6. got
  7. were experimenting / discovered
  8. won
  9. died
  10. opened
